Owns the UI- and Chat-neutral action contracts for reading Household authority
and changing membership, invitations, child capability activation, and explicit
caregiver grants. Persistence remains behind an authenticated adapter owned by
the Household feature.

Native Household settings use these actions now. Chat's authorized Family
Screen Time evidence loader shares the read action, but conversational
Household tools remain unavailable until their authenticated providers,
review proposals, durable receipts, and explicit reversibility boundaries are
implemented.
