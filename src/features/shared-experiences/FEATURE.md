# shared-experiences

Documents the cross-capability contract that lets Maya or David invite someone
into one specific experience, participate immediately, and keep everything else
private.

## Ownership

- Goals owns support invitations, authored check-ins, cheers, replies, and the
  complete access lifecycle.
- Games owns private table discovery, seats, canonical room state, reconnect,
  completion, and rematch.
- Household and Friendship are relationship facts that make people eligible to
  choose; neither grants ambient content access.
- Future Explore, Activity, and recipe participation must define their own
  capability-specific visibility and contribution contracts.

## Notes

This is a documentation feature slice over capability-owned implementation. It
does not introduce a Home surface, feed, universal shared-items destination, or
general participation table.
