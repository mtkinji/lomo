# Contextual Pro Offer Migration Matrix

The reviewed Money upgrade drawer is the canonical template for contextual Pro invitations. This inventory keeps that decision actionable across every active paywall reason without pretending that Money-specific photography or copy fits every paid job.

## Template contract

Every contextual offer uses one immersive Pine drawer, `Kwilt | Pro` lockup, radiused contextual media, one full-width proof notification over the media, one outcome headline, one mechanism sentence, at most one verified commercial line, and one large white bottom CTA. The close affordance is the only dismissal. Exact plan charges, renewal disclosure, Terms, Privacy, Restore, and complete plan comparison belong to the Apple-backed plan chooser.

## Retired reasons

The reasons declared in `RetiredPaywallReason` are Free-product boundaries and must render no offer even if an old caller reaches the paywall service. They are tracked by central policy and regression tests, not migrated into this template.

## Migration acceptance

A row moves to complete only after the outcome, proof moment, asset rights, copy, CTA state, resume behavior, focused tests, smallest-viewport/large-text behavior, real-route screenshot, and purchase-path attribution are verified. Source or Simulator evidence does not prove live StoreKit pricing, purchase, entitlement, or native enforcement.
