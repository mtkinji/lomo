# Feature monetization intake

Use this before assigning a feature to Free or Pro.

1. What customer job becomes meaningfully easier, and is that value already proven?
2. Is the feature a foundation Kwilt needs for habit and trust, a differentiated paid outcome, or merely expensive to run?
3. What is the exact client entry point, direct write boundary, server boundary, and downgrade behavior?
4. Can Free users understand the value before the upgrade, preserve their draft, and keep a safe way to read, disable, delete, export, disconnect, restore, or manage billing?
5. Does a cancelled subscriber retain access through the paid-through date? What happens on grace, expiration, refund, offline use, replay, and account switch?
6. Which price and trial claims come from live StoreKit rather than app copy?
7. What analytics prove the job was completed, not only that a paywall was viewed?
8. What runtime, provider, physical-device, App Review, privacy, support, and cost evidence is required before marketing?
9. Which independent flag can hide the capability without changing subscription truth?
10. If a creator promotes it, what approved claim, attribution window, disclosure, commission event, refund reversal, and reconciliation rule applies?

The default is: keep the product foundation Free; charge for connected, differentiated, or materially costly outcomes; never use creator attribution as entitlement; and never delete customer work merely because payment ended.
