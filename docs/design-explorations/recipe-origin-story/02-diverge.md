# Diverge: Recipe origin story

Axis: how much place context Recipe Home reveals.

1. **Map only.** A compact native map and origin label. Fastest scan, but does not deliver the richer history requested.
2. **Story only.** History and sources without a map. Strong nuance, but loses immediate geographic orientation.
3. **Map-led story.** One native map, origin label, short researched history, and quiet sources in the existing Recipe Home flow. Best balance of appetite, place, and trust.

All three keep AI out of map rendering, do not use user location, and add no dashboard, score, streak, or planning object.
