# Frame: Recipe origin story

## What the user said
> Maps should also not be illustrated generations, but actual map components if we can.

## Restated in user voice
When I am deciding whether a meal belongs in our household, help me understand where it comes from and why it matters without making me sort decorative AI imagery from fact.

## Target audience
`audience-aspirational-family-organizers` — families who want useful food knowledge without a new research hobby.

## Representative persona
Maya wants a Recipe Home that makes a meal appetizing, credible, and cookable at a glance. Decorative cultural shorthand or overconfident origin claims would break trust.

## Hero anchor
`jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`

## Job flow step
Maya feed-household step 3, “Recognize whether it fits tonight,” currently scores 1. Recipe Home needs stronger media, source, and hierarchy.

## Active anchors
- `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter` — make choosing and cooking a meal easier.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` — distinguish researched facts, editorial review, and generated imagery.

## System alignment
Constraint posture: `Fit the system`.

- Recipe Home already owns title, hero media, ingredients, method, notes, and provenance.
- `react-native-maps` is already a production dependency and map precedent.
- Editorial enrichment already carries bounded coordinates, markers, scale, country IDs, history, and sources.
- Hero imagery remains food photography; maps are never generated media.

## Aspirational design challenge
How might we help Maya recognize a meal’s place and story while preserving a calm Recipe Home and a truthful boundary between research, maps, and generated food photography?

## Out of scope
Interactive geographic exploration, routes, user location, and claims of one definitive birthplace.
