# Chosen contract

Job: recognize a recent moment, review it, and share with chosen people.
Three-second read: Share a moment → recent concrete choices → writing/photo alternatives.
Primary action: Post, once a draft has content, in BottomDrawer.footer.
Primary information: real title and source kind; audience remains explicit.
Secondary: optional caption, photos, attachment disclosure.
Reveal later: more suggestions, audience configuration, draft/photo options.
Reuse: canonical BottomDrawer at 95%, keyboardBehavior resize; BottomDrawerHeader withClose; BottomDrawerScrollView; canonical Button/Input and SettingsRow. One gutter owner. No custom centered modal header, duplicate Post action, forced keyboard, nested decorative cards, or auto-publication.

New empty drafts show three suggestions, then bounded View more expansion. Selecting a place/goal sets the existing reviewed attachment; selecting a cooked meal seeds editable text. Preserve authored text and photos. Offered attachments and existing drafts go straight to review; suggestion changes remain explicit. Use a 30-day window; Goals use update recency as a conservative availability heuristic, not evidence of completion time. One suggestion per source entity, newest first with type diversity in the first three.

Bet: recognition makes sharing easier than recall. Revisit with capability browsing if people repeatedly cannot find their intended moment.
