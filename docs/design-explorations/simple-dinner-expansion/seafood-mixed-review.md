Source methods were browsed, with three actual recipe/technique comparisons per meal. Ratings remain null and observed method patterns are explicitly not reviewer consensus. ## Cooking truth and distinctness

Conservative serial timing leaves34/45 at40minutes or less and all45 at60minutes or less. Hands-on estimates are20minutes or less. The portfolio owner must check the overall160/200 quick target; timers were not shortened to pass a lane quota. Potato steaming precedes acidic tomato inDI513; cooked rice pouches remove a hidden second pot sequence. DI442 cakes bake in a single batch after steam-drying potatoes, reaching160°F.

Substantive replacements: DI436 orange-ginger salmon with carrot/couscous replaces mustard salmon with greenbeans/potatoes (existing meal-kit overlap); DI437 tuna-stuffed potato halves replace tuna melts (LU004); DI440 salmon and tomato polenta replaces salmon parcels (DI166); DI514 bean egg tostadas replace tomato white-bean eggs (BR054/BR059 overlap). Other checked neighbors include tuna noodle casserole, Baja fish tacos, Thai pineapple shrimp fried rice, jambalaya/perloo, fish pie, shakshuka, spinach frittata and tomato-egg stir-fry. Differences are in main component, method, sauce and included side, not only titles.

Measured sauce liquids and ingredient splits use relative shares, not fixed spoonfuls that would fail2x scaling. Fish portions and cakes/meatballs retain size; per-person counts scale. At eight servings, preserve pan depth with two batches/pans. All shrimp is peeled and thawed; raw fish cooking assumes fresh or fully thawed fish, not frozen blocks. Egg dishes use set whites/yolks or160°F, fish145°F, ground meat160°F, poultry165°F.
