## Third culinary comparison decisions

- DI381: [Beef Stroganoff](https://www.recipetineats.com/beef-stroganoff/). Ground beef is economical; separately boiled noodles give more control than the one-pot reference, and sour cream is added off heat.
- DI382: [Beef and Rice with Veggies](https://www.recipetineats.com/ground-beef-and-rice-recipe/). Keep the absorption method, use white rice, and finish with peas, cheddar and pickles rather than a burger bun.
- DI383: [Beef and Rice with Veggies](https://www.recipetineats.com/ground-beef-and-rice-recipe/). Use medium bulgur rather than rice; cabbage and paprika supply the vegetable and seasoning base.
- DI384: [Beef and Rice with Veggies](https://www.recipetineats.com/ground-beef-and-rice-recipe/). Delay zucchini until rice is partly cooked so vegetables do not become watery.
- DI385: [Easy Caprese Pizza](https://www.budgetbytes.com/easy-caprese-pizza/). Pre-cooked naan removes dough work; steak is placed on after the cheese bake to protect its doneness.
- DI386: [Beef and Cabbage Stir Fry](https://www.budgetbytes.com/beef-cabbage-stir-fry/). Use canned lentils to extend beef, and keep raw slaw separate rather than cooking cabbage into the filling.
- DI387: [Oven Roasted Potatoes](https://www.budgetbytes.com/oven-roasted-potatoes/). Small sweet-potato cubes steam directly in the skillet; no oven-roasted halves or cheese lid.
- DI388: [One pot creamy tomato beef pasta](https://www.recipetineats.com/one-pot-creamy-tomato-beef-pasta/). Boil shells separately and dollop ricotta rather than simmering all ingredients in one pot.
- DI389: [Pork noodle stir-fry](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pork-noodle-stir-fry). Use whole seared steak sliced after resting, spaghetti and briefly boiled broccoli; no sweet-chili sauce.
- DI390: [Kielbasa and Cabbage Skillet](https://www.budgetbytes.com/kielbasa-cabbage-skillet/). Adapt the potato-cabbage skillet mechanics to ground beef and green beans, with mustard rather than smoky sausage.
- DI391: [Vegetarian French Dip Sandwiches](https://www.budgetbytes.com/vegetarian-french-dip-sandwiches/). Use softened onions rather than falsely claiming quick caramelization; reduce broth so sandwich bread stays intact.
- DI392: [Easy Taco Soup](https://www.budgetbytes.com/taco-soup/). Keep a looser broth and mild separate spices, finishing with lime and yogurt instead of seasoning packets.
- DI393: [Cauliflower Cheese](https://www.recipetineats.com/cauliflower-cheese/). Add browned beef to a smaller roast-first cauliflower gratin and include bread; no potato mash topping.
- DI394: [Pork noodle stir-fry](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pork-noodle-stir-fry). Turn mince-and-noodle mechanics into a ginger broth; no frying of softened noodles or thick glaze.
- DI395: [Beef Taco Skillet](https://www.budgetbytes.com/beef-taco-skillet/). Use separately cooked steak and bake folded tortillas together; corn and pepper filling replaces beans.
- DI396: [Chili Cornbread Skillet](https://www.budgetbytes.com/chili-cornbread-skillet/). Use a modest fresh batter over a beef-pinto filling, with cheddar inside the batter and no spicy chili blend.
- DI397: [Quick Chicken and Vegetable Risoni](https://www.recipetineats.com/one-pot-chicken-vegetable-parmesan-orzo-risoni/). Use ground beef, zucchini, lemon and feta; maintain a loose stirred orzo finish without a milk sauce.
- DI398: [Roast pork with couscous and ginger yogurt](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/roast-pork-couscous-ginger-yogurt). Serve a moist eggplant-beef sauce over independently hydrated couscous; no roast or ginger yogurt.
- DI399: [Beef and Rice with Veggies](https://www.recipetineats.com/ground-beef-and-rice-recipe/). Use ready-cooked rice in a hot beef-mushroom filling and pre-roast shells, avoiding raw-grain cooking inside zucchini.
- DI400: [Straccetti di Manzo](https://apnews.com/article/22b48d1e59de01f2d549cf0a622fe409). Rest a whole steak before slicing; peaches and beans replace tomatoes and the dressing stays un-reduced.
- DI401: [One pot creamy tomato beef pasta](https://www.recipetineats.com/one-pot-creamy-tomato-beef-pasta/). Use quick shelf-stable potato gnocchi, requiring much less liquid than dry pasta, and add spinach late.
