# Vegetarian dinner desk review — DI451–DI510

All 60 current recipes reread after peer review. Four servings each; 50 are at most 40 minutes, eight are 45, DI491 is 55 and DI483 is 60. Hands-on estimates are 13–20 minutes. These are paper estimates, not measured cooking trials.

## Evidence boundaries

Three browsed component-technique sources are recorded per recipe. They are not three published versions of each original dish. Ratings stay null and no reviewer consensus or kitchen-test evidence is claimed. The recipes, ingredient rationales, equipment annotations and recheck explanations are bound to current source hashes in vegetarian-reviews.json.

## Duplication and repair decisions

Compared existing classic and meal-kit titles, including lunch recipes. LU036 homemade masa/pinto/Oaxaca quesadillas differ from the packaged flour-tortilla, white-bean/corn/cheddar dinner. DI507 uses quick barley and mustard cream instead of a second cheesy rice. Peer review rejected DI467 ricotta-pea gnocchi as too close to DI452 and DI491 tortilla strips as too close to DI453. Their final replacements are sesame-butter gnocchi with edamame/snap peas and a pepper-black-bean cornbread skillet. Both require replacement images. DI483 filling quantities were reduced and a deep 3-quart skillet specified to prevent overflow. Fixed liquid splits are now role-specific measured ingredient lines, and oil divisions are proportional.
