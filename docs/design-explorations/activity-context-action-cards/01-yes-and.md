# Yes-and: What the Primitive Could Unlock

1. **Yes, and the To-do can explain itself.** A compact source reference can
   show the sender or capability, the evidence that mattered, freshness, and
   “Why Kwilt suggested this.” This elevates capture into trustworthy recall.

2. **Yes, and the action can happen where it belongs.** “Open email,” “Choose
   meals,” “Review form,” or “Continue checkout” can return the user to the
   authoritative destination instead of duplicating that product inside
   Activities.

3. **Yes, and one card contract can serve native and external capabilities.**
   Meal Planning, Goals, Screen Time, Gmail, calendar, documents, and retailer
   checkout can all contribute without teaching Activity detail their domain
   rules.

4. **Yes, and recurring Activities can start bounded participation rounds.**
   The organizer's reminder may recur, while each family member's invitation is
   generated from the actual Meal Planning round and expires with it.

5. **Yes, and users can teach Kwilt bounded rules.** After reviewing examples,
   Nina might authorize “Suggest a To-do for messages from the school that
   contain a due date,” without granting blanket auto-creation authority.

6. **Yes, and a source can evolve without duplicate To-dos.** A stable source
   binding and normalized action identity can update the existing card when an
   email thread changes rather than generating another Activity.

7. **Yes, and disconnection can be graceful.** The Activity remains; the card
   explains that Gmail is disconnected or its source is unavailable and offers
   one recovery action.

8. **Yes, and receipts can close the loop.** Capability-owned receipts can show
   that a meal choice was recorded, a draft was created, or a checkout handoff
   occurred without allowing model prose to claim success.

## Anti-pattern check

The expansion fails if every integration creates a bespoke card in
`ActivityDetailScreen`, if external providers can execute arbitrary UI actions,
or if Activities become a noisy universal inbox. The primitive must remain
typed, capability-owned, permission-aware, and reductive.
