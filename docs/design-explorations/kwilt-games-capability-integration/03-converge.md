# Converge: Native Games Capability

## Chosen alternative

Option D: one Games owner and one Games destination under Fun, preserving the complete committed Games product behind narrow Kwilt host adapters.

## Capability delta

Today, unified Kwilt cannot open or complete a game. After this integration, Maya can open Games from Fun and use the full established catalog, utility, player setup and persistence, local play, and committed remote-room paths. The remaining gaps are deployment and device proof, not removed product surface.

## Reductive decisions

- One global Games row, not one row per game.
- No Games-local bottom tab, duplicate settings root, dashboard, or onboarding.
- The shelf chooses; the table plays; native back returns to the shelf.
- Games-specific player identity remains, but sign-in and session ownership come from Kwilt.

## Activation

Games is discovered organically under Fun. Entry lands directly on honest playable choices; no promotional interstitial or permission prompt precedes play.

## Bet

We're betting that Games can feel distinct and joyful while sharing Kwilt's shell, and that narrow adapters can retain existing behavior more truthfully than either a nested app or a reduced catalog. If not, revisit the adapter boundary rather than silently dropping games.

## Success signal

The menu, navigation, complete catalog, rules, player persistence, joining, remote command paths, completion, replay, and back behavior pass automated checks, then the real surface is visually and interactively verified on Simulator and signed devices.
