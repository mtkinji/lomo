# Diverge: Chat-Authored Personal Screen Time Limits

Axis: where typed intent becomes authoritative.

## A. Advice or Settings link

Chat explains App Limits or opens general settings. Lowest implementation cost, but it drops the app and duration and repeats the observed failure. Reject.

## B. Chat-owned rule creation

Chat attempts to select apps and persist a complete rule. It appears direct but violates Apple's token privacy boundary and bypasses native consequence review. Reject.

## C. Typed native rule draft

Chat resolves `subject=self`, `suggestedAppLabel`, `limitMinutes`, and `reset=daily`, then stages a durable native handoff. The canonical personal builder collects the private Apple selection and saves the rule. Device Activity enforces the threshold; Chat claims only that review opened. Choose.

All alternatives leave Arcs, Goals, Activities, and Chapters unchanged, do not block capture, and avoid dashboards, streaks, shame, or anthropomorphic AI.
