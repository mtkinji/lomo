2. **Truthful resume** — incomplete Plaid or Money setup can continue from the last safe capability-owned state. Serves `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`; cost: medium.

4. **Decision as activation** — confirming the evidence, adjusting the plan, or choosing whether to continue is the completion boundary. Serves `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse`; cost: medium.
5. **Post-value expansion** — Plan, Goals, widgets, or app controls appear only after Money has helped once. Serves `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`; cost: medium.
6. **Post-value monetization** — Pro packages continued sync and cross-capability value after proof rather than blocking the first trusted result. Serves `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`; cost: medium.
