# Evaluate Learning: Money Progressive Activation

## Learning questions

- Does Maya understand “Know where I stand before I spend” without finance jargon?
- Does she expect current transaction-backed reality and a decision rather than bill prediction?
- Does the Agent opening preserve momentum without repeating onboarding?
- Can she distinguish concept availability from connected Money access?

## Evidence

Use five to eight moderated Guided Overture sessions. Record task recall, predicted next step, selected offer, concept/live comprehension, and whether the opening question helps the participant name a real decision. Do not record financial values, institutions, merchants, or free-form answers in analytics.

## Decision rule

Advance to native activation wiring when at least 80% predict a setup-or-Summary next step, nobody expects automatic bill detection, and the Money opening produces a concrete decision context without facilitator repair.

Hold if participants read the promise as bookkeeping, a spending score, or an unsupported alerting product.
