# Converge: Money Progressive Activation

## Chosen direction

Choose **Agent-Guided Money Door with exact-entry bypass**.

- Unscoped users may recognize the Money task in Guided Overture and continue with that exact intent in Agent.
- Destination-bearing campaigns, resumptions, widgets, and shields bypass the overture.
- Money owns setup, connection, Summary, correction, the intentional decision, and its receipt.

## Capability delta

Today, Money appears in the portfolio lab as “Catch a bill before it surprises me,” a narrower concept that the native product cannot yet promise end to end.

After the learning release, the lab presents the canonical job: **Know where I stand before I spend**, with a deterministic Agent handoff and an explicit first-trusted-decision contract.

Still intentionally unsupported: production assignment, silent connection, invented bill prediction, or declaring first value from route arrival alone.

## Reductive decisions

- Replace the unsupported bill-alert promise; do not add a second Money beat.
- Keep Money concept-only until runtime proof exists.
- Add no global setup checklist, capability adoption score, or pre-value paywall.
- Treat correction as a valid path to trust.

## Activation contract

`money_intent_selected -> money_handoff_arrived -> setup_started_or_resumed -> transaction_backed_reality_visible -> evidence_confirmed_or_corrected -> intentional_choice -> MoneyFirstTrustedDecision`

Valid first-trusted-decision outcomes are: `continue`, `adjust_plan`, `keep_blocked`, or `correct_truth`. Route arrival, Plaid success, and Summary render are necessary progress signals, not activation.

## Bet

We are betting that a decision-shaped promise will produce better comprehension and trust than a feature or bill-alert promise. If people cannot predict the next step or reach a real decision, revisit the entry promise and native decision surface before promoting Money to live onboarding.
