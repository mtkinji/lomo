# Diverge: Money Progressive Activation

Axis: direct native entry versus Agent-guided entry versus ambient decision entry.

## A. Direct Money Door

“Know where I stand before I spend” opens setup when disconnected and Summary when connected. It is fastest for exact campaign intent, but gives unscoped users little orientation.

## B. Agent-Guided Money Door

Guided Overture preserves the chosen Money promise in Agent, asks one concrete question, and hands off to Money for connection and truth. It best fits Kwilt’s accepted continuous-context onboarding, but needs a reliable capability handoff and authoritative completion signal.

## C. In-the-Moment Money Door

A widget, app-control shield, or transaction review becomes the first-use entry. It is closest to the hot decision, but cannot be the only acquisition path and depends on specialized native setup.

All three preserve Money’s native data authority, do not force Arc/Goal creation, avoid finance-score dashboards, and keep permissions just in time.
