# Frame: Money Progressive Activation

## What the user said

> Then let’s create it.

## Restated in user voice

When household spending is about to change the plan, Maya wants to know where things actually stand and make one calm choice, so that Money earns trust without turning her into a finance administrator.

## Target audience

`audience-aspirational-family-organizers` — people coordinating ordinary family life who need useful financial truth without a budgeting hobby.

## Representative persona

Maya is deciding whether a household purchase still fits this month. She wants a trustworthy answer and a clear next move, not a tour of Kwilt or a setup checklist.

## Hero anchor

`jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter` — Money matters when it helps Maya carry family intentions into a real decision.

## Job flow step

`job-flow-maya-review-budget-reality-before-spending`: entry and repeat trust are the weakest seams. Native Money can show and correct reality, but the product does not yet connect an acquisition promise to capability-owned setup, resume, and an observable first trusted decision.

## Active anchors

- `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending` — the direct Money job.
- `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse` — the decision should happen close to spending.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` — transaction truth, permissions, and results must remain inspectable.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Fit the system`

- Guided Overture owns task-oriented orientation and Agent continuity.
- Money owns connection, native destinations, corrections, decisions, and receipts.
- Exact Money links and authoritative resumes bypass generic orientation.
- Money is currently a concept offer in the lab; production first-run remains unwired.

## Aspirational design challenge

How might we help Maya move from a recognizable Money promise to one trusted household decision, while preserving capability ownership, financial truth, and Kwilt’s calm progressive-onboarding model?

## Out of scope

Production first-run assignment, a generic pre-value paywall, predictive bill alerts, household invite creation, or claims of signed-device/TestFlight proof.
