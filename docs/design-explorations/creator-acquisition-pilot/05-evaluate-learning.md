# Evaluate Learning: creator acquisition pilot

## Learning questions

1. Does a single demonstrated advanced Screen Time job attract Marcus more effectively than a broad Kwilt feature story?
2. Do people understand that the creator code identifies a campaign and activation experience, while Apple still controls the subscription offer?
3. Does the seven-day challenge increase first advanced-rule completion and paid renewal?
4. Can first-qualified attribution, lifecycle reconciliation, commission holds, and refund reversals operate without disputes or private-data exposure?
5. Which creator-audience fit produces retained paid value rather than cheap installs or trials?

## Evidence plan

Support for the bet:

- at least 50 valid claims across the pilot;
- at least three of five creators generate one or more qualified first paid periods;
- claimed cohorts complete the promised Screen Time activation at a higher rate than comparable organic Pro-intent cohorts;
- modeled 12-month net revenue / fully loaded creator acquisition cost is at least 3x;
- commission and refund reconciliation has no unexplained material variance;
- disclosure, support, and attribution disputes are rare and resolvable from the audit trail.

Disconfirming evidence:

- creator content produces clicks but not valid claims or paid value;
- customers expect the code to grant Pro or stack a discount;
- most creators cannot produce a qualified renewal;
- refund, fraud, or support burden erases expected contribution;
- activation depends on Screen Time claims that cannot pass signed-device proof or App Review.

## Instrumentation

Track bounded identifiers and enums for campaign landing, App Store handoff,
code claim, challenge start/completion, paid intent, paywall, trial/purchase,
first paid period, hold, approved commission, reversal, and payout batch.

Do not track selected apps, rule sentences, child names, Activities, Money
categories, creator tax/bank data, or content-level browsing histories.

## Decision rule

After six weeks plus the 30-day commission hold for the final qualifying event:

- **Proceed:** permanent threshold passes; expand carefully and evaluate automated partner operations.
- **Revise:** demand exists but one promise, creator segment, claim step, or compensation rule clearly underperforms.
- **Retire:** qualified renewal and payback evidence is absent, or the channel requires unacceptable claims, privacy, support, or App Review risk.

No result justifies adding a full attribution platform before the specific gap
and expected volume are demonstrated.
