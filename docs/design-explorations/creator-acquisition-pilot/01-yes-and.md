# Yes-And: creator acquisition pilot

Original idea: give creators a code that benefits the customer and earns the
creator a kickback when the customer subscribes.

**Yes, and what if it could demonstrate one repeatable job instead of advertising a feature list?**

- Serves: `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse`
- Job elevation: the audience sees the exact moment Kwilt changes.
- New value: creator content becomes product education and demand validation.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no broad productivity promise.

**Yes, and what if each creator supplied a useful seven-day challenge or rule template?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: the claimed code leads to first value, not just a discount.
- New value: the customer benefit is differentiated without distorting subscription pricing.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the challenge is optional and non-shaming.

**Yes, and what if payout required a real paid renewal after the trial?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: creator incentives align with durable customer value.
- New value: Kwilt avoids paying for low-intent trials or refunded transactions.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no pressure mechanic is exposed to the customer.

**Yes, and what if creator claims also became a product-claim approval system?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: customers hear only capabilities Kwilt can actually deliver.
- New value: campaign approval is linked to the release-evidence ledger.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; prevents confident overclaiming.

**Yes, and what if creators received aggregated job-completion feedback rather than private user data?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: partners can improve the teaching without observing intimate behavior.
- New value: useful optimization with a durable privacy boundary.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if thresholds prevent re-identification.

**Yes, and what if Apple offer codes became an optional later experiment rather than the program foundation?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: discounts remain truthful and store-managed without corrupting attribution.
- New value: controlled price tests after the base program works.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if offer eligibility and non-stacking are explicit.

## Frame recommendation

**Run design-thinking-loop with an expanded frame.** The useful product is not
“influencer codes”; it is a creator acquisition and learning system that connects
an approved promise, post-install activation, Apple-paid conversion, retained
value, and auditable compensation.
