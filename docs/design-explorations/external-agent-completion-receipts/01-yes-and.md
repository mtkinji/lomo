# Yes, and — adjacencies

1. **Source confidence:** every receipt names the connected app that made the change.
2. **Exact-object continuation:** when a stable native destination exists, a later iteration may open it directly.
3. **Safe correction:** reversible operations can eventually expose a receipt-backed undo, without making every receipt interactive.
4. **Retry quietness:** idempotent replays collapse into the original visible outcome.
5. **Bounded attention:** only a few recent completions appear; there is no unread count or pressure to clear them.
6. **Connection accountability:** the full per-connection history remains in Settings → Apps & connections.
7. **Shared action authority:** MCP, Phone Agent, and Kwilt Chat converge on the same server-owned operation and receipt contracts.

## Frame recommendation

Expand “stop creating Chats” into **agentic completion with calm in-app receipts**. The sidebar symptom and the Home opportunity share one architectural contract: the initiating surface owns the interaction; Kwilt owns durable truth and a quiet audit projection.
