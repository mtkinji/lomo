# Yes-And: live-better-goal-crossover

Original idea: Kwilt Money can use spending advice as a crossover moment that encourages Blaire to create a Kwilt goal for living better.

**Yes, and what if it could...** turn budget advice into one concrete goal draft instead of a list of tips.

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- Job elevation: moves from "I should spend smarter" to "I am choosing one meaningful change."
- New value: advice becomes actionable without bloating Budget.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the draft is optional and editable.

**Yes, and what if it could...** use budget evidence as the reason for the goal.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: the recommendation feels grounded in what happened, not generic money wisdom.
- New value: Blaire can inspect why Budget suggested the goal.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the app uses humble language and shows the evidence.

**Yes, and what if it could...** offer two paths: fix the budget rule here, or build a goal in Kwilt.

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: distinguishes a settings problem from a life-pattern problem.
- New value: Budget can say, "this is a rule adjustment" or "this is a behavior goal."
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the choice is simple and not a wizard.

**Yes, and what if it could...** create a starter Activity in Kwilt for the first small action.

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: the user leaves with a next move, not only a goal title.
- New value: stronger follow-through loop.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only if capture-first is preserved and the user confirms.

**Yes, and what if it could...** include money-pattern reflections in later Kwilt Chapters.

- Serves: `jtbd-make-sense-of-the-season`
- Job elevation: spending changes become part of the user's season story.
- New value: deeper cross-product meaning.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: risky for V1; defer until data sharing and consent are mature.

**Yes, and what if it could...** use app-gate moments to suggest a goal only after repeated "leave blocked" or "open anyway" decisions.

- Serves: `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse`
- Job elevation: the goal suggestion appears when the user has shown a live tension.
- New value: better timing than a generic Plan-tab prompt.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if it never blocks access or nags.

**Yes, and what if it could...** become a family stewardship Arc in Kwilt later.

- Serves: `jtbd-see-who-im-becoming`
- Job elevation: the money behavior sits inside a broader identity direction.
- New value: Budget can feed Kwilt's becoming model.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: defer; do not force an Arc before the goal value is proven.

## Job Elevation

The bigger job is not "give advice." It is "help me convert money evidence into a chosen life pattern." That belongs at the boundary between Budget's proof and Kwilt's goals.

## Frame Recommendation

**Run design-thinking-loop with an expanded frame** - from "Budget gives advice" to "Budget turns one evidence-backed money pattern into an optional Kwilt goal draft."
