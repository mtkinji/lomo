# Yes-and: Pass the Pattern rhythm elimination

Original idea: turn Pass the Pattern into last-player-standing play over changing genre beats, with immediate, equal-length notes that combine musically.

**Yes, and what if it could make the room's pattern sound like a song?**

- Serves: `jtbd-help-us-enjoy-being-together`
- Job elevation: remembering becomes collaborative music-making.
- New value: even a short run produces a shared audible artifact.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; the music is play, not a score or reward.

**Yes, and what if each elimination opened a fresh musical round?**

- Serves: `jtbd-help-us-enjoy-being-together`
- Job elevation: eliminated players can keep listening to a changing table performance.
- New value: the game has an arc without points or setup.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass when the copy stays neutral and non-shaming.

**Yes, and what if players could author or save songs?**

- Serves: `jtbd-help-us-enjoy-being-together`
- Job elevation: play could become a reusable creative artifact.
- New value: creation beyond the table round.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: failure for this release; it adds administration and distracts from immediate play.

**Yes, and what if phones synchronized the groove remotely?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: distant family could make music together.
- New value: remote rhythm play.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: defer; latency and server authority need a separate truthful contract.

**Yes, and what if the beat adapted to player skill?**

- Serves: `jtbd-help-us-enjoy-being-together`
- Job elevation: mixed-age groups could stay in a shared flow.
- New value: less manual difficulty choice.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: defer opaque adaptation; fixed genre tempos are easier to understand and trust.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with the original frame.** The focused local-table game captures the job elevation. Song authoring, adaptive difficulty, and remote synchronization should not inflate this learning release.
