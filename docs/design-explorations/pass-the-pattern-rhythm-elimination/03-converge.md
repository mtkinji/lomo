# Converge: Beat-window elimination

## Chosen alternative

Pass the Pattern becomes a local last-player-standing rhythm-memory game. A four-style groove rotation supplies a known pulse. Players watch, repeat, and add one immediate 504 ms note on successive beats. A wrong note or an off-beat press eliminates only that player. The next round starts with the next survivor and the next groove; the final survivor wins.

## Capability delta

Today, one miss ends the group's pattern and starts everyone over. Taps sound after release and an awaited rewind, and no beat determines whether timing matters.

After this change, the group can continue through eliminations, hear touch-down immediately, play against a visible and audible pulse, and reach one winner while the genre changes each round.

Still intentionally unsupported: scoring, difficulty settings, saved compositions, adaptive tempo, and remote rhythm play.

## Reductive decisions

- Remove the difficulty picker; genre rotation carries pace and variety without setup.
- Keep one dominant action during handoff/watch/result states.
- Keep the existing beat pad and player launcher rather than add a music screen.
- Do not add a separate failure sound over a wrong musical note.
- Do not expose milliseconds, accuracy scores, or streaks.

## Activation

The groove begins when local play begins. The first handoff teaches one sentence: tap each note when the pulse lands. Later rounds change style automatically and need no configuration.

## Bet

We're betting that a generous, legible pulse will turn memory repetition into shared music without making families debate timing. If misses feel arbitrary, we will widen or remove beat judgment before expanding the music system.

## Success signal

In table play, taps sound on touch-down, players naturally move or count with the beat, a miss removes only the active player, survivors understand that play continues, and the group finishes with one winner without rules explanation.
