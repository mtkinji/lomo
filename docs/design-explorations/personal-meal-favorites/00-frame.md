## Aspirational design challenge

How might we let Maya quietly remember meals she personally wants to find again while browsing, without confusing private preference with the shared family Meal Plan or adding another management surface?

## Anchor assessment

- `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`: a remembered meal shortens the path from browsing to a realistic next plan.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`: the signal must be durable, private to the person who chose it, reversible, and truthful offline.
