# Evaluate learning

The release is useful if a person can mark a meal while browsing, leave and return, and immediately recognize and retrieve it without mistaking the action for adding it to the Meal Plan.

Validation should specifically watch for:

- accidental taps between heart and plan controls;
- whether the two corner controls feel too visually heavy over food photography;
- whether people understand that favorites are personal while the Meal Plan is shared;
- whether the first `Your favorites` shelf shortens repeat discovery.
