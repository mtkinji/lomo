# Diverge

## 1. Quiet heart plus a personal shelf

Place a compact heart at the bottom-right of each meal image. Favoriting is immediate and reversible. When favorites exist, a `Your favorites` shelf appears in Meals.

## 2. One combined action cluster

Put heart and plan controls together in the top-right. This saves corners but visually conflates a private preference with a shared-plan action and makes the image feel tool-heavy.

## 3. Context-menu-only favorite

Keep cards visually minimal and put Favorite behind a long press or overflow menu. This preserves the image but makes a frequent lightweight action hard to discover.
