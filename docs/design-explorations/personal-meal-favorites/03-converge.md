# Converge

Choose the quiet heart plus a personal shelf.

- The plus/check remains top-right and means membership in the shared Meal Plan.
- The heart sits bottom-right and means “I want to find this again.”
- Both use Kwilt's existing 36-point frosted floating-control material.
- The default heart is outlined; the selected heart is filled charcoal without introducing green.
- Favorites are stored per person and cached for calm offline recall.
- `Your favorites` is a shelf within Meals, not a global navigation destination.

The bet is that an always-visible but quiet heart improves recall more than the small amount of added card chrome harms browsing.
