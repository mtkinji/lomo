# Converge: Plan downvotes

## Chosen direction

Choose **Signed downvote**, but reject a single net Reddit score.

The row shows two distinct signals:

- positive emoji reactions, counted as support;
- a down-arrow with its own count, counted as “not for me.”

Tapping the down-arrow toggles the viewer's downvote. If the viewer already has
a positive reaction, downvoting replaces it. Tapping the up-arrow while
downvoted opens the positive picker and the selected emoji replaces the
downvote. Participant names remain revealable for both signals.

## Why this wins

- It answers the explicit request with the smallest model extension.
- It preserves one response per person and the current Plan drawer.
- It shows disagreement without collapsing mixed household input into one score.
- It leaves the final call with the organizer.

## Sorting

Lifecycle remains primary. Within a lifecycle group, sort by positive support
descending, then downvotes ascending, then recency. A downvote is decision
context, not a negative point that can erase several people's enthusiasm.

## Capability delta

Today a household member cannot distinguish “I do not want this” from silence.

After this change they can register one reversible, attributable downvote and
the organizer can see both support and disagreement before shopping.

Still intentionally unsupported: anonymous voting, comments, vetoes, automatic
removal, recommendation training, and majority-rule grocery selection.

## Reduction decisions

- No new screen, label, explanation, notification, or settings surface.
- No persistent “score.”
- No reason prompt.
- Up and down controls share one compact reaction row.

## Activation

The affordance is learned in place. No coachmark is necessary: the paired
up/down arrows communicate the decision, while accessibility labels say
“Upvote” and “Downvote.”

## Stated bet

We are betting that attributable disagreement is more useful than ambiguous
silence and remains calm when it is reversible and never automatically decides.
If family members avoid it or experience it as interpersonal rejection, revisit
with the private “not for me” model.

## Success signal

A household uses downvotes to avoid at least one unwanted grocery commitment,
while members still nominate recipes and use positive reactions normally.
