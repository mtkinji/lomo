# Monetization release readiness — convergence

## Chosen direction

Build the layered monetization control plane from Option C while preserving the existing contextual paywall interaction.

## Stated bet

If Free remains a complete organizing and household system, and Kwilt asks for payment only when a person intentionally requests ongoing connectivity or substantial assistance, more people will reach trusted recurring value and the Pro offer will feel proportionate. If every paid path is enforced and explained consistently, conversion can grow without sacrificing trust or creating unbounded provider cost.

## Fixed decisions

- One customer-facing entitlement: `pro`.
- A one-month Apple introductory offer per eligible person in the subscription
  group; confirming it starts an auto-renewable subscription and activates full
  Pro. Apple manages the payment method; Kwilt does not collect card details.
- Individual and Family products unlock the same capability bundle; Family changes Apple billing sharing only.
- Free retains unlimited core structure and participation.
- The customer-facing launch offer is led by Money and Budgets from first setup,
  advanced personal Screen Time rules, and Family Screen Time administration.
- Hard-Pro cloud AI jobs, AI scheduling, background/proactive AI, and external
  agents/connectors may be supporting Pro inclusions after their own proof gate;
  they are not headline purchase reasons.
- Cook Mode and Live Conversation launch as `free_preview` capabilities. Free
  and Pro receive the same enabled MVP with no paywall. Authentication, bounded
  provider cost, telemetry, and independent exposure flags protect the service;
  the flags default on but can hide either preview. Neither is a headline store,
  subscription, creator, or paid-conversion claim.
- Kwilt does not add a manual account or transaction-entry product. After Money
  expires, retained history remains readable while sync and active editing stop.
- Standard cloud actions use 50 Free / 1,000 Pro monthly successful-action allowances.
- Contextual paywalls appear only at explicit paid intent.
- Free Screen Time contains progressive, secondary upgrade paths from the rules
  overview, premium builder choices, successful first-rule moment, rule detail,
  family learning, and Chat. Each path previews the intended paid outcome and
  preserves the person's draft and return destination.
- Trusted server state, not `x-kwilt-is-pro`, authorizes paid provider use.
- Cancellation preserves access through the paid-through date; cleanup begins only after confirmed expiration or refund.
- Confirmed expiration or refund deactivates every advanced personal and family
  Screen Time rule as a whole. The definition remains readable, native release
  is receipt-backed, and resubscription requires reviewed reactivation.

## Trade-offs accepted

- The release may market fewer paid pillars than exist in source if a pillar lacks signed-runtime proof.
- A variable-cost capability may remain a Free preview while Kwilt learns its
  value, provided cost and exposure controls do not masquerade as a paywall.
- The launch program is larger than a UI-gating task because purchase truth, provider authorization, downgrade, public copy, and support are part of the same customer promise.
- The first release uses one clear bundle rather than experimenting with feature-specific add-ons or weighted AI pricing.
