# Monetization release readiness — yes-and expansion

The offered idea is to prepare the new capabilities for contextual paywalls and launch. The underlying opportunity is larger: make monetization a trustworthy product control plane rather than a collection of locked buttons.

## Adjacencies

1. **Yes, and make the public promise release-gated.** A capability can exist in source without appearing in the paywall value list, App Store copy, or site until its own signed-runtime evidence passes.
2. **Yes, and enforce the intent across every path.** Screen taps, restored navigation, deep links, Chat tools, MCP, scheduled work, and direct server requests all resolve through the same capability policy.
3. **Yes, and make downgrade part of the product.** People keep readable history, free editing, exports, and safety-reducing controls; Kwilt stops new provider cost only after confirmed expiration.
4. **Yes, and give support one inspectable truth.** RevenueCat purchase state, the server mirror, internal grants, client state, lifecycle events, and paid-service cleanup can be reconciled without reading customer content.
5. **Yes, and make the marketing system consistent.** The in-app paywall, subscription screen, website, Terms, support FAQ, App Store metadata, screenshots, and review notes use the same Free/Pro/trial language.
6. **Yes, and measure unit economics by paid intent.** Conversion, cloud
   actions, and provider cost can be segmented by Money and Budgets, advanced
   personal Screen Time, Family Screen Time, advanced AI, background work,
   external agents, Cook Mode, and Live Conversation. The two Free MVP previews
   retain their own usage and margin reporting while staying out of paid
   conversion denominators unless a later decision reclassifies them.
7. **Yes, and preserve a rollback lane.** Additive backend changes and server-controlled capability exposure let Kwilt pause an unready or costly paid promise without removing the free product or corrupting entitlement history.
8. **Yes, and turn future paid features into declared policy.** New capabilities cannot silently invent a paywall reason; they must declare access class, paid intent, trusted enforcement boundary, downgrade behavior, analytics, marketing readiness, and proof owner.

## Triage

These adjacencies belong in the current launch program because they close cross-surface trust and operational gaps. They do not change the accepted price points or create additional tiers.
