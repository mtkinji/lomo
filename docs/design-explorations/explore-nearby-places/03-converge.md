# Converge: Explore Nearby Places

## Chosen direction

Choose **Segmented Places drawer**.

- The resting bottom control becomes **Places**.
- The drawer has **Nearby** and **My Places** segments.
- Nearby offers quarter-mile, half-mile, and one-mile search radii; half-mile is the default.
- Nearby pins appear only while the Nearby drawer is open.
- Selecting a row centers and selects its pin; tapping a pin selects its row.
- My Places preserves visited-place search and collected markers.
- Panning the map does not silently rerun search; the user chooses **Search this area**.

## Reductive decisions

- No separate recommendation destination or map mode.
- No automatic Mission, To-do, Saved Place, or fog clearing.
- No permanent travel state or recommendation notification.
- No reviews feed, category dashboard, or preference setup.

## Stated bet

We are betting that one Places drawer can hold both possibility and memory as long as the segments and marker treatments keep them visibly distinct. If users still confuse recommendations with visits, strengthen the pin and copy distinction before adding more state.
