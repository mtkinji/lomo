# Yes-And: Explore Nearby Places

## Original idea

Show nearby recommendations as map pins synchronized with a list drawer.

## Job elevation

This can become a gentle progression from possibility to lived memory: notice a place, inspect it, walk toward it, and let route evidence decide whether it joins history.

## Adjacencies retained for later

- A user may save a possibility without creating a Mission.
- A deliberate **Make this an outing** action may later create a Mission.
- A verified visit may automatically become a canonical Place after the route-catalog system is proven.
- Corrections such as **Not for me** may improve future ranking without uploading location history.
- Family invitations may use Missions because they already include sender, recipient, objective, and completion contract.

## Frame recommendation

Run the loop with the original frame. Nearby discovery is coherent as a lightweight Explore layer; Missions remain downstream.
