# Diverge: Explore Nearby Places

Axis: explicit switching versus map-layer switching versus inferred context.

## A. Segmented Places drawer

One **Places** control opens a drawer with **Nearby** and **My Places**. The selected segment owns the list and map pins. This is explicit, reuses the current surface, and avoids a new application mode.

## B. Map-layer toggle

Nearby recommendations become another option beside Fog, Places, and My Path. This keeps the map configurable but hides discovery and does not give the synchronized list an obvious home.

## C. Context-changing drawer

The drawer automatically favors nearby results when Kwilt believes the user is traveling. This reduces one tap but creates an unnecessary travel inference and makes the surface unpredictable.

All alternatives preserve capture-first behavior and reject streaks, pressure badges, paid placement, and automatic Mission creation.
