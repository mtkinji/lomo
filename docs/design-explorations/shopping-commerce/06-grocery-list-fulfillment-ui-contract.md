# Grocery List and fulfillment UI contract

Job: When the household is preparing to shop, the user needs to see and update one familiar grocery checklist, so they can cover what they already have and fulfill only what remains.

Authority chain: Andrew's explicit list-first and direct-fulfillment decisions -> `03-converge.md` Grocery Flywheel -> Kwilt UI constitution -> Candidate inventory/list and persistent-decision-region patterns -> existing Recipe ingredient checklist treatment.

Three-second read: This is the household's complete in-store grocery checklist; checked items are covered and carted items are labeled without being checked. The white bottom pill begins `Shop online`: first use collects pickup or delivery and an ordered list of actionable online destinations, while returning use opens `Order this list` with one truthful executable outcome. A circular plus to its right expands into an item composer in the same dock. When a supported local pickup store is the outcome, the user sees its consumer banner and store discovery remains a map with nearby results in a persistent drawer, not a ZIP-code form. After a store is selected, fulfillment becomes an exception-light handoff cart: the ready count and retailer are immediate, deterministic matches stay collapsed, and only ambiguous or unavailable lines demand review.

Primary action: `Shop online` on the list, `Save and continue` after fulfillment and one ordered list of actionable online destinations, then one `Build my pickup cart` or provider-specific action on `Order this list`. `Shop N remaining` appears only after a retailer has acknowledged some items in its cart. Inside a Kroger-backed handoff cart, `Add N items to [retailer banner]` confirms the current product/package quantities. When the exact retailer-store pairing is not already confirmed, it becomes `Set pickup store at [retailer]`, then `Yes, add N items` after the retailer round-trip; no cart write occurs first. Pickup scheduling, fees, substitutions, payment, and checkout happen once, at the retailer. Entering fulfillment is the user's explicit signal that the current remainder is ready for retailer matching; no separate list-review confirmation is required.

Primary information: Grocery items grouped by store section, their quantities, and covered state.

Secondary information: The contributing Meal Plan in the familiar top-right Plan pill, the circular add-item control, offline/sync state, a compact selected-store selector, and quiet per-item `In Smith's cart` receipts. In store discovery, current-location crosshairs and a collapsed ZIP search are secondary to the map and nearby results.

Reveal later: `Change` reopens stable fulfillment and the ordered actionable-destination list; `Try another retailer` reveals lower-ranked actionable outcomes without turning the page into a comparison dashboard. `Add store` contains only approved online destinations and supported nearby-store discovery. Costco, generic `Other`, independents, and co-ops do not appear until Kwilt has an executable online handoff for them; the complete ordinary Grocery list continues to serve in-store shopping. Corporate ownership stays internal, so a Kroger-backed route is always labeled Smith's, King Soopers, Fred Meyer, or the exact banner returned for the selected store. Amazon and Walmart appear as product-link assistance only when their exact mobile surfaces and link formats are approved; a link never claims cart coverage or price. Before a person has ever initiated `Shop online`, one coachmark on the Groceries list spotlights the first still-needed row: `Already have something? Check it off here. It won’t be sent to your online cart.` It requires at least one purchasable grocery row, dismisses durably, pauses behind the capability menu or item composer, and never becomes a tour. Starting an online-shopping flow suppresses it on later lists even if it was never dismissed. Tapping the dock plus expands a focused one-line composer where the control was tapped; submitting adds the item and restores the shopping action. Checked-item review and list maintenance live in the header menu; item editing remains on long press; supported-store choice and product matching appear only when that capability is selected. Store discovery pre-searches only when foreground location is already authorized, uses the crosshairs to ask on demand, and reveals ZIP entry only from the search affordance. A person may choose `Set as my store`; that preference skips discovery on later visits until they choose to change stores. Choosing a store immediately starts product matching; there is no separate matching confirmation step. Deterministic and remembered matches populate a collapsed Ready section. `Edit` reveals alternatives, Needs review contains only consequential exceptions, `Remove` excludes a line from this retailer pass, and quantity changes count retail packages rather than recipe units. Provider authorization occurs only when adding the prepared cart. Before the first write for an exact pickup store, or after a store/account change, the user confirms that store in the correct banner; the person-scoped receipt is explicitly user-confirmed because the public API cannot verify retailer store state. The selected retailer owns pickup timing and fees, substitutions, payment, checkout, and the final total. Up to three savings suggestions may appear only when current same-cart evidence and conservative package normalization support them; accepting a saving is reversible and never changes retailer priority.

Scan order: Groceries title and Plan context -> grouped checklist -> `Shop online` or `Shop N remaining` dock action. On first use: fulfillment -> one ordered list of actionable online destinations -> add, remove, or drag only if needed -> save. On return: `Order this list` and saved default -> one executable outcome -> consequential exceptions -> quiet alternatives.

Must not add: An occasion classifier, an in-person itinerary, retailer scoring weights, introductory count copy, a separate `Review what I already have` button, a `List looks right` confirmation, `Choose products` above a cart, product checkboxes/radios in the default cart, repeating Need/Have labels, repeating Why actions, green CTA styling, mixed-cart or cross-retailer price optimization, a full catalog of alternatives in the default scan, automatic checkout, order inference, or a second durable Kwilt list/basket object.

Behavior sources: Check toggles are Andrew's explicit already-have/purchased model and the current grocery offline queue; they do not carry into the cart. Direct one-store fulfillment is Andrew's explicit decision and the Grocery Flywheel. One Kroger authorization supports Kroger-family banners; Smith's is the current local store, not a separate connection. Store search and matching precede authorization. Store-specific Product API results provide product imagery, regular/promo item prices, and curbside product availability at match time. Kwilt may total those observed item prices as an estimate, never a quote. The public Cart API accepts UPC, retail package quantity, and pickup modality but does not return cart contents, a quote, fees, taxes, time slots, or order evidence. A provider acknowledgement creates a separate per-item cart receipt and removes that item from the next matching pass without checking it off. The retailer owns substitutions, scheduling, payment, checkout, and current order status.

Provider capability levels: `cart_prepare` requires proved store/area evidence, fulfillment-filtered product evidence, and an acknowledged cart-write path. `product_links` requires exact-surface approval plus a configured qualifying-link format, opens only in the retailer app or system browser, and may remember explicit person-reported progress without claiming coverage. `remembered_only` remains an internal legacy or demand state and is excluded from the online priority list. `unavailable` identifies an integration or mode that is disabled, expired, unapproved, unconfigured, or unproved and is also excluded.

Product-link handoff: The actionable starter list contains every currently executable destination, appends newly supported destinations without disturbing the person's existing order, and remembers explicit removal. When Amazon ranks first, `Shop online` bypasses the retailer overview and prepares the whole list in a transient interstitial. The moment reports real preparation, then the provider-supported ready count and exact remainder; it waits for explicit `Open Amazon` consent before opening an approved batch handoff. Every uncertain item stays on the Grocery list. On return, one compact receipt reports only that Amazon opened and what remains; it does not claim checkout or purchase. Internal builds without provider evidence identify that the Amazon cart handoff is not connected; they do not present example matches as ready, expose a disabled cart action, or open a fake cart. Walmart retains its explicit link-assistance pass until an equivalent batch provider contract is proved. `Use another retailer` reveals lower-ranked ready destinations as recovery.

Learning-release gates: Release A is the preference shell plus Kroger-backed pickup concierge and may claim only source, Simulator, signed-device, TestFlight, and production evidence actually observed. Release B enables Kroger delivery only after an independent disposable-cart drill proves delivery product filtering, modality, and market semantics. Release C enables Amazon or Walmart assistance only after approval of Kwilt's exact mobile surface and a live qualifying-link attribution test. Costco remains outside the online priority list. These gates are independent; completing source code cannot enable a later claim.

Unresolved decisions: Provider-authoritative purchase, replacement, delivery, pickup, cart removal, and abandoned-cart states are not currently available from the Kroger cart handoff. Do not infer them from cart addition. Kroger delivery semantics and live Amazon/Walmart qualifying-link formats remain external proof questions.

Required states: Loading; no list yet with an illustrated Plan-oriented empty state; an existing list with nothing to pick up and an add-item-oriented empty state; populated review-needed list; first-visit already-have coachmark; covered and needed items; To Do composer-derived add state and keyboard; offline and queued changes; stale list after Plan revision; no remaining items; first-use preferences; returning preferences; no actionable online destination; an approved link-only retailer; fulfillment preparation failure; Smith's disconnected; location already authorized, not requested, denied, or unavailable; ZIP fallback; empty and failed supported-store search; API-provided coordinates and client-geocoded fallback markers; preferred store restored; matching; collapsed Ready; Needs review; Not found; savings evidence; cart-added; and exact-remainder fallback.
