# Pet Engine Study 55 — The Breeze Finds Your Hand

## Frame and system alignment

> The first playable moment should feel like the tiny anime world noticed the
> person and made room for their hand—not like the person discovered a hidden
> demo gesture.

Charlie, a provisional teen participant within
`audience-aspirational-family-organizers`, has opened Pet because Moss and the
meadow feel worth visiting. The current engine can already turn wind into a
golden leaf that Moss tracks, chases, and catches differently at every life
stage. But that toy is scheduled as a later ambient event and its gesture is
explained mainly in the lab inspector. The most playful proof is therefore easy
to miss.

This serves the underserved non-metric progress step in
`job-flow-sarah-see-who-im-becoming`, currently scored 3, while protecting the
trust required for a calm return.

```yaml
serves: [jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools, jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life]
```

Constraint posture: **Fit and extend the living-day director.** Reuse the
existing breeze arrival, habitat acting, wind brace, golden leaf physics,
stage-shaped catch vocabulary, direct pointer handling, camera, soundscape, and
contextual dock. Add no tutorial modal, joystick, quest, reward, persistent
onboarding flag, new sprite row, or second toy.

**Design challenge:** How might the meadow offer Charlie one immediately
understandable piece of direct play after reunion, while preserving quiet,
discovery, and Moss's autonomy?

## Divergence

### Explain every gesture

Add a first-run overlay listing tap, drag, swipe, pinch, and tree gestures. This
would improve comprehension but replace the world with instructions at the
exact moment immersion matters most.

### Add a permanent play button

Expose `Play with Moss` beside Focus. This would be obvious, but it would make
the capability another action menu and hide the causal relationship between
wind, leaf, hand, and creature.

### Let the first breeze find the hand

After reunion and one quiet beat, the living-day director authors a breeze. The
habitat and Moss notice it first; the existing golden leaf then crosses the
scene. While it is touchable, the contextual dock says only what the hand can
do now. Holding it changes the copy to stage-aware throwing possibility. Once
released, the interface yields and Moss completes the catch through the form's
existing physical vocabulary.

## Convergence

Choose **let the first breeze find the hand**.

The opening playable sentence is:

1. Moss completes the existing reunion;
2. the meadow rests for one short, authored breath;
3. breeze arrives through the existing weather grammar;
4. Moss plants and braces before the old tree releases its golden leaf;
5. the dock says `Catch the golden leaf` and `Hold it, drag it, then let go`;
6. grabbing the leaf changes the invitation to `Moss is reading your hand`;
7. Baby can make a grounded catch, Young can spring, and Guardian can meet a
   high toss in the air;
8. release removes the instruction while Moss owns the complete approach,
   contact, and recovery;
9. the ordinary living-day sequence resumes only after the toy has returned.

We're betting that **one diegetic invitation in the first calm minute** will
teach the larger control language more powerfully than a gesture list. If the
opening feels pushy, repetitive, or like a tutorial event, lengthen the quiet
beat or reduce the dock copy before removing the causal breeze.

## UI contract

Job: When Charlie returns to Moss's meadow, they need to discover one delightful
way their hand can affect the world, so they can feel like a participant rather
than a spectator.

Primary action: Catch, move, and release the golden leaf directly in the meadow.

Must show: the breeze and leaf in world space; one current gesture instruction;
Moss visibly tracking the same object.

Reveal later: toss height changes with maturity; other gestures remain organic
discoveries.

Must not add: modal onboarding, gesture carousel, play button, progress state,
reward, score, inventory, pointer trail, or anthropomorphic dialogue.

Reuse map: living-day director → scene timing; weather episode → causal breeze;
wind leaf → direct toy and physics; contextual dock → transient instruction;
existing stage clips → catch result.

Behavior sources: Study 40 keeps the meadow world-first; Studies 42, 45, and 50
make reach maturity-shaped; the portable wind-leaf contract owns physics and
contact; Study 54 preserves calm daily closure.

Unresolved decisions: none for the private learning release.

Required states: reunion, short quiet, weather arrival, leaf invitation, held
leaf, released catch, ignored invitation, Focus interruption, Reduce Motion.

Proof path: 390 × 844 private site; reset; observe reunion-to-breeze timing;
grab, drag, and release the leaf low and high across Baby, Young, and Guardian;
verify ignored and reduced-motion paths settle without an unfinished need.

## Portable engine contract

- The first living-day episode in each capability visit is `wind-play`.
- It begins only after reunion and the initial quiet interval.
- `wind-play` delegates to the existing directed breeze episode; it does not
  spawn or animate a leaf independently.
- The episode remains active through notice, brace, invitation, held movement,
  catch, and recovery, and completes only when the world is quiet again.
- Subsequent living-day episodes retain roam, memory, rest, visitor, and weather
  variety.
- Direct touch, Focus, care, evolution, evening, and explicit lab controls can
  interrupt the opening exactly as they interrupt any ambient episode.
- Reduce Motion preserves the invitation and stable catch meaning without
  animated travel.

## Learning release and evaluation

The private site remains the release channel. Supporting evidence is that a
fresh tester can discover the leaf without opening the inspector; the breeze
visibly causes the toy; the dock contains one action rather than a gesture
manual; grabbing the leaf makes Moss track the hand without sliding; release
produces a stage-specific catch; and the world becomes quiet afterward.

Disconfirming evidence is a leaf that appears before reunion, an instruction
that persists after release, a hidden or too-small touch target, a breeze that
feels like an arbitrary tutorial trigger, Focus losing authority, repeated
opening loops, or any path that leaves Moss waiting for the person.

## Spec refinement

The existing wind-leaf art, physics, clips, and pointer target are sufficient.
Pure director sequencing is test-first. UI copy and hierarchy are verified in
the rendered phone path. No new generated character art is required.
