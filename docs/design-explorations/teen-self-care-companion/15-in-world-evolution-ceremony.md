# In-world Evolution Ceremony

## Frame

The three Leafling forms are now visibly different, but the transition between
them is still effectively an immediate replacement followed by the new form's
celebration. That proves multiple forms; it does not yet let someone witness
their companion growing up.

In user voice: when the care I have taken in my own life changes this creature,
I want to watch the little body I know become the more capable body it has
earned—without leaving our shared world.

This continues to serve `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`,
`jtbd-see-who-im-becoming`, and `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`. Evolution is
an emotional receipt for accumulated real-life follow-through. It is not a
level-up economy, a collectible reveal, or pressure to preserve a streak.

## Alternatives

### Instant replacement with particles

Keep the current form swap and disguise it with a burst. This is inexpensive,
but the effect celebrates an implementation change rather than showing growth.

### Detached cutscene

Leave the habitat for a full-screen transformation sequence. It could become
beautiful, but it interrupts the feeling that this creature inhabits one
persistent, playable place.

### Two-form ceremony in the habitat

Keep the camera, weather, ground, and shelter exactly where they are. The old
form recognizes the change; leaf-light gathers around it; old and new bodies
briefly overlap in a controlled handoff; the new form grows into its natural
scale and lands in the same patch of earth.

## Convergence

Choose the **two-form ceremony in the habitat**.

- Baby visibly becomes Young; Young visibly becomes Guardian.
- The old body owns recognition and gathering rather than vanishing at the
  first frame.
- The new body enters during the handoff, expands only to its authored size,
  and owns the proud landing and recovery.
- Deterministic leaf-light motes contract inward, then open around the arrived
  form. They are part of the habitat, not currency or a reward shower.
- Autonomous wildlife waits until the ceremony is finished so the emotional
  shot cannot be interrupted.
- The inspector's Evolve control replays the same previous-to-current form
  transition. Baby, which has no prior form, keeps its existing acting study.
- Reduce Motion replaces orbit and scale with a short, stable crossfade.

There is no rarity, item, unlock banner, inventory, loss state, or “level up”
copy. The ordinary growth receipt remains enough.

## Portable engine contract

- Product state still owns the current stage and the care threshold.
- The renderer derives the previous form only while the requested motion is
  `evolve`; no second progression model is introduced.
- A renderer-neutral composition function maps normalized clip progress to
  phase, old/new opacity, new-form settling offset, scale, and flourish
  intensity.
- The scene draws the habitat once and may composite two independent stage
  manifests at the same grounded world coordinate.
- Both the Canvas prototype and a future native or desktop adapter can consume
  the same form, clip, frame, and composition contracts.

## Acceptance evidence

- Crossing the third care day visibly performs Baby to Young in the world.
- Crossing the eighth care day visibly performs Young to Guardian in the same
  world.
- Recognition, gathering, handoff, and arrival are legible at phone scale.
- The habitat does not flash, replace, or detach during transformation.
- No wildlife visitor can arrive during the ceremony.
- Reduce Motion preserves the meaning with a stable two-form dissolve.
- Both transitions can be deterministically replayed from the inspector.
