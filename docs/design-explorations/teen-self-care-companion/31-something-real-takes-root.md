## Divergence

### Make the receipt louder

Keep the engine unchanged and emphasize the care-ready card. This improves
comprehension but leaves the Pet visibly following whichever ambient system
wins the frame.

### Freeze the whole world

Pause weather, wildlife, and touch whenever an action completes. This protects
the vignette but turns the habitat into a cutscene and weakens the feeling of a
living place.

### Give meaningful action scene ownership

The new life echo may interrupt ambient direction and finish its notice,
approach, and hold before care appears. Weather can continue changing the
habitat visually, but a still-pending ambient Pet response yields to the life
echo. To-do opens a bloom, Focus leaves a stilllight, and Play brings paired
seedheads plus stage-appropriate wildlife.

## Convergence

Choose **meaningful action scene ownership**.

The capability sequence becomes:

1. complete To-do, Focus together, or Play together;
2. create one private source-class life echo and one care opportunity;
3. let that action own the Pet's visible attention until its bounded answer is
   complete;
4. show live scene language instead of immediately replacing it with generic
   care-ready copy;
5. withhold the care control for the few seconds Moss is still answering;
6. reveal the unchanged one-daily-care action when Moss is grounded and quiet;
7. allow ambient direction to earn fresh quiet before another episode.

An arriving weather layer may finish visually while the action vignette owns
Moss. Its pending instinctive response is cancelled for this arrival rather
than being queued as a second mandatory performance. Future autonomous weather
episodes still use the full arrive-notice-respond-settle grammar.

### Reductive decisions

- No reward modal, confetti, points, collected garden, or action history.
- No second receipt and no private To-do text in the world.
- No global world pause; atmosphere and scenery continue moving.
- No care button while its prerequisite vignette is visibly unfinished.
- No new animation row; compose existing notice, locomotion, care, visitor,
  camera, and life-echo vocabulary.

We're betting that **one protected causal vignette** will make a Kwilt action
feel as though it entered the anime world, not merely incremented Pet state. If
the scene still feels procedural, revisit source-specific staging and camera
holds before adding more effects.

## Learning release and evaluation

The private site remains the release channel. At phone scale, begin a sunny
morning and immediately complete a To-do. Verify that the bloom opens, Moss
notices, approaches, and holds beside it; the sunny Pet response never steals
the scene; the status language follows the world action; and care appears only
after the vignette settles. Repeat after Focus and Play, then confirm later
autonomous weather still produces its full behavior.

Disconfirming evidence is a generic care card hiding the scene, care that can
erase the answer, queued ambient performances, a frozen habitat, or a life echo
that is only discoverable in the inspector.

## Spec refinement

The pure world state must distinguish a weather layer that is still owed a Pet
response from one that has settled without debt. Weather response may begin
only from yielding actions with no Focus, visitor, hand, or wind-leaf ownership.
Planting a meaningful life echo explicitly clears pending ambient response so
its sequence cannot be stolen. A regression test must reproduce the half-
arrived-sun plus To-do timing before the fix. The capability derives a
`worldAnswering` gate from the pending receipt source and portable world action;
it adds no persisted phase or new product state.
