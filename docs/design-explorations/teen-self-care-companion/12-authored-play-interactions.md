# Authored Play Interactions

## Current gap

The world accepts a high tap, a nearby insect, and a swipe across Leafling, but the resulting performances are still mechanical compositions. Jump and pounce borrow the greeting drawings. Rollover rotates the sleep drawings as one rigid sprite. Those shortcuts prove input and world-state behavior, but they do not prove character acting.

## Interaction contract

Jump, pounce, and rollover become first-class clips for every evolution form.

- **Jump:** attention leads into anticipation, takeoff, a readable reaching apex, landing compression, and recovery. The artwork owns lift; the world owns only the destination and camera.
- **Pounce:** eyes and ears acquire the visitor, the body coils, launches toward screen-right, reaches, lands through the forepaws, and recovers with curiosity. The renderer mirrors the canonical row for screen-left.
- **Rollover:** Leafling lowers one shoulder, curls through its leafy silhouette, turns onto its back, completes the roll through the opposite side, then uncurls and settles. The artwork owns the body turn; the engine never spins the complete sprite.

Every row uses eight authored drawings with uneven limited-animation cadence. Baby is impulsive and compact, young is elastic and playful, and guardian is powerful but controlled. The same action should therefore mature rather than merely scale up.

## Engine boundary

The renderer-neutral manifest names all three clips and supplies their contact, timing, events, and atlas cells. World state supplies intent, horizontal destination, facing, action lifetime, and camera. Canvas remains an adapter. No renderer invents a bounce, tilt, or whole-body rotation to compensate for missing art.

## Acceptance evidence

- All three forms have distinct eight-drawing jump, pounce, and rollover rows.
- Jump contains airborne drawings and returns to planted contact.
- Pounce faces canonical screen-right, mirrors cleanly for leftward targets, and changes from airborne to planted contact.
- Rollover remains grounded and uses no whole-sprite rotation.
- Actual-size previews show no baseline pop, scale change, reversed cadence, missing anatomy, or detached effects.
- Direct high taps, insect pounces, swipes, Reduce Motion, weather, Focus, and ordinary travel continue to work.

## Stated bet

We are betting that authored physical acting will create more attachment than additional scenery because the user's touch will finally receive a creature-specific performance instead of a transformed fallback clip.
