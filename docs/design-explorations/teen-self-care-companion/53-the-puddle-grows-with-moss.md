# Study 53: The puddle grows with Moss

## Experiential gap

> The tiny world should feel like a beautifully directed anime that I can play
> inside, and Moss's evolution should change what is physically possible.

The engine already lets rain arrive, Moss seek shelter, sunlight return, and a
real after-rain puddle remain in the meadow. The interaction is continuous, but
every form currently answers the puddle with the same grounded pounce. That
makes the weather story feel more alive than the evolution story.

## Frame

For Charlie, this is the playful proof that evolution is not a larger sprite or
a stronger badge. It is a new relationship with the same remembered place.
For Olive, the puddle should remain immediately understandable: touch the bright
water and see what this version of Moss cannot resist doing.

This serves `jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools` while preserving the
prototype's shame-free boundary. The puddle creates no score, care credit,
currency, need, failure, or collectible.

## Alternatives considered

1. Add a new weather minigame with explicit controls and a score.
2. Give every form a larger version of the existing splash effect.
3. Let one puddle call forth a different authored performance at each stage.

Choose **one puddle, three physical relationships**. It extends the existing
causal rain-to-sun episode and reuses approved animation vocabulary instead of
adding a second game surface.

## Playable episode

1. Settled rain clears into sunlight rather than disappearing instantly.
2. One bounded puddle catches the new light; Moss notices and looks toward it.
3. The person touches the puddle, committing the scene through one forgiving
   terrain target.
4. Moss approaches on the ground before the form-specific performance begins.
5. Baby investigates with a close, cautious nose-and-paw performance and a
   small ripple.
6. Young springs into one compact pounce and throws a readable fan of water.
7. Guardian coils, vaults high, and returns through the authored aerial contact
   frame; the landing sends a wider spray and one finite meadow wake through
   the grass.
8. Every form recovers on the terrain. The puddle fades without creating an
   unfinished request.

## Engine contract

- Pure world state owns the stage profile: clip, contact time, visual end,
  recovery duration, splash spread, and whether the meadow receives a wake.
- The renderer consumes that profile; it does not infer maturity from sprite
  dimensions or special-case browser input.
- Water begins on the exact authored contact frame, never at takeoff.
- Guardian wake originates at the puddle coordinate and releases once.
- Facing and the chosen puddle coordinate remain immutable through contact.
- Reduce Motion preserves one grounded contact tableau with no aerial travel,
  droplets, or traveling wake.

## Stated bet

We're betting that replaying the same tiny weather consequence across Baby,
Young, and Guardian will make growth feel more emotionally legible than another
evolution label. If the Guardian reads as spectacle detached from the puddle,
we should reduce spray and strengthen the contact frame rather than add effects.

## Learning signal

The useful family-playtest question is not whether the three splashes are
different. It is whether someone naturally wants to switch forms and touch the
same puddle again to see how Moss has grown.
