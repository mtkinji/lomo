# World interaction refinement

## Request

Advance the portable Pet engine with pinch-to-close-up, small insects that recruit attention, a habitat whose fidelity matches Leafling, and Olive's request for rollover.

## Frame

The audience remains aspirational family organizers, represented by Maya and pressure-tested through Charlie. The hero job is `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`; the underserved steps are family participation (2/5) and continued use (3/5). Pet earns its place when a healthy action has a warm, private consequence that someone voluntarily returns to. It must not become a second habit system, a source of guilt, or another surface Maya administers.

This is an **extend the system** refinement: add a portable world, camera, and behavior-target layer beneath the existing Pet capability without changing the shame-free care contract.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Visible game controls.** Add zoom buttons, directional controls, meters, and event menus. This makes every behavior easy to test but turns the capability into a dashboard and shrinks the emotional space around the Pet.
2. **Ambient diorama.** Keep the Pet centered while scenery and insects pass behind it. This looks alive but gives the user little agency and does not prove locomotion or camera portability.
3. **Direct-manipulation living world.** Interpret taps, swipes, and pinches as world input. Let autonomous visitors use the same behavior system. Keep explicit triggers in the prototype inspector only.

## Convergence

Choose the direct-manipulation living world.

- The habitat is 480 logical pixels wide behind a 160-pixel camera.
- Low taps become world-space destinations; Leafling walks or runs and the camera follows.
- High taps become jumps toward the user's touch.
- Pinch changes transient camera zoom from 1x to 2.25x.
- A horizontal swipe beginning near Leafling triggers a grounded curl, roll, and uncurl.
- Fireflies move independently, recruit gaze, and can provoke a pounce when they fly low and close.
- Environmental fidelity comes from layered parallax, finer plants, terrain texture, light, and a shared palette rather than more consumer controls.
- Existing authored clips may be composed with code-driven movement to prove a behavior. Dedicated gait and rollover rows remain an art decision after the interaction proves its value.

## Bet

If Leafling can notice the same world the user touches, then zoom, insects, movement, and tricks will read as one coherent relationship rather than four features. The important learning is not whether every animation is final; it is whether direct manipulation makes someone want to spend another quiet minute with the Pet.

## Learning questions

- Do users discover low tap, high tap, swipe, and pinch without persistent instructions?
- Does the camera follow feel like roaming or like the background is sliding?
- Does close-up zoom increase affection without making touch targets confusing?
- Are insect visits delightful at an occasional cadence, and which reactions feel most animal-like?
- Does the composite rollover feel grounded enough to test, or does it immediately require bespoke frames?
- Is a dedicated walk/run gait the next highest-value art investment?
