# Pet Engine Study 44 — The Meadow Feels the Guardian

## Frame and system alignment

Study 43 lets the person choose a landing from the old tree. Yet the arrival is
still mostly a change in Moss's position. A powerful adult form should alter
the physical relationship with its habitat: not through combat spectacle or a
reward burst, but through believable weight carried into grass, air, and loose
leaves.

The target remains the provisional teen participant Charlie within
`audience-aspirational-family-organizers`. This study serves
`jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools`: the Guardian's maturity should
be felt in the world rather than described by a level. It also serves
`jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` by keeping the response finite, nonjudgmental,
and unrelated to points or care credit.

```yaml
serves: [jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools, jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life]
```

**Design challenge:** How might the same person-chosen landing reveal genuine
adult power while remaining a natural anime performance inside the meadow?

## Divergence

### Add a large impact flash

A screen shake, ring, and bright burst would communicate force quickly, but it
would import combat and arcade reward language into a relationship-centered
world.

### Give every stage a particle landing

Baby, Young, and Guardian could each create a scaled effect. This weakens the
meaning of maturity and turns every ordinary arrival into feedback noise.

### Let only the Guardian move the meadow

Young lands cleanly and quietly. When Guardian completes the same authored
return, contact releases the air already gathered by its body: nearby grass
bows first, a broken pressure line travels across the terrain, and a few loose
leaf motes rise and settle. The response originates at the exact landing the
person chose.

## Convergence

Choose **let only the Guardian move the meadow**.

The authored sentence is:

1. the person chooses a landing from the high canopy;
2. Guardian commits to the chosen coordinate and completes the descent;
3. the exact contact frame releases one finite meadow wake;
4. grass bends outward and sparse habitat matter follows;
5. the wake decays while Guardian settles, then the quiet world resumes;
6. Young completes the same interaction without adult-scale force;
7. Focus may immediately clear the wake and Reduce Motion preserves one static
   bowed-grass contact without traveling particles.

We're betting that **habitat response at the chosen coordinate** will make the
Guardian feel powerful without adding statistics, attacks, or spectacle.

## Learning release and evaluation

At phone scale, choose left and right Guardian landings and verify the wake
origin follows the actual destination. Verify the body reaches terrain before
the wake releases, the camera holds both Moss and the response, and no second
landing or greeting is invented. Repeat as Young and verify the landing remains
quiet. Test Focus during the wake and Reduce Motion at contact.

Disconfirming evidence is a magical aura, a wake centered on the tree instead
of the landing, environmental motion before contact, a repeated impact loop,
screen shake, or the same force at every stage.

## Spec refinement

Reuse the existing portable Guardian meadow-wake contract. Pure world state
must release it from tree-return contact at the committed `landingX`; Canvas
continues to render the same pressure, grass-bend, and leaf-mote presentation.
No new art, score, haptics, persistence, care credit, or production integration
is introduced.
