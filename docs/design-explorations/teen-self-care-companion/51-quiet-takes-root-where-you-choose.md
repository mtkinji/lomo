# Pet Engine Study 51 — Quiet Takes Root Where You Choose

## Frame and system alignment

> I should be able to focus with the creature, and playing with it should feel
> like entering a beautifully directed tiny world.

When Charlie chooses to Focus with Moss, they want to feel that both of them
are entering the same quiet moment—not that Moss has gone to sleep while a
timer runs below the picture.

The provisional teen participant Charlie remains within
`audience-aspirational-family-organizers`. This study serves the moment between
intention and action, while letting the completed session become visible
without a chart or streak.

```yaml
serves: [jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse, jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools]
```

Constraint posture: **Fit and deepen the portable world engine.** Keep the
existing Focus receipt, fifteen-second prototype clock, old tree, weather,
camera, curl animation, and still-light memory. Add one spatial choice; do not
create a breathing exercise, score, focus game, prompt modal, or second timer.

**Design challenge:** How might a person and Moss choose one shared quiet place
inside the living meadow, while preserving calm companionship and a bounded,
shame-free Focus receipt?

## Yes-and check

Broader expansion is intentionally skipped. The larger opportunity is for
different Kwilt actions to leave personally placed traces in the habitat. The
useful proof is one complete Focus scene whose beginning and memory share the
same coordinate.

## Divergence

### Keep the old tree automatic

Moss always walks to the shelter anchor and curls. This is safe and cinematic,
but the person remains a spectator and the timer begins before Moss arrives.

### Turn Focus into a breathing interaction

The person could hold and release the screen to pace Moss's breath. This creates
direct control, but risks turning quiet companionship into a compliance game.

### Let the person choose where quiet takes root

Starting Focus opens the whole meadow and Moss looks to the person. One terrain
touch chooses a bounded resting place. Moss turns, walks there, curls, and only
then starts the Focus clock. If the person simply watches, Moss chooses a safe
place after a generous pause. Rain keeps the chosen point under the old tree's
canopy. The completed still light grows from that exact shared place.

## Convergence

Choose **let the person choose where quiet takes root**.

The authored sentence is:

1. the person taps **Focus with Moss**;
2. transient play yields and Moss looks up instead of immediately disappearing;
3. the whole meadow remains visible while one quiet invitation asks for a place;
4. a terrain touch locks a world-space resting coordinate;
5. in rain, the coordinate stays within the old tree's shelter;
6. Moss turns and walks to the chosen place while the Focus clock remains full;
7. on arrival, the camera moves close, Moss curls, weather hushes, and the clock begins;
8. completion produces the existing privacy-minimized Focus receipt;
9. the still-light memory appears at the same chosen coordinate;
10. no input defaults calmly to the old tree rather than leaving an unfinished need.

We're betting that **choosing the shared place** will make Focus feel like
companionship inside the world instead of a timer attached to a sleeping pet.
If the choice feels like setup work or the resting point is hard to understand,
return to automatic shelter and improve the transition rather than adding more
controls.

## Capability delta and reductive decisions

Today, the user can start Focus, but Moss immediately heads to one fixed anchor
and the timer counts during travel. After this study, the user can author the
place, see Moss travel there, spend the complete duration together, and later
touch the resulting still light where that moment actually happened.

The Pet still cannot grade attention, detect distraction, extend a streak,
award currency, demand a breath cadence, or punish ending early. The dock owns
one contextual sentence; no modal, location picker, or map marker is added.

## Learning release and evaluation

The private mobile-first site remains the learning channel. At 390 × 844, start
Focus from both sides of the meadow in sun, breeze, and rain. Confirm the
invitation keeps the meadow wide; the first terrain touch commits one point;
Moss faces and walks forward; the countdown does not change before arrival;
the camera becomes intimate only after arrival; Moss is grounded in its curl;
and the completed still light grows at the chosen coordinate. In rain, verify
the resting point remains beneath the old tree. Repeat without touching to
prove the safe automatic choice.

Disconfirming evidence is a timer running during setup, a hidden or tiny touch
target, Moss sliding backward, the camera cropping the chosen place, rain Focus
in the open, a persistent location marker, an unfinished invitation, or a still
light appearing somewhere the session did not happen.

## Spec refinement

Pure world state owns a Focus phase, selected anchor, choice timeout, weather-
safe clamping, travel, clock boundary, and completion coordinate. Tests precede
implementation. Canvas only converts terrain touches and keyboard alternatives
into `chooseCompanionFocusPlace`; the existing renderer, walk and sleep clips,
atmosphere, soundscape, receipt path, and life-echo drawing remain authoritative.
