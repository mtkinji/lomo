# Pet Engine Study 39 — It Knows You Came Back

## Experiential gap

The world already reacts to touch, weather, Focus, play, life progress, and
Moss's changing physical reach. But entry still begins as a neutral engine
state. The first seconds therefore prove that Moss is animated without proving
the more important feeling: a small life noticed that *you* returned.

## Direction

Make recognition the capability's opening scene. Moss begins elsewhere in the
meadow, pauses before moving, turns toward the user's side of the world, crosses
a stage-shaped distance, and finishes with one grounded hello. The existing
world remains continuously playable; any touch interrupts the scene immediately.

- **Baby:** starts nearby and takes small walking steps. Courage is the growth
  signal, not speed.
- **Young:** recognizes the user and opens into a delighted run.
- **Guardian:** begins across the widest lane and crosses the meadow with calm,
  powerful speed before softening into the same familiar hello.

The difference between forms is expressed through distance, cadence, scale,
and presence. It does not require a reward, dialogue, affection score, or claim
that Moss was lonely while the user was away.

## Portable engine contract

1. `reunion-notice` owns a planted recognition hold before locomotion.
2. `reunion-approach` uses the approved walk for Baby and run for Young and
   Guardian, with increasing entrance distance and speed by stage.
3. `reunion-greet` lands exactly on the terrain and uses the existing authored
   greeting under an intimate camera.
4. Direct touch, Focus, weather, care, and other meaningful world commands may
   interrupt; the reunion never resurrects itself afterward.
5. A pending care consequence waits until recognition finishes instead of
   covering the opening scene.
6. Reduce Motion resolves travel immediately but preserves the final recognized
   hello.
7. No absence duration, streak, need, guilt, or relationship meter is stored.

## Bet

An authored five-second reunion will create more attachment than another idle
variant because it changes Moss from a character that happens to be on-screen
into a character that visibly distinguishes the user's arrival from an empty
meadow.
