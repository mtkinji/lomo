# Pet Engine Study 18 — Cinematic Scene Language

## The leap

Moss already acts, travels, remembers, rests, and reacts to a living habitat.
The missing anime quality is not more activity. It is direction: the same world
needs to know when to stay wide, when to follow movement, and when to come close
enough for a held expression to matter.

Study 18 gives the renderer-neutral world a small shot vocabulary:

- **establishing** keeps the full meadow readable during quiet;
- **follow** gives travel a slight forward-moving composition;
- **reaction** pushes in for recognition, weather notice, and attention;
- **intimate** holds close for a remembered bloom, bask, shelter, or voluntary rest;
- **focus** settles into a steady shared-stillness composition;
- **action-wide** protects the complete silhouette and target during jumps and pounces;
- **user** preserves a person's pinch composition before gently returning direction to the world.

The camera never cuts to a separate scene or adds cinematic bars. It eases inside
the persistent habitat, keeps the ground anchor stable, and frames Moss with a
visitor during pursuit. The visual result should feel authored without making
the person watch a cutscene.

## Control contract

Direct manipulation has priority. Every pinch owns camera scale for six seconds
after the last change. The scene director may continue behavior underneath, but
it cannot change the user's chosen zoom during that interval. When ownership
expires, the camera eases toward the current scene rather than snapping.

Reduce Motion removes automatic push-ins and holds one stable wide composition.
User pinch remains available because it is an explicit view choice rather than
decorative motion.

## Evaluation

Observe one uninterrupted living-day cycle, then trigger a To-do bloom, Focus,
a visitor, a high tap, and pinch zoom.

The study succeeds when:

- quiet reads as a place, not an empty pause;
- recognition and rest become emotionally legible at phone scale;
- action never crops Moss or the pursued visitor;
- automatic framing never makes travel reverse on screen;
- pinch remains visually unchanged during its ownership window;
- the return from a user close-up is gradual;
- the camera adds rhythm without becoming noticeable machinery.

This is still browser-prototype evidence. It does not prove a native iPhone,
web, or desktop adapter until each renderer consumes the same shot contract.
