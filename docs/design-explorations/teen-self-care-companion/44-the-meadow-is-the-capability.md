# Pet Engine Study 40 — The Meadow Is the Capability

## Experiential gap

Moss now recognizes the person, lives through weather, focuses beneath the old
tree, plays through touch, and carries the visible consequences of meaningful
Kwilt activity. But the resting capability still ends in three equal buttons:
Complete a To-do, Focus together, and Play together. That makes the meadow read
as a reward panel for other features instead of the place where Moss lives.

The system boundary is also slightly false. A To-do is completed in To-do, and
a family or friend game is played in Games. Those events should arrive in the
Pet capability as consequences. Focus with Moss is the one healthy action that
belongs here because the shared stillness happens inside this world.

## Direction

Let the resting capability be mostly world:

1. direct touch remains the primary invitation to play;
2. one quiet `Focus with Moss` action remains available beneath the meadow;
3. To-do and Play receipt simulators move into the lab inspector, outside the
   user-facing capability;
4. the current life stage moves into the light identity HUD beside weather;
5. resting copy teaches only the essential gesture: touch the meadow and Moss
   will answer;
6. when Focus, care, evolution, evening, morning, or a Kwilt consequence is
   active, the existing contextual dock returns and explains that one moment;
7. no capability menu, quest list, currency, progress dashboard, or cross-app
   launcher is added.

## Capability delta

Before this study, three persistent controls compete with the living world and
imply that every healthy behavior begins inside Pet. After it, ordinary Pet time
is direct play plus one native shared-Focus invitation. To-do and family/friend
Play still change Moss's world, but only as receipts from the capabilities that
own those actions.

## Bet

We're betting that removing two false actions and turning the remaining Focus
entry into a quiet invitation will make the same engine feel more like a place
someone inhabits and less like a feature dashboard. If testers cannot discover
direct play or understand how outside progress reaches Moss, improve the
world's invitation and the consequence vignette rather than restoring a row of
buttons.

## Learning release

The private phone prototype should open on uninterrupted Moss-and-meadow
attention, followed by a restrained resting dock with `Focus with Moss` and one
touch invitation. The lab inspector must still trigger To-do and Play receipts
for deterministic testing. Verify that all receipt, care, evening, evolution,
and next-morning states retain their existing contextual UI and that no
meaningful-action path becomes unreachable.
