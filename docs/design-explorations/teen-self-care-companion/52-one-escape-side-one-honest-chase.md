# Pet Engine Study 52 — One Escape Side, One Honest Chase

## Frame and system alignment

> When the pet is jumping to chase a flying insect, it will often jump
> backwards. It should turn to face it and realistically pounce. The baby
> could notice something crawling, the young a bug, and the Guardian something
> flying higher.

This is a refinement of Study 46, not a second wildlife system. Charlie should
feel Moss becoming capable of reaching more of the same meadow while every
chase remains physically trustworthy.

```yaml
serves: [jtbd-feel-arc-progress-without-tracking-tools, jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life]
```

Constraint posture: **Fit and repair the portable world engine.** Preserve one
visitor state machine, one stage boundary, and one authored animation
vocabulary. Do not add levels, capture, combat, rarity, rewards, or a bestiary.

## Root condition

The engine sampled an intercept from the visitor's incoming crossing direction,
then changed the visitor to flee outward when Moss committed. Facing and world
travel technically agreed, but the landing was left behind relative to the
visitor's new escape. That contradiction shortened the leap and could make the
authored motion read backward.

## Chosen correction

1. The visitor's latest visible side chooses the outward escape direction.
2. Moss plants and turns toward that side.
3. The landing is predicted from the same outward escape through the species'
   visible contact window.
4. Facing, visitor flight, camera action line, horizontal travel, and landing
   remain immutable through the authored performance.
5. Baby keeps a crawler on the terrain, Young gets a low firefly and a modest
   spring, and Guardian gets a higher sky moth, longer intercept, and deeper
   aerial lift.

The existing inspector remains the deterministic proof: select each form and
invite its visitor from both sides. A valid release shows a readable turn before
translation, no backward launch, progressively higher targets and arcs, and one
grounded recovery.
