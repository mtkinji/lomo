# Yes-And: Meals, Recipes, and Groceries

Original idea: preserve family and found recipes in an ad-free collection, let
the household choose the next meals together on the cadence that fits them, and
turn the finalized plan into pickup or delivery groceries.

## Adjacencies

**Yes, and what if it could remember what the household actually repeats?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: planning starts from lived favorites rather than an empty
  calendar.
- New value: “regulars,” “haven't had lately,” and family-specific notes reduce
  search without turning taste into a score.
- Cost delta: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if suggestions remain optional and explainable.

**Yes, and what if it could preserve why a family recipe matters?**

- Serves: `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`
- Job elevation: a useful instruction also carries attribution, story, and
  continuity.
- New value: “From Grandma Ruth” survives copying and adaptation.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if meaning is invited, never required for capture.

**Yes, and what if the planning horizon followed the next shop instead of the calendar?**

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- Job elevation: the household commits to a small set of meals without making
  Tuesday's dinner another overdue task.
- New value: **Next meals** can mean three dinners, through Monday, two weeks,
  or an open set; leftovers, eating out, and day placement remain optional.
- Cost delta: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; avoids rigid productivity framing.

**Yes, and what if every family member could privately say what sounds good?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: meal planning becomes a bounded shared decision instead of one
  organizer guessing, chasing answers, or absorbing complaints.
- New value: selected household members pick up to a few candidates, pass, or
  suggest one idea from their own device; the organizer sees a calm aggregate
  and finalizes the plan.
- Cost delta: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass with plan-specific invitations, private responses,
  no winner/loser language, and organizer authority over safety/cost/schedule.

**Yes, and what if To-dos carried the moments of action without owning the plan?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: participation reminders, scheduled cooking, and grocery trips
  appear where the family already acts, while Meal Planning preserves the
  collaborative decision record.
- New value: the plan can project an optional Activity and receive its receipt
  without becoming a checklist-shaped substitute for planning.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if deleting/completing the Activity never silently
  finalizes or destroys the plan.

**Yes, and what if list arithmetic were reviewable rather than magical?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: the user sees why Kwilt combined “2 onions” and “1 onion,” and
  can split or correct it.
- New value: ingredient normalization saves work without hiding uncertainty.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass with provenance and undo.

**Yes, and what if the plan asked only what is already at home?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: avoid buying obvious pantry staples without requiring a
  high-maintenance inventory system.
- New value: a fast “Already have” review replaces a full pantry database.
- Cost delta: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; do not create inventory admin before demand.

**Yes, and what if every retailer handoff told the truth about its last mile?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: users know whether Kwilt created a list, proposed product
  matches, added items to a cart, or merely opened a store.
- New value: fewer failed expectations and a reusable integration contract.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no false completion receipts.

**Yes, and what if Kwilt made couponing disappear into the outcome?**

- Serves: `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending`
- Job elevation: the household asks “what is the best worthwhile total?” rather
  than learning offer taxonomies and remembering to clip things.
- New value: Kwilt compares unit prices, public promotions, eligible offers,
  fees, and likely waste; it activates offers only through authorized write
  APIs and otherwise gives one exact retailer action.
- Cost delta: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only if Kwilt ranks net household value rather than
  coupon count, preserves preferences, and proves **applied**/**saved** states.

**Yes, and what if the receipt taught Kwilt what actually worked?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: estimates become a truthful learning loop instead of marketing
  claims.
- New value: itemized receipt evidence reconciles expected discounts, fees, and
  actual total while improving future product preferences.
- Cost delta: medium-high
- Anti-pattern check: pass with explicit permission, private item data, and no
  claim that a bank transaction alone proves coupon use.

**Yes, and what if sharing earned its complexity?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: send one independent recipe copy first; form a live family
  collection only after repeated collaboration appears.
- New value: Grandma can contribute without joining the household's operational
  data or paying for a full seat.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no default-public family feed.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with the expanded frame:** one household feeding
loop with three truthful capability owners—Recipes for knowledge, Meal Planning
for flexible-horizon family choice, and Groceries for execution, Savings
Autopilot, and retailer handoff. Activities are optional projections. The first
savings surface should be promotion-aware and receipt-verified; automatic
coupon activation remains the authorized north star.
