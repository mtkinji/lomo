# Expansion

Original idea: Home provides useful setup guidance until a person's feed becomes useful.

| Adjacency | Job elevation / new value | Serves | Cost delta | Anti-pattern check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start from what brought you here | A chosen outcome makes the next step relevant; someone using Meals need not connect a bank | carry-intentions-into-action | Medium | Optional choice, reuse known explicit intent |
| Get a solo benefit before family joins | Value survives the social cold start; plan a meal or capture a commitment now | carry-intentions-into-action | Low-medium | No invite gate |
| Make invitations a resumable handoff | Show sent / accepted / next action without making the inviter responsible for someone else's delay | invite-the-right-people-in | Medium | No repeated solicitation or automatic sending |
| Show progress toward selected outcomes | Make finite setup legible without requiring every Kwilt capability | trust-this-app-with-my-life | Medium | Progress describes factual setup, not personal worth |
| Turn setup into a first real payoff | Follow connection with a usable budget answer, or family setup with shared participation | carry-intentions-into-action | Medium-high | No fabricated readiness or private financial feed post |
| Let setup and life coexist | First content gets space immediately; incomplete setup remains resumable | invite-the-right-people-in | Medium | No feed unlock condition |
| Support a new need months later | The same Home region can help add another child or start Meals when chosen | trust-this-app-with-my-life | Medium | No perpetual new-feature checklist |

IDs in the table use the jtbd- prefix from the taxonomy.

Frame recommendation: expand to Home as the place that helps Kwilt become useful, then gives that space to ordinary shared life. Setup tracking is one expression of this job. No new taxonomy node is necessary at this stage.
