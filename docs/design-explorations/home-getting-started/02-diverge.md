# Initial three directions for review

Historical first-pass sketches. The broader onboarding audit narrows and refines these in [03-converge.md](03-converge.md); the accepted value-door reel remains the orientation owner. Do not implement this initial lifecycle in isolation.

Status: proposals, not accepted behavior. Variation axis: user-directed overview, guided next action, conversational planning.

## A. Setup overview
A finite checklist occupies empty Home, with progress across user-selected capabilities and links into their setup. Best for people who want to see the whole path. Fails when everything looks required or a large denominator creates work before benefit. Lowest extension cost; still needs trustworthy capability-owned completion evidence.

## B. One useful next step
Home foregrounds one concrete action based on explicit intent and actual readiness. A secondary setup overview provides the whole path. As moments arrive, they appear immediately and the setup region becomes compact. Best for interruptible family adoption. Fails when ranking is unexplained, changes unpredictably or keeps promoting features. Requires a small deterministic eligibility and resume policy.

## C. Make a start through conversation
A short optional conversation asks what would help, then leads into real capability actions. Best when the person is unsure how Kwilt can help. Fails when another conversation delays a clear intent or invents operational readiness. Reuses Chat but requires stronger action handoff and resumption contracts.

All three preserve capture-first, do not create compulsory Arcs/Goals/Activities for app configuration, do not auto-publish, and keep setup outside feed records. Existing capabilities continue to own records and mutations. No identity scores, streaks, public setup activity, KPI grids or required commitment to dismiss.

## Recommended direction for discussion
B, with A available on demand. Use C only as optional help. This is a recommendation awaiting product review, not authorization to build.

Before: a new user sees a prompt to share despite potentially having no recipients or reason to share.
After: they can choose a purpose, take one real step, leave, and resume the correct next step; meanwhile actual posts remain visible immediately.

Proposed lifecycle:
- No intent: a short optional choice of desired benefit.
- Intent, actionable setup: one next action plus progress detail.
- Waiting: truthful pending state and a useful independent action.
- First benefit and emerging posts: compact resumable setup above the feed.
- Established or explicitly dismissed: feed leads; setup remains available from overflow.
- Newly chosen capability: temporarily resume guidance for that capability.

Progress: prefer '2 of 4 steps' for a named chosen path. If percentage is used, name its denominator. Distinguish not-started, in-progress, waiting, ready and skipped. Skipped is not completed. A disconnect is maintenance, not a loss of earned onboarding progress. New features never silently reduce completion.

Suggested first learning slice: organic Home entry, chosen purpose, one resumable path through Household plus one solo capability, actual readiness and pending states, graceful feed coexistence. Broader Screen Time promotion follows verified supported device/role flows. Use local build, then TestFlight after review.

Bet: people will return because they experienced a useful outcome and can see the next step. Measure first payoff and later independent use, separately from setup taps, connection rates or checklist completion. Disconfirmation: repeated setup dismissal, missed real posts, confusion about readiness, or many connections without later use.

Before implementation: settle default landing, initial paths, readiness ownership, role/device/entitlement eligibility, skip/resume behavior, and transition rules. No production behavior changed in this exploration.
