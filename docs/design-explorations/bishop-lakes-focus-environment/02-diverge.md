# Diverge

## A. Static alpine poster

Smallest delivery, but loses the subtle water movement that makes the recorded place feel inhabited.

## B. Recorded video with an existing soundscape

Keeps motion but breaks the truth of place and the existing coordinated-environment model.

## C. Recorded video with its recorded ambience

Preserves the real place, keeps picture and sound coherent, and fits the existing Focus environment contract. This is the selected direction.
