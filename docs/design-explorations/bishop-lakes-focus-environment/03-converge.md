# Converge

Choose the real Bishop Lakes recording with its original stereo ambience. Deliver a content-addressed 1280×720 H.264 video from the existing Focus CDN, a bundled local poster, and a bundled 48 kHz stereo audio track decoded by the native seamless-loop engine.

Trade-off: the 103-second source reduces repetition, but its picture and sound seam still require repeated-loop review on a physical iPhone. The implementation may ship to local testing before that perceptual gate is satisfied; it must not claim the seam has been accepted.
