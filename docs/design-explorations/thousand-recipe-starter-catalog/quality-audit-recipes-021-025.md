# Quality audit: recipes 021–025

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

The five-recipe South Asian production slice passes the schema, uniqueness, type, ingredient-allocation, and editorial reviews. It is suitable to continue, with one substantive correction made before the next batch.

## Correction

BR021 originally treated “prepared vegetable sambar” as an ingredient even though sambar is named in the recipe title. That failed the standalone-executability standard. The recipe now includes toor dal, vegetables, sambar powder, tamarind, and a mustard-curry-leaf tempering with complete directions.

## Signals preserved

- Dosa and idli use honest warm-kitchen fermentation endpoints rather than misleading fixed prep promises.
- Poha uses a tactile hydration test because flake thickness varies.
- Upma controls lumps through dry-roasting, boiling liquid, and gradual addition.
- Aloo paratha controls seam failure through cool, dry, lump-free filling and equal portions.

## Scaling rule added

If a substantial preparation appears in a recipe title, the recipe must either author it completely or reference a separately validated canonical recipe through an explicit catalog link. A vague “prepared” ingredient is not sufficient.
