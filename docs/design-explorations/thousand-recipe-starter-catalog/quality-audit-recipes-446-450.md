# Quality audit: recipes 446–450

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

SI006–SI010 pass source comparison, roster continuity, original-body checks,
ingredient allocation, cultural-identity review, safety review, familiar-title
review, and the strengthened familiarity/effort gate. The staged corpus now
contains 450 of the approved 500 recipes; the live catalog remains untouched.

The batch contains five household anchors and no discovery recipes. The full mix
is now 190 household anchors, 225 cuisine anchors, and 35 discovery recipes.

## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- All five are recognizable vegetable, steakhouse, Southern, or holiday sides.
  None is retained for geographic coverage alone.
- Brussels sprouts, carrots, and creamed spinach take 30 to 40 minutes with an
  ordinary pan or pot and no specialty ingredient.
- Collard greens take about two hours but require one pot and little supervision;
  long braising is not misclassified as high active complexity.
- Cornbread dressing is the batch's one true project. Its scratch cornbread,
  drying interval, aromatic skillet, stock judgment, bake, and rest are fully
  disclosed.

## Stories and naming

- Brussels sprouts explain why modern roasting changed an often-disliked boiled
  vegetable without resorting to a restaurant-style sugar-and-bacon pileup.
- Glazed carrots explain the one-pan reduction that defines a glaze.
- Creamed spinach uses the instantly legible `Steakhouse` name and explains why
  frozen spinach is well suited, not inferior.
- Collard greens name smoked turkey up front and tell the Southern and African
  American potlikker story with care.
- Cornbread dressing explains the regional dressing-versus-stuffing distinction
  while leading with the familiar Southern name.

## Safety and execution checks

- Brussels sprouts and carrots require dry produce, pan space, and continuous
  attention when honey is reducing.
- Creamed spinach reaches 165°F and keeps dairy below a hard boil.
- Collards are repeatedly washed, smoked-turkey salinity is checked late, meat is
  deboned, and leftovers have a two-hour cooling boundary.
- Dressing is baked outside the bird to 165°F; mandating a stock range accounts
  for bread dryness without producing soup.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Brussels sprouts combine more than 45 five-star reviews with technique and AP
  sources. Dry halves, high heat, space, cut-side contact, tender centers, and an
  acidic finish recur.
- Glazed carrots combine a method comparison and two editorial authorities.
  Covered tenderizing, honey, butter, orange, uncovered reduction, and only a
  little remaining syrup recur.
- Creamed spinach combines popular steakhouse-copycat, reader, and widely viewed
  one-pot patterns. Dry spinach, buttered aromatics, roux-thickened dairy, nutmeg,
  and modest cheese recur.
- Collards combine Black Southern, regional, and chef-informed reporting.
  Meticulous washing, smoked meat, onion, broth, long tenderness, potlikker,
  chile, and vinegar recur.
- Cornbread dressing combines a four-recipe comparison with two Southern-style
  household patterns. Dry savory cornbread, some white bread, celery, onion,
  sage, stock by feel, egg, and separate baking survive.
