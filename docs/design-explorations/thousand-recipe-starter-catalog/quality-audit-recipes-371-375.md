## Roster and naming correction

- Every dish now leads with a recognizable description and retains its
  traditional name in parentheses.
- İskender kebab moved from household anchor to discovery after Turkish sources
  confirmed that real İskender depends on döner and that credible home methods
  require stacked, partially frozen, thinly shaved meat.
- No easier beef-chunk recipe is mislabeled as döner. The recipe openly states
  that its freezer-and-skillet technique cannot duplicate a vertical spit.
- Hünkar beğendi remains a weekend cuisine anchor rather than discovery because
  its time is primarily a straightforward lamb braise; the active work uses
  ordinary pots, a broiler, and a saucepan.
- Fatteh and stuffed peppers are the strongest low-complexity counterweights.

## Familiarity and effort check

- Syrian chickpea-yogurt pita bowls take about 35 minutes and use canned
  chickpeas, pita, yogurt, tahini, and ordinary cookware.
- Turkish tomato-stuffed eggplant uses a simple roast-and-braise sequence. It
  takes time for onions and eggplant to soften but requires no specialist
  equipment or fragile finish.
- Turkish beef-and-rice stuffed peppers are recognizable household comfort food
  using a one-pot simmer and supermarket ingredients.
- Hünkar beğendi is a longer weekend meal: lamb braises while eggplants char and
  drain. Its two components are explicit, and it cannot enter quick shelves.
- İskender is the sole discovery project, with an overnight meat stack,
  controlled shaving, batch searing, sauce, bread, yogurt, browned butter, and
  grilled vegetables.

## Safety and execution checks

- Fatteh pine nuts and butter are watched closely; yogurt remains separate from
  direct high heat and the bowls are assembled immediately.
- İskender beef remains refrigerated until stacking, freezes promptly, is
  shaved with a stable sharp-knife setup, and reaches 160°F in uncrowded
  batches. Raw marinade is not reused.
- İmam bayıldı eggplants are salted and dried before oil, then braised until
  fully tender; no raw animal ingredients are present.
- Hünkar beğendi lamb shoulder reaches fork tenderness after a long simmer;
  whole charred eggplants are covered, peeled, drained, and folded into a
  gently heated béchamel.
- Stuffed-pepper filling reaches 165°F, raw rice receives expansion room, and
  the peppers rest before service.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Fatteh lacks reliable high-volume star data, so a Syrian PBS cook, a detailed
  editorial method, and a Syrian contributor cookbook establish the repeated
  warm chickpea, crisp pita, garlic-yogurt-tahini, and hot nut-butter pattern.
- İskender's sparse 5/5 reader data is weaker than its failure evidence. Turkish
  editorial and in-country sources agree on Bursa origins, döner, pide, tomato,
  yogurt, butter, and grilled vegetables; reviewers explicitly reject ordinary
  meat as equivalent to spit-cooked döner.
- İmam bayıldı combines Turkish government authority with 24 community reviews
  that praise the flavor while warning about oil absorption, inaccurate prep
  time, and under-scaled filling.
- Hünkar beğendi relies on Turkish government guidance and two Turkish cuisine
  specialists rather than artificial rating confidence. All require braised
  lamb over smoke-charred eggplant béchamel.
- Stuffed peppers follow the Turkish culture ministry's hot meat-filled
  main-dish branch, corroborated by its official meatless branch and a Turkish
  cultural explainer. Expansion room, tomato caps, upright simmering, herbs,
  and yogurt recur.
