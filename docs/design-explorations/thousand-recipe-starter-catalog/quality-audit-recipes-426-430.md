## Safety and execution checks

- Both chicken recipes reach 165°F and rest before slicing. Ground beef cooks
  until browned with no pink remaining.
- Ahi must be previously frozen, sold specifically for raw consumption, kept at
  40°F or below, cut on a dedicated clean surface, served promptly, and
  discarded after two hours above refrigeration. The story states that cooking
  is safest and high-risk people should choose cooked seafood or tofu.
- Warm rice cools before contact with raw fish; hot fajita filling and taco meat
  meet cold greens only in individual bowls.
- Quinoa is rinsed and rested; corn and peppers are charred at high heat;
  avocado and crunchy toppings enter only at service.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

  and food reporting about American taco night. Review criticism directly

- The quinoa bowl follows three meal-prep and restaurant-demand sources.
  Rinsed quinoa, black beans, corn, peppers, avocado, cumin, lime, cilantro, and
  crunchy seeds form the repeated success pattern.
