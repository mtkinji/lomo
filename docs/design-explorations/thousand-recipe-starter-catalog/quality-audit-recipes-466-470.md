## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- `Crusty baguettes` is recognizable on sight and uses four core ingredients, an
  overnight cold rise, a regular sheet pan, and 30 minutes of active work.
- `French country bread` leads before `Pain de campagne` and deliberately uses
  commercial yeast so starter ownership is not a hidden prerequisite.
- `Herbed Provençal flatbread` leads before `Fougasse`; its leaf shaping uses a
  bench scraper or pizza wheel and adds crisp edges rather than decorative burden.
- Brioche openly requires a stand mixer and careful butter incorporation. It is
  advanced, but it avoids lamination and fits a one-day schedule.
- Croissants are the single true project: two-day scheduling, butter-temperature
  matching, three folds, precision rolling, shaping, cool proofing, and deep baking
  are all disclosed.

## Stories and naming

- Baguettes are presented as familiar French daily bread whose character comes
  from fermentation, shape, score, and steam rather than an exotic ingredient list.
- Pain de campagne is described as a broad country-bread family, not one immutable
  sourdough formula.
- Croissants are situated within viennoiserie and their honeycomb structure is
  explained without turning the catalog title into a lesson.
- Brioche explains enrichment and the pull-apart Nanterre-style loaf on the detail
  page while retaining a plain-English title.
- Fougasse's Provençal name and leaf story remain available in `About this meal`,
  while the list first says that it is a herbed flatbread.

## Safety and execution checks

- Baguette steam instructions keep face and hands clear and remove the pan only
  when safe.
- Country bread uses parchment handles, oven mitts, and an explicit warning for
  the hot Dutch oven and escaping steam.
- All five recipes include internal-temperature or strong visual doneness cues;
  croissants additionally warn that pale pastry is usually underbaked inside.
- Croissant proofing stays below 78°F to avoid melted laminating butter, and brioche
  uses a 190°F endpoint to protect its tender crumb.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to dough, pan preparation, finishing, steam,
  or service.
