## Identity checks

- Aloo gobi is a dry North Indian home-style sabzi with staggered potato and cauliflower cooking, not a restaurant gravy.
- Baingan bharta preserves direct-fire smoke, collapsed eggplant, and Punjabi onion-tomato masala rather than becoming generic eggplant purée.
- Goan fish curry uses a fresh coconut and whole-spice masala with tamarind and gentle fish poaching, while acknowledging household and regional variation.
- Pork vindaloo preserves its Portuguese-Goan vinegar-and-garlic lineage, uses fatty pork shoulder, and explicitly rejects both potato and maximum heat as defining traits.
- Kerala chicken stew remains a pale, mild Syrian Christian ishtu: thin coconut milk cooks the chicken, thick coconut milk enters late, and fresh shallot-curry-leaf tempering finishes it.

## Safety and execution checks

- Aloo gobi gives potatoes a head start and adds acidic tomato late, preventing the false choice between hard potato and mushy cauliflower.
- Eggplants are slit and inspected before live-fire roasting; hot resting, peeling, and draining are sequenced explicitly.
- Fish is poached without hard boiling or stirring and reaches 145°F at its thickest point.
- Retained pork marinade is brought to a full boil and the pork passes 145°F before continuing to fork-tender braise completion.
- Bone-in chicken reaches 165°F at the bone; thick coconut milk is warmed without boiling.

## Highest-rated pattern synthesis

- Baingan bharta's 4.99/5 across 236 votes supports direct-flame roasting and concentrated masala; two additional Indian sources corroborate the defining pattern.
- Goan fish curry lacks a dependable rating-and-count pair across the selected sources, so the decision rests on three-way agreement about coconut, whole spices, true souring, and brief gentle fish cookery.
- Pork vindaloo likewise prioritizes credible lineage and technique agreement over unverifiable rating widgets, preserving vinegar, garlic, whole spices, overnight marination, and slow braising.
- Kerala chicken stew's smaller 5/5 signal is treated as directional only and balanced with Kerala-published cultural and technique sources.
