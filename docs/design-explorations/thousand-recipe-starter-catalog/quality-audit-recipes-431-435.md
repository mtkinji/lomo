## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- All five are recognizable party and game-day favorites. None is retained for
  category or regional coverage alone.
- Buffalo wings use a rack and two oven temperatures instead of a pot of frying
  oil. The 70-minute total is stated honestly and most of it is hands-off.
- Deviled eggs use a saucepan, steamer basket, and one filling bowl. Steaming
  and sieving solve peeling and texture problems rather than adding novelty.
- Spinach-artichoke dip is one bowl and one baking dish; rigorous vegetable
  drying prevents a watery result.
- Jalapeño poppers use one cheese filling and one crumb topping, avoiding bacon
  wrapping, a three-bowl breading station, and frying.
- Maryland crab cakes are the batch's one premium ingredient, but the method is
  one bowl, a disclosed 30-minute chill, and broiling without flipping—not a
  hidden restaurant project.

## Stories and naming

- Buffalo wings explain the Buffalo, New York origin and define the sauce as
  cayenne hot sauce plus butter, not generic barbecue.
- Deviled eggs acknowledge older stuffed-egg precedents while explaining the
  American mayonnaise-and-mustard potluck form and the historical meaning of
  `deviled`.
- Spinach-artichoke dip is truthfully framed as a late-twentieth-century
  American restaurant and party-food classic.
- Jalapeño poppers explain both fried and bacon-wrapped branches while choosing
  the strong baked branch for household effort.
- Maryland crab cakes lead with the familiar regional name, explain blue crab
  and minimal filler, and warn that `Maryland-style` does not prove harvest
  origin.

## Safety and execution checks

- Wings are dried, raised on a rack, and cooked to at least 175°F near the bone.
  The recipe distinguishes baking powder from baking soda.
- Eggs steam 13 minutes, chill completely, stay refrigerated, and have an
  explicit two-hour service boundary.
- Spinach-artichoke dip reaches 165°F and is refrigerated within two hours.
- Jalapeño prep requires gloves, eye-contact avoidance, and surface cleanup;
  the story discloses natural heat variability.
- Pasteurized crab is checked for shell, handled gently, chilled after shaping,
  and broiled to 165°F.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Buffalo wings combine two oven-technique authorities with Associated Press
  food reporting. Thorough drying, baking powder, low-then-high heat, buttered
  cayenne sauce, blue cheese, celery, and immediate service recur.
- Deviled eggs combine 5/5 across 10 votes, a 4.2/5 Gourmet pattern, and a Bon
  Appétit texture method. Fully set eggs, mayonnaise, mustard, acid, smooth
  yolks, paprika, and chilled service survive.
- Spinach-artichoke dip uses a four-recipe comparison with two 10/10 winners,
  then checks it against restaurant and broad test-kitchen patterns. Dry
  vegetables, pronounced spinach, artichoke pieces, several cheeses, garlic,
  mild heat, and browned service recur.
- Jalapeño poppers combine 4.9/5 across 113 reviews and 4.9/5 across 23 reviews
  with a baked appetizer source. Cream cheese, cheddar, garlic, scallion, tender
  fresh pepper, and crisp topping survive; bacon and deep frying do not.
- Maryland crab cakes combine 5/5 across 24 reviews with Food Network testing
  and Maryland state seafood guidance. Lump crab, minimal binder, gentle
  folding, chilling, restrained Old Bay, and broiling define the result.
