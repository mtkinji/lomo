## Identity checks

- Stuffed peppers select the American beef-rice-tomato-cheese form while acknowledging a much broader global family.
- Tuna noodle casserole preserves the pantry classic but uses a controlled scratch mushroom sauce.
- Chicken-broccoli-rice casserole is explicitly distinct from Chicken Divan and corrects common salt and liquid failures.

## Safety and execution checks

- Stuffed-pepper beef reaches 160°F before filling; chicken reaches 165°F before casserole assembly.
- Broccoli and pepper shells are pre-cooked and drained to protect final hydration.
- Cooked turkey enters finished sauce off heat and receives only a short reheating bake.
- All casseroles rest before service so sauce and starch can stabilize.

## Highest-rated pattern synthesis

- Lamb shepherd’s pie has the strongest signal at 4.5/5 across roughly 6,100 ratings.
- Stuffed peppers carry 4/5 across 372 ratings, supporting the beef, tomato, grain, pepper, and cheese structure.
- Turkey tetrazzini’s visible 4.5/5 comes from only two ratings and is treated as weak evidence.

## Carry-forward rules

- Precook and drain watery produce before it enters a closed casserole system.
- Cooked leftover meat should enter a finished sauce late and spend minimal time in the oven.
- Nostalgic identity can survive a scratch sauce; condensed soup is a convenience method, not the only defining feature.
