# Quality audit: recipes 066–070

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

BR066–BR070 pass batch and global validation, unique-body checks, TypeScript, formatting, ingredient allocation, food-safety review, and cultural-identity review. One research record was strengthened after validation: BR068 now preserves the publisher's visible 4.93-star, 13-rating signal instead of discarding it.

## Identity checks

- Nordic oat porridge is hot-cooked and fluid, with berry compote kept separate for color and texture; it cannot collapse into the cold-soaked Bircher entry beside it.
- Swiss Birchermüesli is defined by soaked oats, freshly grated whole apple, lemon, cultured dairy, and nuts. It avoids becoming a generic overloaded overnight-oats jar.
- German Quarkpfannkuchen integrates quark into an aerated flour batter. The notes explicitly distinguish it from shaped syrniki and potato-based Quarkkäulchen.
- Austrian Kaiserschmarrn is one thick pancake that sets before being torn and then caramelized; it is materially different from ordinary griddled pancakes.
- Polish cheese naleśniki use a thin rested crêpe batter and a lightly textured twaróg filling, followed by a second butter-sear.

## Safety and execution checks

- The measured maple syrup, sugar, butter, apples, eggs, dairy, and toppings are all allocated in the instructions; no partial measurement is orphaned.
- BR070 uses a pasteurized yolk and requires the filled crêpes to reach 160°F.
- Thick quark batter and Kaiserschmarrn both use moderate heat and explicit center-set cues, preventing browned exteriors with raw centers.
- Milk porridge is simmered gently, rested, and loosened at service rather than cooked to a fixed-looking thickness that becomes gluey in the bowl.

## Highest-rated pattern synthesis

- The clearly visible rating evidence was retained only where the publisher showed both stars and count: King Arthur's Bircher muesli at 5.0 from 4 reviews and Dirndl Kitchen's quark pancakes at 4.93 from 13 votes.
- The successful patterns were technical rather than ornamental: a long oat soak, fresh apple, substantial quark, controlled lift, a fully set pancake before tearing, and a second fry for filled naleśniki.
- Sources without visible star-and-count evidence remain `null`; comments, saves, and editorial prominence were not misrepresented as ratings.

## Carry-forward rules

- When neighboring recipes share a visual family, compare batter hydration, primary dairy or starch, lift, cooking vessel, finishing method, and cultural role before approving both.
- Preserve rating evidence only as an auditable pair of score and count.
- A named filled or composed dish must fully specify its filling and final cook, including a safety endpoint when egg remains inside.
