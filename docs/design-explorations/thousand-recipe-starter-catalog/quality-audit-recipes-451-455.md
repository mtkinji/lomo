Date: 2026-08-06

## Result

BA001–BA005 pass source comparison, roster continuity, original-body checks,
ingredient allocation, cultural-identity review, safety review, familiar-title
review, and the strengthened familiarity/effort gate. The staged corpus now
contains 455 of the approved 500 recipes; the live catalog remains untouched.

The batch contains five household anchors and no discovery recipes. The full mix
is now 195 household anchors, 225 cuisine anchors, and 35 discovery recipes.

## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- All five are familiar home-baking favorites. None is retained for geographic
  breadth or baking prestige.
- Sourdough is the single project recipe. It explicitly requires a healthy
  established starter, several fold/check intervals, an overnight proof, a Dutch
  oven, and dough judgment; creating a starter is excluded as a separate job.
- Sandwich bread and dinner rolls use conventional kneaded dough, ordinary flour,
  and standard pans rather than tangzhong, milk powder, or a stand mixer.
- Biscuits and cornbread finish in under 50 minutes using ordinary equipment.

## Stories and naming

- Sandwich bread leads with its household function and explains the close crumb.
- `Boule` remains only after `Beginner sourdough`; the detail story translates it
  as a round loaf and strips away unnecessary jargon.
- Buttermilk biscuits explain the American quick-bread meaning and acknowledge
  Southern family variation without claiming one universal authentic method.
- Cornbread tells its Indigenous and regional American context and identifies the
  chosen savory Southern branch.
- Dinner rolls use the familiar `pull-apart` cue and explain why close baking makes
  the sides soft.

## Safety and execution checks

- Yeast liquids have explicit 100–110°F boundaries; loaf and roll centers reach
  190°F or above.
- Sourdough uses visual fermentation cues, safe hot-pot lowering on parchment, and
  a 208°F center target.
- Biscuit butter stays cold; cornbread and sourdough call out hot-pan handling.
- Every bread has a realistic cooling or service boundary.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Sandwich bread combines 4.6/5 across 678 reviews, 4.7/5 across 419 reviews, and
  beginner guidance. Milk, butter, honey, elastic dough, two rises, tight shaping,
  and complete cooling recur.
- Sourdough combines beginner and no-knead authority with an approachable home
  recipe. Ripe starter, folds, visual bulk cues, tension, overnight cold proof,
  Dutch-oven steam, and full cooling recur.
- Biscuits combine 4.8/5 across 179 ratings, 4.1/5 across 461 reviews, and comparative
  testing. Cold butter and dairy, brief mixing, folds, calibrated leavening, and a
  hot bake survive.
- Cornbread combines AP reporting, technique, and baking authority. Cornmeal-first
  batter, buttermilk, hot buttered cast iron, minimal mixing, and a roughly
  20-minute bake recur.
- Dinner rolls combine baking, test-kitchen, and household patterns. Enriched
  tacky dough, equal tight balls, close proofing, egg wash, and hot butter recur.
