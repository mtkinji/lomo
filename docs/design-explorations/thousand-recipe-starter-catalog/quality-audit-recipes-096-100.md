## Identity checks

- Chicken salad is cooked specifically for the croissant, fully cooled, and combined with celery, grapes, pecans, scallions, and tarragon in a restrained lemony dressing. A dry lettuce leaf and cooled toasted croissant protect the shell.
- Egg salad follows the clearest high-rated differentiator: mash yolks smoothly into the dressing, then fold in chopped whites. Celery and chives add freshness without burying the egg.
- The Reuben retains rye, hot corned beef, dry sauerkraut, Swiss, homemade Russian dressing, and slow griddling. The note acknowledges both its Jewish-American deli context and that meat with dairy is not kosher.
- The Philadelphia cheesesteak uses thin-shaved ribeye, optional-in-the-tradition griddled onions, accepted white American cheese, and a soft-crusted long steak roll. Bell peppers are deliberately not a default topping.
- The Louisiana shrimp po’boy uses 31–40-count shrimp, seasoned flour-cornmeal crust, light New Orleans-style French bread, and the fully dressed lettuce-tomato-pickle pattern.

## Safety and execution checks

- Chicken is roasted to 165°F, rested, cooled on a shallow plate, and mixed only when cold.
- Eggs are cooked by a covered off-heat method, ice-chilled promptly, and held cold after mixing.
- Cooked corned beef is reheated until steaming and the sauerkraut is squeezed before warming.
- Shrimp is marinated no longer than 20 minutes, fried in small batches at 340–350°F, and cooked to 145°F.
- All lettuce, tomato, pickles, and sauerkraut placed against bread are dried or blotted first.

## Highest-rated pattern synthesis

- Spend With Pennies’ egg salad is the decisive quantitative signal at 4.98 from 947 votes. Its separate-yolk technique is also the most specific explanation for the rating: it produces a creamy binder while leaving tender white pieces intact.
- The remaining recipes rely more on regional and editorial authority than unverifiable star widgets. Visit Philadelphia supplies the cheesesteak’s accepted ingredient boundaries; Louisiana sources and the Associated Press establish po’boy bread, shrimp size, and the meaning of “dressed.”
- Across all five, the recurring quality pattern is temperature and moisture control: cool creamy fillings, warm hot fillings, dry produce, and assembly only at the point each bread can best receive it.

## Carry-forward rules

- Treat exact regional bread as an ingredient, not incidental packaging.
- Prefer a traceable regional authority over a high rating when cultural identity is the question being answered.
- Record a rating only when both the score and current vote count are visible; the egg-salad count was refreshed from 946 to 947 during final review.
- List even small measured additions, such as cheese-steaming water, when the method depends on them.
