# Quality audit: recipes 091–095

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

LU001–LU005 pass batch and global catalog validation, category-transition continuity, unique-body checks, scoped formatting, ingredient allocation, food-safety review, and sandwich-architecture review after two manual corrections. LU001 and LU003 each listed twice the salt their methods used; both quantities now match the tomato-seasoning step exactly.

Repository-wide TypeScript remains independently red in unrelated uncommitted Recipe Library and Chat changes; this batch does not touch those files.

## Identity checks

- Tomato grilled cheese sandwiches salted and blot-dried fresh tomato between two cheese layers, preserving tomato flavor without a steamed center.
- The classic BLT follows the winning restrained pattern from a four-recipe comparison: ripe tomato, bacon, lettuce, toast, and one garlic-mayonnaise upgrade.
- The turkey club retains its defining three toast slices, two balanced tiers, picks, and four triangles. It is not relabeled ordinary turkey-bacon toast.
- The tuna melt uses drained oil-packed tuna, fresh crunch, capers, and cheese on both inner bread faces; tomato is omitted after comparison with wetter, lower-rated versions.
- The turkey-avocado wrap uses turkey as a moisture barrier, seeded cucumber, dry lettuce, and drained pickled onion. Tomato is intentionally absent for packed-lunch durability.

## Safety and execution checks

- Bacon is rack-baked until crisp and held away from wet produce until assembly.
- All tomato and lettuce used against toast is drained, blotted, or completely dried.
- Tuna is pressed in a sieve and tasted before salt is considered.
- Wrap tortillas are warmed for flexibility but cooled until no longer steaming before filling.

## Highest-rated pattern synthesis

- HelloFresh's tuna melt supplies the strongest volume at 4.5 from roughly 1,200 ratings; Bon Appétit's 4.5 from 30 adds more useful technique detail around briny crunch and cheese-first cooking.
- Food Network UK's tomato grilled cheese at 4.6 from 16 supports tomato concentration and full melting, while this version preserves fresh rather than roasted tomato.
- The BLT comparison is more informative than an isolated star score: its 10/10 winner succeeded through restraint, and the maximalist version failed by obscuring the tomato.

## Carry-forward rules

- Sandwich quality must be audited as moisture architecture, not only ingredient quality.
- Formal sandwich identities such as a club require their defining construction, even when a simpler sandwich would taste similar.
- Ingredient-allocation review should reduce an erroneous measurement rather than create an unnecessary use for the excess.
