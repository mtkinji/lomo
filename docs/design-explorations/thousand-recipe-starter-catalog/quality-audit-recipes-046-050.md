# Quality audit: recipes 046–050

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

BR046–BR050 pass structural validation, independent-body checks, TypeScript, formatting, ingredient allocation, named-component completeness, food-safety review, and technique review after one correction.

## Correction made

- BR048 now requires pasteurized filling eggs rather than mentioning pasteurization conditionally in the method. The recipe intentionally serves a soft yolk, so the safety control belongs in the ingredient contract.

## Technique checks

- Roti canai uses a long fat-coated rest, translucent stretching, folding, repeated griddle turns, and clapping to separate layers; the named dhal is fully authored.
- Quiche Lorraine blind-bakes its shell, drains the bacon, and removes the low-temperature custard while its center still trembles.
- Savory crêpes rest their mixed-flour batter and use a thin pan layer with a covered, softly set filling egg.
- Apple pain perdu drains stale brioche after soaking and cooks apples separately; alcohol is added only with the pan off heat.
- The Dutch baby preheats cast iron for 20 minutes and treats post-oven collapse as normal rather than a defect.

## Carry-forward rules

- Safety requirements for intentionally soft eggs belong in the measured ingredient list, not as an optional aside.
- Long rests and preheats must be reflected in inactive time, even when active preparation is short.
- Dramatic presentation is not a substitute for a sensory endpoint; record crispness, internal set, and expected post-service change.
