# Quality audit: recipes 031–035

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

BR031–BR035 pass structural validation, independent-body checks, TypeScript, formatting, ingredient allocation, food-safety review, and identity review after corrections.

## Corrections made

- BR031 and BR034 originally used “prepared miso soup” inside recipes presented as complete breakfast sets. Both now include dashi, miso, tofu, wakame, and a method that avoids boiling miso.
- BR031 source review counts were removed from `ratingCount`; the page did not establish that they were star-rating votes.

## Safety and identity checks

- BR032 requires a pasteurized egg and explicitly states that hot rice does not make an ordinary raw egg safe.
- BR034 uses a refrigerated measured cure and cooks salmon to 145°F.
- Okayu retains soft visible grains and minimal stirring, distinguishing it from broken-down Chinese congee.
- Kimchi fried rice requires mature kimchi, cold rice, moisture reduction, and sesame oil added off heat.

## Carry-forward rules

- A “set” recipe must fully author its substantial components unless a validated catalog relationship is explicit.
- Review counts are not rating counts.
- Raw or cured breakfast foods require concrete safety controls in the consumer method, not only editorial notes.
