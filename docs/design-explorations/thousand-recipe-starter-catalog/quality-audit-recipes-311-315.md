## Identity checks

- Mustard fish curry is Bengali shorshe maach built around freshly ground mixed mustard, nigella, mustard oil, green chile, and intact fish—not a generic yellow curry.
- Undhiyu preserves the labor and seasonal architecture of Surti undhiyu: fenugreek muthia, stuffed baby vegetables, winter beans, pigeon peas, yam, plantain, and layered cooking.
- Laal maas derives its red gravy from Mathania-style dried chile rather than tomato and keeps bone-in lamb, mustard oil, garlic, yogurt, and Rajasthan context central.
- Chicken karahi follows the onion-free Pakistani family pattern with small bone-in pieces, chopped tomato, no added water, active high-heat bhooning, and fresh ginger finish.
- Beef nihari uses shank, marrow, long low cooking, fennel-forward masala, measured atta thickening, and bright table garnishes.

## Safety and execution checks

- Mustard sauce is finely ground and gently heated; fish reaches 145°F without hard boiling.
- Undhiyu's muthia are steamed first, vegetables are layered by cooking time, and water is measured to prevent both scorching and collapse.
- Lamb in laal maas is braised well beyond 145°F to fork tenderness, with yogurt added gradually to prevent splitting.
- Karahi chicken reaches 165°F at the bone before the final high-heat reduction is accepted.
- Nihari beef is cooked until spoon-tender; the atta slurry is mixed cold, tempered with broth, and simmered to remove raw flour taste without becoming gluey.

## Familiarity and effort check

- Chicken karahi is a household anchor and an active-cooking meal of roughly 55 minutes total.
- Mustard fish curry is a cuisine anchor with moderate ingredient specificity but under one hour of total work.
- Undhiyu is a 135-minute, many-component cuisine anchor and must be treated as weekend or celebration cooking in discovery surfaces.
- Laal maas is explicitly discovery-tier and includes a two-hour marinade plus a long braise.
- Beef nihari is a cuisine anchor but a seven-hour slow cook. Its cultural importance justifies catalog inclusion; its effort excludes it from quick-meal and ordinary weeknight shelves.

The catalog plan now contains a mandatory final familiarity-and-effort gate: discovery remains a minority layer, time metadata governs quick-meal eligibility, and default discovery emphasizes household anchors plus genuinely approachable cuisine anchors.
