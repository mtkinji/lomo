# Quality audit: recipes 471–475

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

DE001–DE005 pass source comparison, roster continuity, original-body checks,
ingredient allocation, safety review, familiar-title review, and the strengthened
familiarity/effort gate. The staged corpus now contains 475 of the approved 500
recipes; the live catalog remains untouched.

All five are household anchors. The full mix is now 206 household anchors, 232
cuisine anchors, and 37 discovery recipes.

## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- All five names are immediately recognizable American favorites; none requires a
  translated catalog label.
- Cookies, brownies, and apple crisp use ordinary pans, familiar ingredients, and
  15 to 20 minutes of active preparation.
- Cheesecake requires a springform pan, water bath, and overnight chill; those needs
  are explicit, while its active method remains one crust and one filling.
- Double-crust apple pie is the single true project: cold pastry, three pounds of
  apples, juice reduction, assembly, staged baking, and four-hour setting are all
  counted rather than hidden.

## Stories and specificity

- Cookie notes explain why melted butter, brown sugar, yolk, cornstarch, and chilling
  create chew rather than presenting arbitrary tricks.
- Brownies explain the American cake-to-candy spectrum and connect dissolved sugar
  to the requested shiny crust.
- Pie acknowledges European roots without using the inaccurate slogan that apple
  pie itself originated in America.
- Crisp is framed as pie's practical household sibling and distinguishes its oat
  clusters from a fine streusel.
- Cheesecake explains what makes the New York style dense-creamy and graham-crusted.

## Safety and execution checks

- Brownies use a moist-crumb and temperature cue rather than the misleading clean
  toothpick test.
- Pie and crisp require visible bubbling from the fruit, not top color alone.
- Cheesecake water is poured only after the roasting pan is in the oven; the
  springform is double-wrapped and the water must stay out of the filling.
- Every recipe includes required cooling or chilling as part of texture development,
  and every listed ingredient is allocated.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Cookies center a 4.7/5 pattern across 1,991 reviews, with 4.5/5 across 549 and a
  long-running community favorite. Brown sugar, extra yolk, chilling, set edges,
  soft centers, and pan carryover recur.
- Brownies center 4.6/5 across 907 reviews plus two test-driven home sources. Hot
  butter-sugar, cocoa, chips, minimal flour mixing, moist crumbs, and cooling recur.
- Apple pie combines 4.7/5 across 200 reviews with two detailed test kitchens. Mixed
  firm apples, cold pastry, controlled juice, high initial heat, center bubbling,
  and full cooling recur.
- Apple crisp combines three broad household sources. Firm apples, cinnamon, cold
  butter, oat clusters, loose topping, and bubbling fruit recur.
- Cheesecake centers 4.8/5 across 1,138 reviews plus two major test kitchens. Full-fat
  room-temperature cream cheese, low mixing, gentle water-bath heat, wobble, gradual
  cooling, and overnight chilling recur.
