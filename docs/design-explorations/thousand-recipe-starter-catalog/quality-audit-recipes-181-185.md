## Identity checks

- Cast-iron steak uses dry brining, a hard dry sear, late aromatic basting, compound butter, measured doneness, and resting.
- Grilled cheeseburgers stay backyard-style rather than becoming smash burgers, with loose 80/20 patties and classic toppings.
- Baked macaroni and cheese deliberately selects the creamy béchamel branch and controls bake time to prevent dryness.
- Chicken pot pie uses concentrated filling and a scratch top crust, with regional crust variations documented rather than blended.
- Turkey meatballs use regular ground turkey, panade, Parmesan, aromatics, gentle handling, and sauce finishing.

## Safety and execution checks

- Steak includes culinary doneness ranges and separate USDA whole-cut guidance instead of obscuring the distinction.
- Burgers and turkey meatballs reach 160°F and 165°F respectively.
- Macaroni sauce melts cheese off heat; pot-pie chicken reaches 165°F before the cooled filling meets pastry.
- Pot pie vents steam and rests until the bubbling gravy can set.

## Carry-forward rules

- When high heat and dairy share a recipe, establish browning first and add butter or cheese only under controlled heat.
- Ground-meat tenderness depends on fat, limited handling, and correct salting as much as final temperature.
- A baked favorite needs intentional extra moisture before the oven because pasta and pastry continue absorbing liquid.
