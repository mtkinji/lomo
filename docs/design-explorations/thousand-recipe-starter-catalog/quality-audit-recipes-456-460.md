## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- Banana bread, zucchini bread, New York bagels, and soft pretzels are highly
  recognizable favorites.
- The less familiar Anadama loaf survives only because regional and baking sources
  independently establish its concrete value for toast, sandwiches, and soup. Its
  catalog name therefore leads with `Molasses-cornmeal sandwich bread`.
- Bagels are the batch's one true project: stiff kneading, individual rope shaping,
  overnight proof, float test, boiling, topping, and baking are all disclosed.
- Pretzels have shaping and a household baking-soda bath but remain a single
  roughly 75-minute session. Both quick breads use two bowls and a loaf pan.

## Stories and naming

- Banana and zucchini breads explain their American quick-bread roles and lead
  with familiar names.
- Bagels tell the Eastern European Jewish-to-New York story and reject tap-water
  mythology in favor of flour, fermentation, boiling, and baking.
- Soft pretzels explain Pennsylvania German roots and why the home recipe uses
  baking soda rather than professional lye.
- Anadama's disputed fisherman-and-Anna folklore is not repeated as fact; the
  reliable New England cornmeal-and-molasses identity is retained in the detail.

## Safety and execution checks

- Quick-bread centers reach 200–205°F and have foil-tenting and complete-cooling
  boundaries.
- Bagels use a float test before a controlled boil and reach 200°F.
- Pretzel baking soda is added in portions to limit foaming; the recipe explicitly
  excludes caustic lye and bakes to at least 195°F.
- Anadama's hot cornmeal mixture cools below 115°F before yeast is added.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

- Banana bread combines 4.9/5 across 221 reviews, 4.69/5 across 996 votes, and an
  editorial classic. Very ripe banana, butter, moderate sugar, brief mixing, tall
  center doneness, and cooling recur.
- Zucchini bread combines a roughly five-star 10,000-rating pattern with two major
  household versions. Coarse zucchini, oil, eggs, cinnamon, measured moisture,
  optional nuts, and brief mixing survive.

  beginner patterns. Strong flour, stiff dough, rope rings, overnight proof,

- Pretzels anchor on 4.8/5 across 535 reviews with home and test-kitchen methods.
  Elastic same-day dough, twists, baking-soda bath, salt, hot bake, and butter recur.
- Anadama combines New England baking, test-kitchen, and extension sources. Hydrated
  cornmeal, mild molasses, butter, conventional yeast dough, pan shaping, and broad
  toast-soup-sandwich usefulness recur.
