# Quality audit: recipes 056–060

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

BR056–BR060 pass structural and global validation, independent-body checks, TypeScript, formatting, ingredient allocation, safety review, and cultural-identity review without post-validation corrections.

## Identity checks

- Greek yogurt with honey and walnuts is intentionally complete at five ingredients; it uses the contract exception for canonical simplicity rather than low effort.
- Strapatsada reduces grated ripe tomato before forming moist egg curds and presents feta as common rather than universal.
- The full English describes a family of plates, uses recognizable British components, and coordinates separate cooking paths backward from egg service.
- Beans on toast deliberately retains British canned baked beans as culturally central convenience food rather than hiding an unfinished bean recipe.
- Bubble and squeak preserves its leftover purpose, roughly equal potato-and-greens ratio, and long undisturbed crusting.

## Safety and execution checks

- All intentionally soft fried eggs specify pasteurized eggs.
- Sausages reach 160°F; components are held warm without crowding them into one steaming pan.
- Cold mash and squeezed-dry greens control bubble-and-squeak structure.

## Carry-forward rules

- Convenience ingredients may be the honest canonical choice; distinguish them from unresolved prepared components by cultural role and recipe scope.
- Multi-component breakfasts require a service-timing plan, not just independent cooking instructions.
- Use the five-ingredient floor sparingly and only when every ingredient is structurally meaningful.
