## Desirability, familiarity, and effort

- All five are highly familiar American dinner or holiday sides. None exists to
  fill a cuisine quota.
- Scalloped potatoes are explicitly the one project recipe: uniform slicing,
  stovetop par-cooking, layering, a 75-minute bake, and a 15-minute rest are all
  visible rather than hidden.
- Mashed potatoes, sweet potato casserole, and green bean casserole use ordinary
  pots, bowls, and baking dishes. The latter two offer straightforward make-ahead
  boundaries instead of adding day-of coordination.
- Crispy roasted potatoes take 70 minutes but use one pot, one bowl, and one sheet
  pan; the time buys a major texture improvement and is mostly hands-off.

## Stories and naming

- Mashed potatoes explain why Yukon Gold plus russet creates a familiar balance
  without borrowing a chef's name as a quality claim.
- Crispy roasted potatoes tell the modern technique story in plain language and
  explain the baking soda rather than presenting it as a magic trick.
- Scalloped potatoes distinguish the overlapping scalloped and au gratin names
  without burdening the catalog title.
- Sweet potato casserole presents pecan and marshmallow toppings as two familiar
  American branches and clearly chooses the less-sweet pecan branch.
- Green bean casserole credits Dorcas Reilly and Campbell's 1955 test kitchen,
  while keeping the recognizable mushroom-cream-fried-onion architecture.

## Safety and execution checks

- Mashed potatoes have an explicit 30-minute warm-hold boundary.
- Garlic is strained from roasting oil before the 450°F potato roast so it does
  not burn.
- Scalloped-potato instructions require a mandoline hand guard and continuous
  broiler attention; the center must be fully tender.
- Egg-enriched sweet potatoes cool before the egg is added and bake to 165°F;
  nuts are tented if they darken early.
- Green beans are blanched, chilled, and dried; the assembled casserole reaches
  165°F.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or
  service.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Mashed potatoes combine five-star household responses with Good Food and Bon
  Appétit technique. Fully tender potato, steam-drying, warm dairy, butter, and
  restrained mixing recur.
- Crispy roasted potatoes combine the well-known alkaline-parboil method, 4.51/5
  across 97 votes, and a test-kitchen target. Rough starch, space, hot roasting,
  and garlic protected from burning recur.
- Scalloped potatoes combine a comparison of a five-star recipe, repeated
  five-star reader comments, and a home-cooking method. Uniform slices,
  garlic-thyme dairy, par-cooking, modest Gruyère, and resting recur.
- Sweet potato casserole combines a four-recipe comparison, Food Network testing,
  and a Southern holiday favorite. Sweet-potato-forward filling, moderate sugar,
  warm spice, and crisp pecan crumble survive.
- Green bean casserole combines the originator's recipe, a four-way comparison,
  and a chef method. Fresh beans, browned mushroom, thick cream sauce, soy-backed
  savoriness, and late-added fried onions recur.
