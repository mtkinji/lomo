# Yes-And: Thousand-Recipe Starter Catalog

Original idea: replace the shallow 100-recipe seed with 1,000 distinct,
research-informed, high-quality Kwilt recipes.

## Useful adjacencies

**Yes, and what if the catalog could make its coverage legible?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: users can see that abundance reflects cuisines, meal roles,
  dietary needs, effort levels, and occasions rather than random volume.
- Cost delta: low.

**Yes, and what if every recipe carried a private editorial record?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Kwilt can explain which source patterns informed the recipe,
  what reviewers consistently praised or corrected, and what was intentionally
  changed without republishing copyrighted expression.
- Cost delta: medium.

**Yes, and what if popularity and cultural authority were separate signals?**

- Serves: `jtbd-understand-why-ai-suggested-this`
- Job elevation: a highly rated Americanized version cannot silently define a
  regional classic; Kwilt can balance household accessibility with named
  tradition and disclose adaptations.
- Cost delta: medium.

**Yes, and what if the thousand recipes improved planning retrieval?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: independent facets for meal role, cuisine, diet, time,
  equipment, cost posture, freezer fit, and leftovers make the catalog useful
  to Meal Planning without turning Recipes into a dashboard.
- Cost delta: medium.

**Yes, and what if quality could improve without changing recipe identity?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: editorial corrections can publish a new immutable version,
  preserve prior cooking records, and avoid silently changing what a household
  previously made.
- Cost delta: medium.

**Yes, and what if household learning stayed separate from the base recipe?**

- Serves: `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`
- Job elevation: “we doubled the sauce” or “Sam preferred less heat” remains
  private household knowledge layered on top of a stable Kwilt recipe.
- Cost delta: low after Cook learning exists.

## Frame recommendation

Run the loop with the original frame. The thousand-recipe roster is already the
right bounded capability. The adjacencies define quality infrastructure and
retrieval metadata; they should not become extra launch surfaces.
