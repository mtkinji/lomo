## Identity checks

- Moroccan msemen is square-laminated from nearly translucent dough with butter, oil, and semolina between folds. It is differentiated from round meloui and holey one-sided baghrir.
- Nigerian akara uses freshly peeled black-eyed peas, minimal blending water, whipped batter, late salt, and hot oil. Its companion is fermented ogi/akamu pap, not generic cornmeal porridge.
- Australian avocado toast relies on deeply toasted sourdough, rough lemony avocado, and compact poached eggs rather than decorative excess.
- Modern overnight oats are chia-thickened and berry-forward, with no grated apple; this keeps BR089 distinct from Swiss Birchermüesli.
- The parfait fully authors a pressed, cluster-rich granola and packages it separately for make-ahead use, distinguishing it from the simple Greek yogurt, honey, and walnut bowl.

## Safety and execution checks

- Akara oil is maintained between 340°F and 360°F, and moisture is controlled before frying.
- Poached eggs are pasteurized, cooked below a boil, and blotted before touching toast.
- Overnight oats specify refrigeration at 40°F or colder and a three-day limit.
- Granola cools completely before storage or assembly.
