- Sheet-pan chicken encodes timing by vegetable density rather than pretending everything roasts equally.

- Honey-mustard sauce is divided before raw-chicken contact.
- Ground beef-and-pork meatballs reach 160°F before entering the glaze.

## Carry-forward rules

- “One pan” describes cleanup, not a requirement that every ingredient cook for the same duration.
- Sugar-heavy glazes belong late; fresh garlic and acid often benefit from a finishing layer.
- Remove quick proteins while slow starches cook, then reunite them only for service.
