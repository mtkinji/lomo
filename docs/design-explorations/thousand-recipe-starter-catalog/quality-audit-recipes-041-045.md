# Quality audit: recipes 041–045

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; no kitchen-test claim.

## Result

BR041–BR045 pass structural validation, independent-body checks, TypeScript, formatting, ingredient allocation, complete-component review, food-safety review, and cultural-identity review after two corrections.

## Corrections made

- BR042 now gives fried salted fish a 145°F endpoint instead of relying only on crisp appearance.
- BR045 removed an unused oil measurement and an unnecessary instruction to fry already-spent strained aromatics; the yellow broth is now strained, concentrated, and adjusted directly.

## Identity checks

- Longganisa is labeled as one sweet-garlic skinless style within a diverse Filipino sausage tradition.
- Champorado uses glutinous rice and tablea, with tuyo kept visible alongside for controllable sweet-salty contrast.
- Nasi lemak teaches the compact coconut-rice, sambal, anchovy, peanut, egg, and cucumber breakfast foundation.
- Kaya toast includes the pandan egg jam, crisp bread, cold butter, soft eggs, soy, and pepper.
- Bubur ayam keeps plain porridge, yellow chicken broth, shredded chicken, sweet soy, and dry garnishes as separate layers.

## Carry-forward rules

- Preserved seafood still needs a consumer-facing safe reheating or cooking endpoint.
- Do not add a cooking step merely to use an ingredient; remove redundant ingredients and protect a cleaner method.
- Regional variability belongs in notes and adaptation decisions, not in vague or generic recipe bodies.
