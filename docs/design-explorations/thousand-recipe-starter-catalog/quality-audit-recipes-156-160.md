- The Austrian Schnitzelsemmel source is 4.3/5 across 68 ratings; its toppings inform the handheld, while Austria Tourism’s official frying technique controls the schnitzel itself.
- Currywurst vendors protect sauces and regional styles conflict, so the recipe uses documented convergence—sliced pork sausage, curry-tomato sauce, curry dust—rather than claiming a secret formula.

## Carry-forward rules

- Use the right evidence for each layer of a composed dish; a sandwich rating should not override an official cutlet technique.
- When regions legitimately disagree, name the selected regional lean rather than blending all variants invisibly.
- Remove moisture before crisp bread assembly and add wet sauces after baking where tradition supports it.
- Rested minced-meat texture and cooked-bean texture need explicit endpoints, not vague timing alone.
