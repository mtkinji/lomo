## Familiarity and effort check

- Chicken shawarma rice plates and beef kofta with tahini are familiar household formats with ordinary equipment, accessible ingredients, and make-ahead marinating or chilling.
- Halloumi and vegetable souvlaki is the quickest recipe in the batch: 37 minutes of active preparation and cooking plus a 30-minute vegetable marinade.

- Sheftalia cooks quickly but is not an ordinary weeknight recipe because fresh caul fat requires advance butcher sourcing and careful raw-meat handling. The recipe explicitly directs cooks to choose beef kofta if caul fat is unavailable rather than making an inauthentic substitution.

- Chicken shawarma thighs reach at least 175°F and rest before slicing.
- Beef kofta reaches 160°F and rests before service.
- Halloumi and vegetables contain no raw meat; separate marinating protects texture and avoids unnecessary salt.
- Every listed ingredient is allocated to preparation, cooking, finishing, or service.
