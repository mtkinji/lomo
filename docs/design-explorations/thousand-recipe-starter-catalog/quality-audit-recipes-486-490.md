## Familiarity, story, and effort

- Peach cobbler and banana pudding retain their immediately familiar titles and use
  ordinary baking dishes; chilling and setting time are not active labor.
- Hummingbird cake keeps banana, undrained pineapple, cinnamon, toasted pecans, and
  cream cheese frosting but uses one sheet pan instead of three stacked layers.
- Its detail story explicitly follows the recipe from Jamaican Doctor Bird roots to
  the famous 1978 Southern Living publication rather than claiming Southern invention.
- Bread pudding uses stale French bread and a separately served bourbon sauce, with
  an alcohol-free portion available.
- Beignets are the single true project because overnight dough, rolling, hot-oil
  temperature control, serial batches, and immediate service cannot honestly be
  described as low effort.

## Highest-rated and authority pattern synthesis

- Peach cobbler centers 4.7/5 across 246 ratings plus two test kitchens. Firm fruit,
  cold biscuit dough, topping gaps, vigorous bubbles, and cooked undersides recur.
- Banana pudding combines three Southern authorities around cooked custard, fresh
  banana, abundant vanilla wafers, layered assembly, chilling, and a soft topping.
- Hummingbird cake centers 4.8/5 across 82 ratings with historical reporting. Oil,
  banana, pineapple juice, cinnamon, pecans, cream cheese, and Jamaican roots recur.
- Bread pudding combines Louisiana and regional authorities around stale French
  bread, full soaking, raisins, nuts, a 170°F custard, and separately cooked sauce.
- Beignets combine destination, restaurant-review, and tested home evidence. Square
  cold-fermented dough, 350–365°F frying, airy centers, immediate service, and heavy
  powdered sugar recur.

## Safety

- Beignets include pot-fill limits, a thermometer, turned-in handles, child and pet
  exclusion, no water on oil fires, a nearby lid, and complete oil cooling.
- Bourbon is added off heat away from direct flame.
- Egg custards have cooking temperatures or full-boil requirements, and dairy-rich
  leftovers are refrigerated promptly.
