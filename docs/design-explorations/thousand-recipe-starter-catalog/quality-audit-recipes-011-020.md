# Quality audit: recipes 011–020

Date: 2026-08-06
Proof state: desk-reviewed; none of these recipes is represented as kitchen-tested.

## Result

The second ten pass the editorial contract and are strong enough to continue the workflow. They are independently authored, use three traceable references apiece, state intended textures, and distinguish cultural origin from later adoption. The batch should still receive actual kitchen testing before any recipe is labeled cooked or repeat-validated.

## What held up

- Each recipe preserves its defining structure rather than accumulating popular toppings.
- Texture endpoints are observable: reduced salsa, steam-dried potatoes, glossy eggs, drained pico, spoonable ful, and spotted flatbread bottoms.
- Mexican, Texas Mexican, Austin Tex-Mex, North African, Turkish, Egyptian, and broadly Levantine labels are specific without making exclusive origin claims the sources cannot sustain.
- Legitimate variation is disclosed where it matters: crisp versus soft chilaquiles, onion in menemen, optional feta in shakshuka, and canned versus dried favas.
- All ingredients are accounted for in the method and all methods include timing or visual endpoints.

## Corrections made during audit

- **BR015 Molletes:** removed an unused half portion of measured salt; the pico now uses the stated amount cleanly.
- **BR020 Za’atar manakish:** reduced the home-oven target to 475°F and required parchment rated for that temperature, avoiding an unsafe assumption about generic parchment at 500°F.

## Recurring risks to carry forward

- Do not infer authenticity from star count. Ratings identify useful execution signals; regional and authorial sources establish identity and context.
- Treat garnishes as controlled finishing elements, not a license to overload a canonical dish.
- For egg dishes, the stop point must be written before carryover heat makes the recipe dry.
- For bread, chips, and tortillas, moisture management must appear explicitly in both ingredients and method.
- Continue the manual ingredient-allocation audit; structural validation cannot detect a measured ingredient that is only partly used.

## Gate

Automated checks: pass. Editorial audit: pass with the two corrections above. Continue to recipe 021 only with the same source-triangulation, texture, cultural-context, and ingredient-accounting requirements.
