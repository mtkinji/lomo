# Chosen Three-Piece K — F Shapes + Open Air + Fair Curves Round 06

Status: visual refinement only. No production logo, app icon, or lockup has changed.

K1 consolidates the three accepted properties into one primary construction:

- **F silhouette vocabulary:** lemon/leaf upper-right piece and quarter-sphere lower-right piece.
- **Open Air proportions:** approximately 16% lighter stem, wider horizontal counters, and greater separation between the two right pieces.
- **Fair contour standard:** complete, continuous curves wherever a surface is meant to curve.

## Lower-right construction

The lower-right piece is not rounded around its entire perimeter. Its upper-left boundary is one uninterrupted fair arc. Its right and bottom boundaries are intentional cut planes, meeting through one controlled outer corner. This preserves the quadrant character without reintroducing a bump or turning the piece into a pebble.
