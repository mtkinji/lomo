# Chosen Three-Piece K — Fair-Curve Refinement Round 02

Status: visual refinement only. No production logo, app icon, or lockup has changed.

Option F from Round 01 is the positional baseline. Its optical centering, three-piece structure, negative-space `K`, and piece relationships are locked. This round redraws only the contours.

## Curve brief

- Curves must look complete rather than truncated by accidental flats.
- Deliberate straight sections are allowed, but every entry into and exit from a curve must be eased.
- The stem uses asymmetric curvature: a relatively tight lower-left turn flows into a much larger lower-right sweep without a horizontal bottom.
- The stem crown is a complete capsule-like curve rather than two radiused corners joined by a short flat.
- The right edge of the upper-right piece is a continuous convex sweep, not a flattened side.
- The lower-right piece uses a continuous outer bowl with no clipped-looking bottom or right edge.

## Options

- **F1 — Controlled fair:** preserves the most geometric discipline and is the recommended baseline.
- **F2 — Pill-forward:** fuller crowns and terminals, especially on the stem.
- **F3 — Soft fair:** minimizes straightness and gives the pieces the most organic fabric character.
