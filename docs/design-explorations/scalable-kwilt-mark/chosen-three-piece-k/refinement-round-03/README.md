# Chosen Three-Piece K — Stem Weight and Spacing Round 03

Status: visual refinement only. No production logo, app icon, or lockup has changed.

F1 Controlled Fair is the contour authority. This round keeps its fair curves intact and changes only stem width and spacing.

- **G1 — Moderate air:** stem narrowed 8%; right pieces remain in F1 positions.
- **G2 — Balanced air:** stem narrowed 12%; right pieces move slightly outward and apart.
- **G3 — Open air:** stem narrowed 16%; all gaps are opened more assertively.

## Recommendation

G2 is the intended balance point. It gives the negative-space `K` more room and reduces the stem's dominance without making the three pieces feel unrelated. G1 is the conservative fallback; G3 tests the upper boundary before cohesion begins to weaken.
