# Chosen Three-Piece K — Restored Lower Quadrant Round 04

Status: visual refinement only. No production logo, app icon, or lockup has changed.

Balanced Air (G2) is now the primary spacing system. This round corrects one overreach from the fair-curve work: the lower-right piece had become rounded around almost its entire perimeter and began reading as a pebble or rock.

## Correction

- Preserve G2's 12%-slimmer stem and expanded gaps.
- Preserve the fair stem crown, asymmetric stem bottom, and fair upper-right contour.
- Restore the lower-right piece's intentional vertical right cut and horizontal bottom cut.
- Join those cut planes with a controlled rounded outer corner.
- Preserve one broad fair diagonal surface across the upper-left side, recreating the earlier quarter-sphere or quadrant character.
