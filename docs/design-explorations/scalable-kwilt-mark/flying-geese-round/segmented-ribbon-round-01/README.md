# Segmented Ribbon Round 01

Status: visual exploration only. No production logo, app icon, or lockup has changed.

This round isolates concept 1.3 from the first Flying Geese board: three broad diagonal lanes assembled from softly tapered segments. The weave is carried by repeated direction, interrupted mass, and the ivory channels between pieces rather than by a literal over-under crossing.

![Twelve refinements of concept 1.3](boards/segmented-ribbon-refinements-12.png)

## Precision brief

Preserve the original mark's gentle curve tension and segmented rhythm while resolving it as one geometric family:

- one shared diagonal axis and coordinated curve cadence;
- repeatable band widths and optically equal negative channels;
- systematic segment proportions and seam phases;
- related terminal radii and taper logic;
- balanced visible mass within a calm, nearly square silhouette.

The rows explore faithful optical cleanup, controlled changes to segmentation rhythm, and reductions intended to survive at 16–24 pixels. Mathematical precision here means disciplined relationships between organic pieces—not replacement with rectangles, capsules, or generic diagonal stripes.

## Review gate

Select the two or three variants whose character survives both full-size viewing and small-size reduction. Those should be reconstructed as exact vectors and compared at 16, 24, 32, and 64 pixels before any color or app-icon treatment.
