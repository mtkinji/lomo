# Direction Brief: The Arc

Study board: [12 generated variants](boards/the-arc-12.png)

## Core idea

Two broad trajectories converge and cross once. The mark keeps the continuity and over-under logic of weaving while leaving behind the square, knot, and textile pattern.

## Brand meaning

Kwilt helps life in motion become coherent. Separate paths can meet, redirect, and continue without being turned into a rigid plan. The crossing is a moment of meaning rather than a permanent bind.

## Desired character

- Calm motion rather than speed.
- Organic but not wellness-coded.
- Open, breathable, and optimistic.
- Distinct from a swoosh or navigation arrow.

## Construction rules

- Exactly two broad arcs or curved bands.
- One crossing, convergence, or clearly ordered pass.
- Open terminals and generous negative space.
- No closed loop unless it is visibly not an infinity symbol.
- Avoid leaves, hearts, waves, globes, swooshes, roads, arrows, eyes, fish, and yin-yang forms.

## Twelve variant prompts

1. Two rising arcs crossing once near the lower-left.
2. One vertical arc and one diagonal trajectory meeting near center.
3. Two opposing curves forming an open, asymmetric `S` relationship.
4. One sheltering arc crossed by a shorter upward path.
5. Wide low composition with a single calm convergence.
6. Tall narrow composition with long open terminals.
7. Two quarter arcs whose negative space suggests a path forward.
8. Offset crossing that creates a subtle abstract `K` without a stem.
9. One clean interruption expresses the underpass.
10. Nearly circular flow with a deliberately open side and no loop closure.
11. Angular-curved hybrid with broad softened bends.
12. Most reductive form: two strokes, one crossing, one memorable directional silhouette.

## Generation brief

Create a professional black-and-white logo exploration board for a privacy-conscious family-life app named Kwilt. Show twelve clearly different standalone marks in a precise 4-column by 3-row grid, read left to right and top to bottom. Every mark must follow The Arc: exactly two broad curved bands or trajectories that converge or cross once, expressing calm life in motion and continuity without forming a knot or textile pattern. Vary arc radius, crossing position, openness, orientation, proportion, asymmetry, terminal angle, and negative-space flow according to the twelve variant prompts above. Use solid near-black shapes on a perfectly flat warm-ivory background. Give every mark equal optical size and generous clearspace. Clean vector-logo aesthetic, crisp edges, no texture. No words, letters, numbers, captions, grid lines, app-icon containers, shadows, gradients, mockups, thin strokes, infinity symbols, leaves, hearts, waves, globes, swooshes, roads, arrows, eyes, fish, yin-yang forms, sparkles, needles, or literal quilt imagery. Each silhouette must plausibly survive at 16 pixels.
