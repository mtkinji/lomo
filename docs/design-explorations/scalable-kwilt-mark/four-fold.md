# Direction Brief: Four Fold

Study board: [12 generated variants](boards/four-fold-12.png)

## Core idea

Four broad folded corners turn toward a shared open center. The result suggests a quilt, gathering, and mutual structure without drawing fabric, stitches, or a complete woven pattern.

## Brand meaning

Different parts of life can remain distinct while contributing to one coherent place. The open center is important: Kwilt creates room for meaning rather than filling every space with a system.

## Desired character

- Composed and welcoming.
- Modular without looking corporate.
- Softly rotational, not energetic or celebratory.
- Recognizable from silhouette before internal detail.

## Construction rules

- Three or four broad folded-corner forms, with four preferred.
- One open central counter: diamond, rounded square, or irregular soft aperture.
- No external rounded-square outline.
- Rotation should feel settled rather than spinning.
- Avoid an eight-point star, flower, aperture, camera shutter, pinwheel toy, six-lobed knot, or literal patchwork block.

## Twelve variant prompts

1. Four equal rounded `L` folds around a small diamond opening.
2. Four soft chevrons with a larger calm center.
3. Four quarter-turn bands with one understated over-under rhythm.
4. Compact blocky folds with clipped inner corners.
5. Open, airy folds with wide gaps between pieces.
6. Slightly asymmetric center while preserving four-part balance.
7. Three visible folds plus one implied by negative space.
8. Four short curved brackets gathered around a rounded-square opening.
9. Four folded tabs with alternating long and short reach.
10. Diamond-oriented composition with horizontal and vertical calm.
11. Nearly circular outer silhouette built from four squared folds.
12. Most reductive form: four chunky corner gestures and one large center.
