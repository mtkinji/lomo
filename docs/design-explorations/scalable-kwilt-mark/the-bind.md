# Direction Brief: The Bind

Study board: [12 generated variants](boards/the-bind-12.png)

## Core idea

Two independent rounded forms clasp around a small shared space. The gesture is reciprocal: neither form contains, dominates, or absorbs the other.

## Brand meaning

Kwilt helps people hold responsibilities, relationships, and intentions together without surveillance or forced sameness. The Bind expresses dependable connection and consent more than textile craft.

## Desired character

- Protective without appearing locked.
- Relational without becoming sentimental.
- Compact, grounded, and reversible.
- Clear enough to draw from memory.

## Construction rules

- Exactly two filled forms.
- Forms may resemble rounded brackets, bent bands, or soft `L` shapes.
- Preserve a small but open central space.
- One overlap, tuck, or alternating corner relationship at most.
- Avoid chain links, handshakes, hearts, shields, locks, speech bubbles, and interlocking rings.

## Twelve variant prompts

1. Opposing rounded `L` forms clasping diagonally.
2. Horizontal bracket forms meeting around an open center.
3. Vertical bracket forms with one tucked corner.
4. Two bent bands offset like protective bookends.
5. One larger form supporting a smaller equal-weight partner without enclosure.
6. Compact square silhouette with opposing open corners.
7. Wide low silhouette with a gentle central overlap.
8. Tall narrow silhouette with a generous center gap.
9. One clean negative-space notch reveals which form passes above.
10. Softly rotated clasp with balanced, non-circular mass.
11. Two near-mirrored forms whose mismatch creates a memorable center.
12. Most reductive form: two chunky hooks, one open center, no decorative cut.

## Generation brief

Create a professional black-and-white logo exploration board for a privacy-conscious family-life app named Kwilt. Show twelve clearly different standalone marks in a precise 4-column by 3-row grid, read left to right and top to bottom. Every mark must follow The Bind: exactly two broad rounded filled forms clasping around a small open shared space, reciprocal and balanced, with no form swallowing the other. Vary orientation, proportion, bracket shape, asymmetry, overlap, central gap, and terminal geometry according to the twelve variant prompts above. Use solid near-black shapes on a perfectly flat warm-ivory background. Give every mark equal optical size and generous clearspace. Clean vector-logo aesthetic, crisp edges, no texture. No words, letters, numbers, captions, grid lines, app-icon containers, shadows, gradients, mockups, thin strokes, chain links, rings, handshakes, hearts, shields, locks, speech bubbles, infinity symbols, sparkles, needles, or literal quilt imagery. Each silhouette must plausibly survive at 16 pixels.
