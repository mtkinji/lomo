# Direction Brief: The Join

Study board: [12 generated variants](boards/the-join-12.png)

## Core idea

Two broad ribbons meet in one legible over-under event and together imply a soft, abstract `K`. The letter should be discovered after the mark is recognized, not announced as a monogram.

## Brand meaning

Kwilt helps separate parts of life become coherent without flattening them into one system. The two ribbons retain their own paths while forming a stable whole. The single crossing carries the weave equity with far less visual noise than the current pattern.

## Desired character

- Quietly ownable rather than clever.
- Balanced but not mechanically symmetrical.
- Broad, rounded, and warm.
- Stable enough for intimate life data; alive enough to imply movement.

## Construction rules

- Exactly two primary ribbon forms.
- One clear crossing or underpass.
- A subtle `K` may emerge through a vertical or near-vertical spine and two rightward gestures.
- No exterior container or border.
- Open negative spaces must remain generous at 16 pixels.
- Avoid thin strokes, extra interior bands, Celtic-knot repetition, sharp arrowheads, and a literal typographic `K`.

## Twelve variant prompts

1. Centered upright spine with a soft diagonal ribbon crossing at the middle.
2. Wide, low stance with the crossing slightly above center.
3. Tall, narrow stance with a low crossing and generous right-side opening.
4. Curved spine with restrained upper and lower diagonal gestures.
5. Straight spine with softly clipped diagonal terminals.
6. Compact square silhouette with a small central underpass.
7. Asymmetric upper arm, stable lower arm, and an open center.
8. Asymmetric lower arm, stable upper arm, and a high crossing.
9. Crossing expressed by one clean negative-space interruption.
10. Two nested ribbon ends that imply joining without touching at every point.
11. Slightly rotated, diamond-weighted silhouette with no enclosing diamond.
12. Most reductive form: two chunky pieces, one cut, one unmistakable `K` echo.

## Generation brief

Create a professional black-and-white logo exploration board for a privacy-conscious family-life app named Kwilt. Show twelve clearly different standalone marks in a precise 4-column by 3-row grid, read left to right and top to bottom. Every mark must follow The Join concept: exactly two broad rounded ribbons, one unmistakable over-under crossing, and a subtle abstract K that is discovered rather than read as typography. Vary proportion, crossing height, curvature, asymmetry, openness, terminal shape, and use of negative space according to the twelve variant prompts above. Use solid near-black shapes on a perfectly flat warm-ivory background. Give every mark equal optical size and generous clearspace. Clean vector-logo aesthetic, crisp edges, no texture. No words, letters, numbers, captions, grid lines, app-icon containers, shadows, gradients, mockups, thin strokes, six-lobed knots, infinity symbols, chain links, Celtic knots, sparkles, needles, or literal quilt imagery. Each silhouette must plausibly survive at 16 pixels.
