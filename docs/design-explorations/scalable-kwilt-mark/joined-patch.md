# Direction Brief: Joined Patch

Study board: [12 generated variants](boards/joined-patch-12.png)

## Core idea

Two or three broad geometric pieces become a single, memorable whole through one overlap or fitted seam. The mark uses the compositional logic of a quilt rather than the visual language of thread.

## Brand meaning

Life is made of pieces that do not need to look alike to belong together. Kwilt creates coherence through arrangement and relationship, not through uniformity.

## Desired character

- Warm, tactile, and constructed.
- Contemporary rather than crafty.
- Stable enough to feel like a place.
- Simple enough to stamp, embroider, or render in one color.

## Construction rules

- Two primary patches preferred; three allowed when the silhouette stays simple.
- One overlap, fitted notch, seam, or shared edge.
- Rounded outer corners with selective straighter internal edges.
- Do not use a four-square grid or familiar software-tile arrangement.
- Avoid stacked-document, copy, Dropbox, Microsoft, photo-gallery, fabric-swatch, or craft-store cues.

## Twelve variant prompts

1. Two offset rounded squares joined diagonally by one fitted notch.
2. One tall patch and one wide patch sharing an interlocking corner.
3. Two soft rectangles overlapped with a single visible step.
4. Two unequal pieces forming a balanced near-square silhouette.
5. One rounded piece nested against a clipped-corner piece without enclosure.
6. Three broad patches forming one open central seam.
7. Two pieces joined by a shallow dovetail-like negative space.
8. Diagonal composition with one corner appearing folded beneath the other.
9. Horizontal composition with a calm offset seam.
10. Vertical composition with a compact, stamp-like silhouette.
11. Two pieces whose shared negative space creates a subtle `K` echo.
12. Most reductive form: two rounded blocks and one decisive fitted cut.

## Generation brief

Create a professional black-and-white logo exploration board for a privacy-conscious family-life app named Kwilt. Show twelve clearly different standalone marks in a precise 4-column by 3-row grid, read left to right and top to bottom. Every mark must follow Joined Patch: two or at most three broad geometric pieces becoming one memorable whole through one overlap, fitted notch, seam, or shared edge. The logic should evoke composed life pieces and modern quilt construction without depicting fabric or thread. Vary proportion, offset, seam type, orientation, asymmetry, corner treatment, and use of negative space according to the twelve variant prompts above. Use solid near-black shapes on a perfectly flat warm-ivory background. Give every mark equal optical size and generous clearspace. Clean vector-logo aesthetic, crisp edges, no texture. No words, letters, numbers, captions, grid lines, app-icon containers, shadows, gradients, mockups, thin strokes, four-square grids, stacked documents, copy icons, Dropbox-like diamonds, Microsoft-like tiles, photos, swatches, sparkles, needles, or literal quilt imagery. Each silhouette must plausibly survive at 16 pixels.
