# Evaluate Learning: Unified Plan Reaction Guide

## Questions

- Does the resting row stop implying an unrecorded dislike?
- Can a person find Upvote, Not for me, and Hard pass without option word labels?
- Does the optional reason feel conversational rather than like a form?
- Does a lead understand that Include anyway acknowledges rather than erases the Hard pass?
- Does the floating guide feel compact and native over the Plan drawer?

## Evidence

Support: Andrew can identify the neutral entry point, choose either direction without hesitation, and distinguish the idle row from recorded reaction pills in Simulator use.

Disconfirming: negative reactions are missed, the expanding guide feels modal or oversized, Hard pass looks like an absolute veto, or reasons create pressure to explain every response.

## Decision rule

Keep the expanded guide if all three response strengths are discoverable, reasons remain optional in practice, and leads correctly understand the one-time review gate. Otherwise simplify the negative set or remove the reason field before adding persistent row chrome.
