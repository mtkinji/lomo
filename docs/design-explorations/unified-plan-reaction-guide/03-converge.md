# Converge: Unified Plan Reaction Guide

## Chosen direction

Use one neutral smile-plus affordance. Open the existing floating, non-blocking bottom guide with five emoji-only choices under **Upvote**, five under **Not for me**, and one isolated 🚫 under **Hard pass**. Choosing Hard pass expands the same guide for an optional short reason.

Existing reaction pills remain evidence. A thumbs-down appears in the row only after a person has actually chosen it. Once the viewer responds, the smile-plus disappears; tapping their selected pill removes the response and restores the neutral entry point.

## Reduction

- Remove the standalone idle thumbs-down.
- Remove visible word labels from individual emoji tiles.
- Add no preset reason taxonomy, permanent veto, net score, or new settings.
- Require a household lead to acknowledge a Hard pass before that recipe moves to Groceries.

## Bet

We're betting that balanced expressive reactions plus one deliberately separate Hard pass will give a household enough range without making participation feel like a survey. If Hard pass reads as either meaningless or absolute, revisit the Grocery acknowledgement—not the resting row.
