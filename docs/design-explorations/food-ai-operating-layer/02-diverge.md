# Diverge: AI Operating Models

## A. Import assistant

AI exists primarily in capture flows: OCR, URL extraction, voice structuring,
and ingredient parsing.

Strengths: immediately understandable, measurable, and valuable.\
Weaknesses: leaves planning, coordination, savings, and fulfillment as manual
islands; creates an AI feature rather than an operating model.

## B. Chat-first food agent

Most food work begins in Unified Chat. Chat maintains conversational state and
calls food-specific tools.

Strengths: flexible, fast to explore, and aligned with natural requests.\
Weaknesses: risks duplicating native capability state, hiding consequential
review inside a transcript, and making Food feel inaccessible without good
prompting.

## C. Capability-native AI operating layer

Recipes, Meal Planning, and Groceries register typed operations once in the
canonical capability manifest. Native surfaces, Unified Chat, action cards,
Phone, and future Gmail ingestion use the same schemas, evidence, confirmation,
proposal, mutation, and receipt contracts.

Strengths: operation parity without duplicated authority; native and
conversational entry points remain coherent; each capability validates its own
rules; channel coverage is mechanically testable.\
Weaknesses: requires upfront domain and authority work before visible screens.

## D. Autonomous household food autopilot

AI watches context, chooses meals, applies deals, builds carts, and completes
orders with minimal interaction.

Strengths: maximal apparent convenience.\
Weaknesses: exceeds available retailer authority; hides preference and rights
decisions; makes allergy, family, budget, and substitution errors more
consequential; weakens trust precisely where proof matters.

## Convergence criteria

The choice must:

- reduce work for a person who never opens Chat;
- allow natural-language operation without creating a second data model;
- make transcription uncertainty and source evidence inspectable;
- keep invitations, publication, product selection, offers, and checkout at
  their correct authority levels;
- work without a retailer provider; and
- expand to future channels without rewriting food business rules.
