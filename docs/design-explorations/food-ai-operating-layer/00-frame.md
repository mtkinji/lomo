# Frame: AI-Native Household Food

## Offered idea

AI should make every household-food job dramatically easier and, within
reason, be able to perform every meaningful operation a user can perform in the
native product. Import is foundational: photographing a handwritten card or a
cookbook page should produce a reviewable Recipe draft instead of a data-entry
project. The release should be a polished vertical experience, not a minimally
functional collection of screens.

## Target audiences and personas

Primary: `audience-aspirational-family-organizers`, represented by **Maya**.
Maya's goal is not to operate an AI agent; it is to feed the household with less
repeated decision, transcription, coordination, arithmetic, and retailer
re-entry.

Secondary: `audience-ai-native-life-operators`, represented by **Nina**. Nina
expects to ask Kwilt to do work in natural language, but grants authority only
when the inputs, effects, confirmation boundary, and result are inspectable.

## Hero JTBD and job-flow gaps

The household outcome remains anchored in
`jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`. The AI trust contract is anchored in
`jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`.

This improves two weak steps in `job-flow-maya-move-family-life-forward`:

- **Know the next doable action — 2/5:** turn scattered food evidence into the
  next reviewable action.
- **Schedule or hand off — 2/5:** carry an approved choice into a Recipe, plan,
  grocery list, and external retailer without re-entry.

It also exercises Nina's full trust flow: declare scope, inspect evidence,
review a proposal, authorize a consequential action, receive a durable receipt,
and return to the exact work context.

## Active anchors

- `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`
- `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life`
- `jtbd-understand-why-ai-suggested-this`
- `jtbd-stay-in-control-of-ai-actions`
- `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending`

## Aspirational design challenge

How might Kwilt let Maya move from a photo, URL, spoken memory, or simple
request to a trustworthy meal and grocery outcome with dramatically less work,
while giving Nina operation-level evidence, authority, correction, and receipts
and preserving capability ownership, private family boundaries, public rights,
and retailer truth?

## System posture

Extend the existing capability manifest and proposal/receipt control plane. AI
is an operating layer over Recipes, Meal Planning, Groceries, Activities, and
future connectors. It is not a fourth food capability and not a parallel copy
of the domain model.
