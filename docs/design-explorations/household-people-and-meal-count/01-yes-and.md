# Yes-And: Household People And Meal Count

## Original idea

Give Meals an honest household counter, then let Kwilt remember the people
behind that count—with optional birthdays, contact import, and delivery details
that can later support thoughtful gift help.

## Adjacencies

### Yes, and what if the roster earned its detail gradually?

Meals can begin with `7 people` and no setup debt. When Maya later records a
food need, birthday, gift note, or invitation, Kwilt can offer to attach that
fact to a named Person or create one in place.

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- Job elevation: household context becomes more useful through ordinary life
  instead of becoming a profile-completion project.
- New value: immediate utility and a progressively richer household model can
  coexist.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if every enrichment moment is optional and the
  original action remains completable without it.

### Yes, and what if quantity and identity improved meals independently?

The usual count remains the recipe-quantity default. Known diners add only the
context they can truthfully provide: recorded food needs, preferences, and past
serving corrections. Kwilt can say `7 people · 2 known` without implying that
the other five people do not belong.

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- Job elevation: Maya can cook enough now while gaining better fit as explicit
  household knowledge accumulates.
- New value: incomplete identity data no longer produces a false quantity.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if unknown diners never trigger invented household
  knowledge or false safety claims.

### Yes, and what if a Person could exist before participation?

A baby, partner, grandparent, or frequent guest can have a lightweight Person
record without receiving an account, device profile, paid seat, Household
authority, or shared-data access. If that person later joins Kwilt, the accepted
identity can bind to the existing Person rather than creating a duplicate.

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: Kwilt can remember who matters without confusing care with
  software participation.
- New value: one durable human identity can support household context today and
  consensual participation later.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only if participation, permissions, auth, and payment
  remain independent and reviewable.

### Yes, and what if Contacts were a one-person shortcut rather than a growth hack?

From Add person, Maya can choose one iOS contact and review which fields to
bring into Kwilt. The default is name; birthday and postal address are separate
choices. Manual entry remains equally supported, and Kwilt never uploads the
address book or uses contacts for discovery or invitations.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: existing facts reduce retyping without turning private contact
  data into a social graph.
- New value: a useful Person record can be created in seconds.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass only with contextual access, per-field review,
  visible provenance, and full deletion.

### Yes, and what if birthdays became gentle occasion context?

An optional birthday can support a calm upcoming-occasion cue and a user-owned
follow-through action. Month and day are sufficient by default; year is
optional. Kwilt does not shame missed dates or create recurring work the user
cannot dismiss.

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: remembered dates help Maya show up for people she loves rather
  than merely maintaining a calendar.
- New value: person context can become timely, meaningful action.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if prompts are opt-in, calm, correctable, and never
  anthropomorphic or guilt-driven.

### Yes, and what if gift help began with remembered intent, not a storefront?

A Person can hold explicit gift context Maya chooses to save—interests, sizes,
past ideas, budget, and `not this` notes. When Maya asks, Kwilt can prepare a
small explained shortlist. It does not infer sensitive traits, mine activity,
or push shopping outside a real occasion.

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: the hard part becomes choosing a thoughtful fit, not searching
  from scratch under deadline pressure.
- New value: birthday context and explicit knowledge can become a differentiated
  assistance loop with willingness to pay.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only if AI stays user-activated, explains which
  explicit facts shaped each idea, and remains useful without a purchase.

### Yes, and what if affiliate commerce were only the final handoff?

After Maya chooses a gift, Kwilt can open a qualifying retailer link with a
plain commission disclosure. The shortlist ranks recipient fit, budget, timing,
availability, and delivery confidence—not payout. Initially the address can be
copied after review; any future retailer-address transfer requires an explicit
partner contract and a separate confirmation.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: monetization helps complete an already-chosen act of care
  without turning Kwilt into an ad surface.
- New value: affiliate revenue can follow genuine utility and high-intent
  commerce.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only with disclosure, no pay-to-rank behavior, no
  disguised ads, and no address in ordinary affiliate URLs.

### Yes, and what if every sensitive fact had a clear home and exit?

Each Person shows only the context Maya intentionally saved, where it came
from, and what uses it: Meals, occasions, gift help, or participation. She can
correct a field, remove one capability's use, unlink a source contact, or delete
the Person without hunting through settings.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Maya can let Kwilt become useful without losing confidence in
  what it knows or why.
- New value: privacy controls become part of the value proposition rather than
  compliance residue.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if there is no dashboard, trust score, or blanket
  permission switch that hides capability-specific use.

## Job elevation

The original counter fixes one false answer. The larger opportunity is a calm,
private Person foundation that lets Kwilt remember enough explicit context to
help Maya feed, include, celebrate, and care for people with less repeated work.
The Person is the reusable value unit; account membership and commerce remain
separate relationships around that person.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with an expanded frame.** Keep the immediate
learning release narrow—count-first Meals plus lightweight named People—but
design the Person contract so birthdays, one-contact import, later account
binding, and user-requested gift help can reuse it. Do not include occasion
prompts, gift recommendations, or affiliate handoff in the first build.
