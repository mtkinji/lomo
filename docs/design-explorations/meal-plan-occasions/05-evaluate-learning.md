1. Did the organizer understand that placement was optional?
2. Could the household answer what is fixed, what is covered, and what remains
   flexible without opening an editor?

4. Did selected meals visibly leave the cart while unselected ideas stayed?
5. Did the Grocery handoff name the exact committed plan version?
6. Which timing controls were ignored, misunderstood, or overused?

two-account household evidence, native accessibility follow-ups, and the actual
