# Yes-And: Meal Plan Occasions

## Original idea

After the household chooses meals, let Maya identify what is for a particular
day and meal period without requiring a fully populated weekly calendar.

## Adjacencies

**Yes, and what if it could answer the household's recurring coverage questions
without five duplicate assignments?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: moves from isolated meal placement to a legible food rhythm.
- New value: **Weekday lunches — leftovers and sandwiches** can be complete as
  one decision.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if coverage remains optional and compact.

**Yes, and what if meals could remain explicitly flexible rather than merely
missing a date?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: preserves a real decision without manufacturing certainty.
- New value: groceries can be ready while the family chooses the exact night
  later.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no forced commitment.

**Yes, and what if the settled plan became the household's calm awareness
surface?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: family members can answer **what are we eating?** without
  asking Maya again.
- New value: participation in the cart ends in shared understanding, not just a
  private organizer artifact.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if it is not a feed or dashboard.

**Yes, and what if placement could change without silently corrupting the

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: the plan can adapt while downstream work stays explainable.
- New value: a moved, removed, or added meal produces a reviewable Grocery diff.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium

**Yes, and what if selected meals naturally left the cart while unselected ideas

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`

- New value: the cart stays useful across cycles without duplicating settled

- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if stale ideas remain easy to remove.

**Yes, and what if cooking completion made the next plan begin with less work?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: a plan becomes a living food cycle rather than a saved calendar.
- New value: cooked or skipped meals leave **Next meals**, while useful learning

- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass only if completion is natural and never becomes a
  tracking ritual.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with an expanded frame.** The right frame is not
“add meal slots.” It is **progressive commitment from shared possibility to a
shop-ready, sparsely timed household plan**. Exact occasions, flexible meals,
coverage, and downstream Grocery truth must be designed as one system, while
cooking-history automation remains outside the first release.
