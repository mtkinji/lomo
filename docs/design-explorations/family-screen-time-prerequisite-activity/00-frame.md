# Frame: Family Screen Time Prerequisite Activity

## What the user said

> Create a rule that Charlie has to read scripture for at least five minutes before he can unlock other apps like games.

## Restated in user voice

When a child reaches for entertainment before a family intention, Maya wants the device to carry out the calm agreement the family already made, so the intention does not depend on another reminder or manual unlock.

## Target audience

`audience-aspirational-family-organizers` — families who want reliable household rhythms without adopting a productivity system.

## Representative persona

Maya is setting one understandable agreement for Charlie.

- Current situation: She repeatedly explains or manually enforces what should come before Games.
- What she is trying to do: Let the phone follow one predictable family agreement.
- Emotional tension: She wants less negotiation without turning Kwilt into surveillance.
- What would feel wrong: Claiming five foreground minutes prove that Charlie read, learned, or acted with good intent.

## Hero anchor

`jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter` — the rule matters only if it helps the family carry a valued intention into ordinary life.

## Job flow step

Maya's **Family participation**, **Schedule or hand off**, and **Keep using the system** steps score 3, 2, and 3. Household participation exists, but recurring family follow-through still depends on caregiver administration.

## Active anchors

- `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` — Chat carries a stated family intention into a durable rule.
- `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse` — one chosen app activity comes before entertainment access.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` — the proposal, device state, and measurement boundary must stay explicit.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Fit the system`

Current system facts:

- Existing surface: Unified Chat already stages consequential Screen Time proposals.
- Existing flow: Caregivers manage a named child under Household; Apple selection remains a native step.
- Existing model: A Family Access Rule combines selected targets, schedule, responsibilities, and usage criteria with AND semantics.
- Existing technical affordances: saved semantic selections, versioned agreements, Managed Settings stores, and Device Activity as the planned threshold owner.
- Existing copy: name the real action, show one current truth, and never shame the child.

Constraints to preserve:

- Chat proposes; a caregiver approves.
- Apple app tokens remain in the authorized native boundary.
- Foreground time is evidence of app use, not evidence of reading or spiritual intent.
- No generic boolean-rule canvas, points, streaks, or child activity feed.

Design implication: extend the existing agreement rule with one prerequisite-selection threshold. Do not create a Gospel Library integration or a visible rules-engine product.

## Aspirational design challenge

How might we help Maya carry a family intention into a predictable device agreement for Charlie, while preserving child dignity, privacy, and truthful evidence?

## Out of scope

Content inspection, reading verification, background-audio counting, Android enforcement, and arbitrary nested automation.

## Open question

None blocks the first build: daily reset is the bounded default when the request does not name a schedule.
