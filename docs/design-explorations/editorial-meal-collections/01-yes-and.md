# Yes-And: Editorial Meal Collections

Original idea: rotate editorial Collection offers through Meals and let some
Collections provide a complete plan the household can review and adopt.

## Adjacencies

**Yes, and what if a Collection could offer different-sized commitments?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: move from browsing to an appropriately sized first step.
- New value: the same Collection can offer a three-dinner sampler, five-meal
  plan, or open exploration without pretending every theme should fill a week.
- Cost delta: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; the user chooses the commitment.

**Yes, and what if a prepared plan were designed as one grocery basket?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: reduce shopping work and waste, not only meal-choice work.
- New value: shared ingredients, intentional leftovers, and sensible package
  use become editorial properties of the plan.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if savings and waste claims state their assumptions.

**Yes, and what if the household reacted to a proposal instead of a blank page?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: make family participation easier without turning dinner into a
  general poll.
- New value: invited people can keep, swap, pass, or suggest against one bounded
  prepared plan; the organizer still finalizes.
- Cost delta: low-medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no majority rule or default household access.

**Yes, and what if adopting a plan preserved its origin but ended the live link?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: make prepared help inspectable without compromising ownership.
- New value: “Started from Dinners on a Budget” remains visible, while later
  editorial revisions cannot mutate the household's copy.
- Cost delta: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; provenance is explanatory, not promotional.

**Yes, and what if the plan adapted during review, before it became household state?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: turn a generic starting point into something usable with
  minimal configuration.
- New value: servings, meals needed, dietary exclusions, and already-on-hand
  ingredients can reshape the proposal before copying.
- Cost delta: medium-high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if changes are previewed and confirmed; fail if AI
  silently substitutes meals.

**Yes, and what if a household could repeat its own successful variation?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: convert a one-time editorial discovery into durable household
  continuity.
- New value: reuse the family's modified plan rather than reconstructing it or
  returning to the original Collection.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; explicit reuse is preferable to opaque ranking.

**Yes, and what if rotation were an edition rather than a feed algorithm?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: keep discovery fresh without extracting attention.
- New value: a deterministic weekly composition can balance cuisine,
  circumstance, and season while remaining stable for the household.
- Cost delta: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass with a strict placement budget and no notifications.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with an expanded frame:** build an editorial
Meals system in which authored Collections may contain versioned prepared-plan
templates, deterministic editions distribute them, and explicit copy semantics
turn a template into a private household draft.
