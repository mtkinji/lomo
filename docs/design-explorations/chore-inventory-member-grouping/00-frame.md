# Frame: Chore Inventory Member Grouping

## What the user said

> I expect to see a group of chores for Charlie, a group for Olive, and a group for Household. “Group by member” is probably the right language.

## Restated in user voice

When Maya reviews the household's recurring work, she wants to scan each person's responsibilities and the shared family work as coherent groups, so she can understand the household rhythm without repeatedly filtering or reading every assignee pill.

## Target audience and persona

`audience-aspirational-family-organizers`, represented by Maya. She wants family organization that is immediately legible and does not become a view-building hobby.

## Hero anchor and job-flow step

- `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- `job-flow-maya-move-family-life-forward`: **Family participation**, currently 3/5. The caregiver inventory exists, but responsibility is only repeated row-by-row instead of being scannable by participant.

## Active anchors

- `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` - recurring responsibilities need to be easy to review and maintain.
- `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in` - the inventory should make each member's bounded participation clear.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` - filtering and grouping must have distinct, predictable effects.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Fit the system`.

- Existing surface: the caregiver's stable Chore-series inventory.
- Existing affordance: a funnel filters the inventory to one child or Household.
- Existing pattern: `InventoryControlGroup` uses `layers` for grouping in To-dos and Plan.
- Existing row truth: each series already has a named-member or Household assignment.
- Constraint: no new route, settings screen, saved-view model, dashboard, or persistence contract.

## Aspirational design challenge

How might we help Maya scan recurring household work by participant at a glance, while preserving the quiet inventory and keeping filter and grouping behavior plainly distinct?

## Out of scope

Sorting, collapse state, saved views, drag-and-drop, caregiver assignment, rotations, and production Household authorization.

## Open question

None for this local learning slice.
