# Evaluate Learning: Bank Roll Pacing

## What to observe

- Can players tap through rolls one, two, and three without a countdown?
- Does the three-second countdown appear after roll three while at least two players remain unbanked?
- If another player banks during that countdown, does the sole remaining roller become able to roll immediately?
- Does rapid play still feel readable after the dice-settle animation?

## Evidence boundary

Pure rule coverage and repository verification prove the state policy and integration compile. Simulator, signed-device, or TestFlight interaction remains separate runtime evidence.
