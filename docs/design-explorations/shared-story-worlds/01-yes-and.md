# Yes-and: Durable Play Identities

## Original idea

Original idea: Give each player a durable story identity—a name, defining trait, special ability, treasured item, and relationship—and optionally let their Kwilt pet become that character or take part in the adventure.

## Frame recommendation

**Run design-thinking-loop with an expanded frame** — the opportunity is not simply persistent RPG characters or a Pixel Pet crossover. It is a **durable play identity** that can project into different shared stories while keeping capability ownership clear.

The working model should have three connected layers:

1. **Player** — the real participant across Games. Owns durable display identity such as name, color, and feedback preferences.
2. **Character** — who that player is in one story world. Owns the defining trait, ability, treasured item, and story growth.
3. **Pet** — an optional Pet-owned identity that can be projected into a story as the character or a companion, without gaining mechanical power from real-life productivity.

Relationships belong to the shared story or campaign, not to either person's global profile. They need to be visible and mutually appropriate because they describe two participants, not one.

For the first learning slice, offer **play as yourself** and **make a character**, both with an instant AI suggestion that can be accepted in one tap. Let the completed session earn a saveable character and recap. Integrate pets only after this human-first identity and story loop proves that families want to return.
