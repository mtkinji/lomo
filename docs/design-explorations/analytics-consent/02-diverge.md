# Diverge: Analytics consent

Axis: when and where the choice appears.

1. **Settings-only withdrawal.** Default on; one explanatory control in Legal & privacy. Preserves the prior product posture and adds durable control, with explicitly accepted policy risk.
2. **First-run consent gate.** Force a yes/no decision before entering Kwilt. Maximizes visibility but adds coercive setup friction and makes optional analytics feel required.
3. **Contextual invitation.** Ask after a meaningful outcome. Better timing, but adds prompt orchestration and risks confusing feedback with tracking permission.

All options require the same versioned persisted state and consent-managed runtime. None may use streaks, default sharing, anthropomorphic copy, or feature penalties.
