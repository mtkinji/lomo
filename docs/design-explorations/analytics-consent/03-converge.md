# Converge: Analytics consent

Choose the Settings-only toggle with analytics on by default. It fits the existing privacy surface, preserves prior behavior, and avoids forced commitment. Denied and withdrawn states are off; unknown and granted states are on. A disclosure-version change preserves the user's last explicit preference.

Before, Nina cannot withdraw production analytics. After, she can withdraw and renew from Legal & privacy, with withdrawal immediately removing the active client, clearing queues, resetting identity, and opting out. No feature becomes unavailable.

Reduction: no new route, prompt, dashboard, confirmation dialog, or event-specific switches. The three-second read is “Optional analytics → Share product analytics → Off/On.”

Activation is organic discovery in Legal & privacy. We are betting that a plain, persistent control protects trust better than an interruptive first-run prompt. If users cannot find it, revisit with a contextual link rather than a blocking gate.
