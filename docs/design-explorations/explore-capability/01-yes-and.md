# Yes-And: Explore capability

## Original idea

Kwilt turns real-world movement into a private-first family exploration game, clearing a 65-foot core through fog with a long, wispy transition while coloring the route by elevation.

## Adjacencies

1. Elevation becomes part of the visual story rather than a score.
2. Overlapping walks create familiar territory while new outings extend the known world.
3. Named parks, trails, overlooks, and summits become confirmed Place visits.
4. Completed adventures can replay route, fog clearing, elevation, and photos.
5. Opted-in family members contribute to a shared territory layer while retaining private exact paths.
6. Chapters can retrospectively connect outings and Place visits into a seasonal family story.
7. GPS accuracy can shape confidence so buildings and weak signals do not create false discoveries.
8. Territory-only sharing can synchronize coarse explored cells without exposing raw routes.

## Anchor relationship

The expansion serves `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`, `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`, and `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`. It also suggests a future anchor candidate: “Help us explore our world together and remember where we have been.” This candidate is not added to the taxonomy in this release.

## Frame recommendation

Run the loop with the expanded frame: a private-first family exploration layer, not a map novelty or passive location tracker.
