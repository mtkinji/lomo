# Yes-And: Category Budget Planning

Original idea: remove the confusing quick-edit drawer and clarify which jobs belong to Category settings versus a future amount-planning flow.

**Yes, and what if it could show where each category amount came from?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: moves amount editing from raw input to explainable plan maintenance.
- New value: Maya can tell whether an amount came from history, a recurring bill, a starter template, or her own override.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if it stays receipt-like, not dashboard-like.

**Yes, and what if changing one amount showed the living-target impact?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: turns the living percent from onboarding copy into an operational plan constraint.
- New value: Maya can see whether Shopping going up means the plan is over target or simply uses buffer.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if it answers one consequence, not a spreadsheet.

**Yes, and what if Category settings became only behavior and maintenance?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: reduces ambiguity around where durable category behavior lives.
- New value: rollovers, forecast source, Screen Time rules, and naming feel predictable.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass because it collapses surfaces instead of adding one.

**Yes, and what if Budget Detail hosted the amount decision only when the meter makes it relevant?**

- Serves: `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending`
- Job elevation: activates planning at the moment Maya sees a category is unrealistic.
- New value: an adjustment feels contextual rather than like a buried setting.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the affordance is quiet and does not nag.

**Yes, and what if one-off monthly exceptions stayed separate from durable limits?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: lets Maya handle school supplies, trips, or annual bills without corrupting the regular category plan.
- New value: temporary adjustments can be honest without changing the baseline.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if exceptions are few and plain.

## Job elevation
The bigger job is not "edit a budget." It is "keep this category's role in the household plan truthful."

## Frame recommendation
Run design-thinking-loop with the expanded frame: retire the drawer now, and define the amount-adjustment feature as a future allocation flow rather than a generic settings form.
