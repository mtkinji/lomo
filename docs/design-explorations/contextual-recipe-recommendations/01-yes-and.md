# Yes-and: Contextual Recipe Recommendations

This is a bounded refinement of the accepted Recommended surface, not a new
capability or open-ended AI behavior. Broader signals such as recency, family
input, ingredient reuse, and budget fit remain worthwhile future inputs, but
adding them here would dilute the sharp job: stop suggesting a meal period that
has already passed.

Job elevation: the change makes the existing shortlist begin from the user's
real planning horizon instead of merely rotating catalog popularity.

Frame recommendation: ship the original bounded refinement.
