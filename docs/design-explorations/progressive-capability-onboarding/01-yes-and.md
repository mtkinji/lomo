# Yes-And: Progressive Capability Onboarding

## Original idea

Original idea: Kwilt should replace one universal, Goals-shaped onboarding funnel with a thin
global orientation followed by progressive, capability-owned first-value journeys.

The accepted frame keeps Maya's live need at the center. These adjacencies extend the idea only
where they make Kwilt calmer, more coherent, or more useful over time.

## 1. Remember capability progress without exposing a setup dashboard

**Yes, and what if it could remember where Maya is with every capability, so leaving, returning,
or switching devices never makes her repeat irrelevant setup?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`, `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life`
- Job elevation: Progressive onboarding becomes continuity. Kwilt remembers what value Maya
  reached, what she deferred, what blocked her, and where she should resume.
- New value: A user can partially connect Money, pause Screen Time authorization, or leave a Goals
  creation flow and later return at the truthful next step. Existing Money or Games users can be
  recognized from their data instead of treated as beginners.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if this state stays mostly invisible and produces calm resumption. It
  fails if Kwilt exposes a completion dashboard, percentage, checklist, or red badge for every
  capability.

## 2. Route from evidence of intent, not only a first-run question

**Yes, and what if it could use the way Maya arrived as the first routing signal—an invitation,
deep link, existing data, selected App Store promise, or explicit request—before asking her to
choose from a catalog?**

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`, `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: Orientation starts closer to the real situation that made the job urgent.
- New value: A parent following a Screen Time invitation, a Money tester with connected accounts,
  and a new user who says “help me organize family tasks” can enter different useful paths while
  still sharing one Kwilt identity and shell.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if routing is legible, correctable, and never traps the user. It fails
  if opaque personalization hides capabilities, over-interprets marketing attribution, or lets AI
  silently decide what the user needs.

## 3. Give every capability a minimum meaningful outcome contract

**Yes, and what if it could require each capability to define the smallest real outcome that
earns its place in the user's Kwilt?**

- Serves: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`, `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: “Onboarded” stops meaning that setup screens were completed and starts meaning
  that the user received a durable, inspectable result.
- New value: Goals creates a Goal and Arc; Money reaches a trustworthy Summary from real or
  recognized data; Games gets a playable table underway; Screen Time applies and acknowledges a
  real policy; Stories captures or reveals a memory. Each journey ends in its native value.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if outcomes remain capability-specific and user-meaningful. It fails if
  a shared metric flattens unlike capabilities into one activation score or encourages artificial
  “aha moment” theater.

## 4. Turn permissions and data reuse into explicit value exchanges

**Yes, and what if it could show exactly what a capability is asking to use, what becomes possible
now, and what already-authorized Kwilt context can be carried forward?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`, `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life`,
  `jtbd-stay-in-control-of-ai-actions`
- Job elevation: The user gets continuity without surrendering authorship or granting blanket
  access to an expanding mega app.
- New value: Maya does not re-enter household identity or preferences, but Kwilt still asks before
  a capability uses sensitive cross-domain context. OS permission, in-app consent, data ownership,
  and AI action authority can be explained as separate truths.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if scope is concise, concrete, revocable, and attached to immediate
  value. It fails if consent becomes a wall of legalistic toggles or if prior use is treated as
  permission for unrelated future capabilities.

## 5. Coordinate an attention budget across the entire app

**Yes, and what if it could let the shell arbitrate when a capability may teach, celebrate,
request permission, show a paywall, or recommend an adjacent capability?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Progressive learning becomes calm and situational instead of an accumulation of
  individually reasonable interruptions.
- New value: A Screen Time permission prompt cannot collide with a Goal celebration, a Focus
  coachmark, a paywall, and a cross-capability recommendation. Deferred education can wait for a
  later relevant moment rather than disappearing forever or firing immediately on the next screen.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the mechanism protects attention and remains invisible. It fails if
  it becomes an engagement engine that optimizes prompt impressions, urgency, or feature adoption
  at the expense of the user's current job.

## 6. Make cross-capability expansion a handoff from one achieved outcome

**Yes, and what if it could introduce another capability only when the current result creates a
natural next job, with a visible explanation of the connection?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`, `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`,
  `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life`
- Job elevation: Kwilt's breadth becomes compounding usefulness rather than a menu of products to
  sample.
- New value: A family Goal may make shared participation relevant; an Activity may make Focus or
  scheduling relevant; a connected Money view may reveal a planning question; a finished season
  may make a Chapter meaningful. The handoff carries only the context needed and returns to the
  source outcome.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the adjacency is evidence-based, optional, and quiet. It fails if
  capabilities cross-sell one another, if the Agent acts like a salesperson, or if Kwilt invents
  work merely to increase breadth adoption.

## 7. Let onboarding belong to a person, role, device, and space—not merely an account

**Yes, and what if it could adapt activation to who is participating, what authority they have,
which device they are on, and which private or shared Space owns the result?**

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`, `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Family adoption becomes truthful coordination instead of one account owner
  configuring everyone through a generic household funnel.
- New value: A caregiver, child, invited collaborator, and solo owner can encounter different
  explanations and required actions. Screen Time authorization can be device-specific; a shared
  recipe or Goal can make its ownership boundary explicit; a person can participate in Household
  and broader-family Spaces without capability access leaking across them.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if participation is private by default and roles are created only when
  needed. It fails if ordinary onboarding creates empty households, assumes a family structure,
  or makes sharing the price of progress.

## 8. Treat dormancy, product change, and migration as reorientation—not failure

**Yes, and what if it could recognize that life capabilities are seasonal and offer a lightweight
return path when the person, capability, or product has materially changed?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`, `jtbd-recover-when-i-drift-from-an-arc`
- Job elevation: Kwilt supports a durable relationship with uneven real life instead of optimizing
  only for first-session completion and daily active use.
- New value: A dormant Goals user can return without guilt; a redesigned Money capability can
  explain only the changed contract; a standalone-app tester can migrate through recognized data;
  an interrupted setup can resume without starting over.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if return language is neutral and useful. It fails if dormancy triggers
  streak-loss framing, punitive reminders, forced recommitment, or a promotional “what's new” tour.

## What the expansion reveals

The original progressive-onboarding idea is correct, but the word *onboarding* is too narrow for
the product system being described. The durable concept has three layers:

1. **Capability activation** - reach one minimum meaningful outcome through a capability-owned
   journey.
2. **Adoption continuity** - remember progress, configuration health, scope, role, device, and
   truthful resumption without exposing a management dashboard.
3. **Expansion orchestration** - coordinate attention and offer cross-capability handoffs only
   when demonstrated relevance makes the broader Kwilt more useful.

This is not a marketplace, setup center, or feature-adoption program. It is the operating contract
that lets one Kwilt contain deep capabilities without making the user absorb the whole system.

## Anchor coverage

No new JTBD node is required yet. The expansion is well covered by:

- `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter` for intent-to-outcome routing;
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` for calm, continuity, consent, and reliability;
- `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life` for bounded context reuse;
- `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` for outcome-producing activation;
- `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in` for role- and Space-aware adoption.

“Help me discover what Kwilt can do” is not yet a missing anchor. Discovery is valuable only when
it advances one of these existing user jobs; treating product discovery as an end in itself would
pull the work toward feature promotion.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with an expanded frame: Progressive Capability Adoption and
Continuity.**

Keep the existing design challenge and Maya pressure test, but let divergence cover the entire
lifecycle from first intent through first value, deferred setup, relevant expansion, dormancy, and
resumption. “Progressive Capability Onboarding” can remain the artifact slug and familiar shorthand;
the expanded name prevents the design from collapsing back into first-run screens.
