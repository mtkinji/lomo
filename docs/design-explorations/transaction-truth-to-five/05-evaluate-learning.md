# Evaluate Learning: Transaction Truth To Five

## Evidence ladder

- Move to 4 only after authenticated review persistence and a five-merchant self-use pass.
- Move toward 4.5 after split persistence, one-row inventory, exact meter reconciliation, and reversal pass in TestFlight.
- Award 5 only after a complete statement cycle and representative households show the step is easy, trustworthy, and not routinely administrative.

## Disconfirming evidence

- Split rows duplicate or disappear across surfaces.
- Allocation totals diverge from the bank amount or category meters.
- Users abandon the editor or split routine purchases frequently.
- Merchant memory becomes less reliable after split exceptions.

Privacy-preserving instrumentation may count split starts, saves, abandonment, and replacement; it must not include merchant, description, account, or category-label content.
