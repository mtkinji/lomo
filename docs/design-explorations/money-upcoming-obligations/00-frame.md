# Frame: Money Upcoming Obligations

## What the user said

> I wonder what features or capabilities we might add without disrupting other parts of the app.

## Restated in user voice

When an ordinary month contains bills and deposits that have not happened yet, Maya wants to know what money is already spoken for before making a choice, so she can act confidently without managing a cash-flow spreadsheet.

## Target audience

`audience-aspirational-family-organizers` — people coordinating ordinary family life who need useful financial truth without a budgeting hobby.

## Representative persona

Maya is checking whether a household choice still fits. She can already see what happened and what remains in the monthly plan, but she also needs to understand the near-future obligations that could make today’s apparent room misleading.

## Hero anchor

`jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter` — Money matters when it carries a household intention into a real decision.

## Job flow step

`job-flow-maya-review-budget-reality-before-spending`: Kwilt is strongest once Maya reaches plan evidence and correction, but weaker at entry, repeat trust, and helping her account for obligations that have not posted yet.

## Active anchors

- `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending` — the direct Money job.
- `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` — future obligations must affect the decision, not merely become another report.
- `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life` — detected patterns must be provisional, inspectable, and correctable.

## Competitive signal

Origin makes recurring and upcoming obligations a first-class part of the spending experience. The useful adaptation is not its breadth or dashboard density; it is the way future commitments become legible before the user makes another decision.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Fit the system`

- Money owns the route, derived evidence, confirmations, and any later forecast contribution.
- The existing Money snapshot already contains transactions, categories, accounts, dates, planned amounts, due days, pending status, and forecast inputs.
- The first release can be a nested Money surface reached from Summary, with at most one contextual Summary line when the evidence is material.
- No new global tab, Home card contract, notification system, cross-capability queue, or Chat behavior is required.
- Detection may suggest a recurring obligation; it must not silently change the plan or claim cash is safe through payday without authoritative balance and income-timing evidence.

## Aspirational design challenge

How might Money show Maya what is likely already spoken for, using evidence she can inspect and correct, while remaining a quiet extension of the existing Money model rather than a new app-wide system?

## Out of scope

Subscription cancellation, financial advice, arbitrary purchase approval, automatic plan mutation, scheduled notifications, household invitations, full cash-flow forecasting, and “safe to spend until payday” claims.
