# Yes-And: Money Upcoming Obligations

Original idea: add useful Origin-inspired Money capabilities without disturbing Kwilt’s other capabilities or global shell.

## Adjacencies

1. **Likely recurring obligations** — identify repeated merchants or providers from existing transaction history and show the evidence behind the pattern. Serves `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending`; cost: low to medium.
2. **Lightweight confirmation** — ask “Recurring?” only when a yes or no would materially improve the projection. Serves `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`; cost: low.
3. **Already spoken for** — distinguish planned monthly room from likely obligations that have not posted yet, without overclaiming cash availability. Serves `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`; cost: medium.
4. **Explain the change** — answer why this month looks different by tracing a projected amount to exact transactions, timing, and user corrections. Serves `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`; cost: low.
5. **Quiet Money attention** — show one material, dismissible item on Summary when an upcoming obligation, stale connection, or meaningful anomaly deserves review. Serves `jtbd-review-budget-reality-before-spending`; cost: low to medium.
6. **Forecast contribution** — let confirmed obligations improve the existing category projection instead of creating a second planning model. Serves `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`; cost: medium.
7. **Through-next-payday view** — later combine confirmed obligations with reliable income cadence and balances. Serves `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse`; cost: medium to high and evidence-gated.

## Frame recommendation

Run the loop around upcoming obligations. It closes the clearest competitive gap with a Money-owned capability island and creates reusable evidence for later forecasts. Treat the attention line as a presentation of that capability, not a separate inbox or engagement system. Defer payday safety until balance and income-timing confidence are demonstrably strong.
