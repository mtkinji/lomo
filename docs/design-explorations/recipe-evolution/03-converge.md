# Converge: One Draft, Two Inputs

## Chosen experience

- Catalog action: **Edit for me**.
- Personal-edition action: **Update recipe**.
- One screen contains the natural-language/voice instruction and the complete structured draft.
- AI returns a bounded structured diff; the user applies, adjusts, and saves.
- Offline manual saving creates a device-current pending version.
- Version history is reached from the quiet identity line.
- Historical selection is read-only; restoration appends a new version.

## Reductive decisions

No separate AI chat, editing modes, version dashboard, required change-log field, silent AI save, silent upstream merge, or destructive rollback. Detailed compare is deferred.

## Bet

We are betting that people will prefer stating the meaningful change while still trusting the recipe because every proposed field is visible and directly editable. If AI usage is low but manual evolution is valuable, retain the same screen and reduce the prominence of the composer rather than weakening offline editing.
