# Frame: Recipe Evolution

## Restated in user voice

When cooking teaches me how this recipe works for me, help me update the recipe I will actually use next time, without losing the original, my earlier versions, or my work when I am offline.

## Target and job flow

Maya, representing `audience-aspirational-family-organizers`, activates `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` and `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`. This directly serves Food step 18, **Keep what was learned**, and step 19, **Begin again with less work**.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Extend the system`.

- Recipe versions are already immutable and saves use optimistic version authority.
- Recipe Home already exposes provenance/version and has a low-frequency actions drawer.
- The existing editor is a complete structured editing surface.
- Cook Complete already creates a reviewed recipe-edit proposal rather than silently changing content.
- Recipe drafts may be local-first; shared/versioned authority reconciles with Supabase.
- AI prepares; Recipes owns validation, mutation, receipts, and destination.

## Design challenge

How might we make updating a recipe feel as easy as saying what changed, while preserving direct offline editing, review, source lineage, and truthful immutable history?

## Out of scope

Shared household edit authority, public publication of personal editions, automatic upstream merging, and detailed side-by-side diff tooling.
