# Diverge: Recipe Evolution

## Axis

AI-primary versus direct-manipulation-primary, under unreliable connectivity.

## A. AI gate with manual fallback

The user must first describe a change; manual editing is secondary. It feels conspicuously AI-first but fails when the network is absent or unreliable and makes dependable editing feel like recovery from failure. Rejected.

## B. Traditional editor with AI helper

The structured editor remains primary with an optional AI button. It is dependable and fits the system, but AI feels bolted on and does not begin from the user's natural statement of what changed.

## C. One draft, two inputs

The Update Recipe screen begins with **Tell Kwilt what changed** and keeps all structured fields directly editable below. Connected AI prepares a reviewable diff into the same draft. Offline, direct editing and local saving remain complete. This fits capability ownership and avoids a parallel Chat artifact.

All alternatives preserve private-by-default state, explicit review, and non-anthropomorphic AI. C best answers the job without blocking capture or save.
