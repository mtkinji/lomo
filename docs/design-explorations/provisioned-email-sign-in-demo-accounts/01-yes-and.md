# Yes-And: Provisioned Email Sign-In And Demo Accounts

Original idea: add email sign-in partly so Kwilt can support one or more dependable demo accounts.

## Adjacencies

### Yes, and what if it could give App Review full account-backed access without a social-provider handoff?

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: reviewers can inspect the same ordinary authority boundaries users rely on.
- New value: fewer review delays and less dependence on special instructions.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if credentials open an ordinary account rather than a privileged review mode.

### Yes, and what if paired accounts could prove sharing and Household boundaries?

- Serves: `jtbd-invite-the-right-people-in`
- Job elevation: private participation can be evaluated from both sides.
- New value: reusable two-account QA for invitations, follow-along, leave, revoke, and Household roles.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the roster remains separate from private Goals, chats, Money, and Activities.

### Yes, and what if every evaluator received an isolated clone instead of sharing mutable state?

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: a representative experience stays coherent across repeated evaluation.
- New value: sales, press, usability, and support sessions do not overwrite one another.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if cloning remains server-owned and never creates a second product model.

### Yes, and what if demo reset became a repeatable release preflight?

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: the experience begins from a known, inspectable state.
- New value: App Review credentials, seeded content, entitlements, and backend readiness can be checked together before submission.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if reset evidence is operational and not presented as user behavior.

### Yes, and what if synthetic household data became the canonical source for screenshots and demonstrations?

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Kwilt can show intimate capabilities without exposing a real family.
- New value: one governed fictional data set can support App Store imagery, support docs, and demonstrations.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the data is clearly fictional internally and never implies provider evidence that does not exist.

### Yes, and what if provisioned email access later supported invited pilots without committing to public signup?

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: a person can receive bounded access without choosing a social provider.
- New value: a reversible learning path toward broader email authentication.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass if the invitation is honest about access and does not masquerade as general availability.

## Job elevation

The larger opportunity is not “another login button.” It is a governed, repeatable way to enter a representative account-backed Kwilt experience without weakening identity or using real household data.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with an expanded frame.** Build provisioned email sign-in and the synthetic demo-account lifecycle as one release. A login form without provisioning, reset, and safety controls would not solve the demonstrated job.
