# Yes-And: Workflow Experience Pulse

## Original idea

Add a sparse, reusable bottom-guide utility that can gather customer feedback at deliberately selected points in any end-to-end Kwilt workflow.

## Adjacencies

**Yes, and what if it could make every important workflow declare its measurable beginning, completion, failure, and abandonment states?**

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: distinguishes whether the user accomplished the job from whether they liked the experience.
- New value: prevents a positive opinion from masquerading as completion proof and a completion event from masquerading as satisfaction.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; this is an internal contract, not a user-facing dashboard.

**Yes, and what if it could offer a governed registry of question categories rather than one generic feedback question?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: asks about satisfaction, ease, confidence, clarity, or perceived success only when that construct fits the moment.
- New value: contextual wording without losing measurement validity.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; questions stay product-authored, bounded, and calm.

**Yes, and what if one portfolio-level arbiter protected the user's attention across every caller?**

- Serves: `jtbd-capture-and-find-meaning`
- Job elevation: lets the user keep moving while Kwilt samples feedback sparingly.
- New value: adding capabilities or invocation points cannot silently increase interruption frequency.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; dismissal is always available and no action is blocked.

**Yes, and what if low or mixed responses could reveal a bounded driver without opening a survey?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: identifies whether the experience was confusing, effortful, untrustworthy, incomplete, or technically broken.
- New value: turns a score into an actionable product signal without collecting private narrative.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass when the follow-up is optional and uses one-tap reason codes.

**Yes, and what if every question and sampling policy were versioned so changes remain interpretable over time?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: keeps product learning inspectable instead of silently changing what a trend means.
- New value: enables honest pre/post comparisons and controlled experiments.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; versioning is internal and creates no customer administration.

**Yes, and what if the signal could graduate from analytics into first-party product data only when a real product use appears?**

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: avoids collecting durable personal data before Kwilt needs to show, edit, learn from, or act on it.
- New value: preserves a migration path without prematurely creating a feedback inbox or customer profile.
- Cost delta vs. original: low initially, medium if graduation occurs
- Anti-pattern check: pass; no anthropomorphic “learning about you” claim and no hidden personalization.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with the expanded frame.** The offered bottom guide is the visible utility, but the job-serving product is a workflow measurement contract plus a governed experience-question system and portfolio-wide attention policy. This expanded frame matches the user's clarification and does not add a new customer-maintained concept.
