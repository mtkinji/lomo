# Frame: Cuisine Discovery

## What Andrew said

Cuisine shortcuts should be fun, small, square illustrations on white, but the
user-facing cuisine set should be much smaller than the catalog's regional
labels. Subcuisines can be represented another way.

## Audience and job

Maya, an aspirational family organizer, wants a quick way to choose a food
direction when she does not have a specific meal in mind. This primarily serves
`jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action` at job-flow step 7, **Prepare a plausible
short list**, currently scored 2/5.

## System alignment

Constraint posture: `Fit the system`.

- Meals already has exact cuisine metadata, inventory filtering, discovery
  shelves, editorial Collections, and Meal Plan selection.
- Exact regional cuisine labels remain recipe provenance and filter evidence.
- Cuisine families are presentation-owned navigation, not a replacement for
  authored recipe truth.
- Collections remain authored sets; cuisine families remain deterministic
  inventory lenses.

## Design challenge

How might we help Maya choose an inviting cuisine direction in seconds while
preserving the catalog's cultural and regional specificity underneath?

## Out of scope

- Treating a generated illustration as cultural authority.
- A flat illustrated card for every exact catalog label.
- Personalized cuisine ranking or passive taste inference.
