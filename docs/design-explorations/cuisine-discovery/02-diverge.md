# Diverge: Cuisine Discovery

## A. Flat exact-cuisine rail

Illustrate all catalog labels. This preserves specificity but creates roughly
150 competing cards, repeated art direction, and no useful orientation.

## B. Illustrated families with regional reveal

Use a compact canonical family set. The Meals home features twelve; `See all`
shows every family; opening one reveals its regional labels as quiet chips above
matching meals. This reuses inventory filtering and keeps provenance intact.

## C. Collections-only cuisine discovery

Represent cuisines only through authored Collections. This creates stronger
stories but leaves ordinary deterministic cuisine browsing dependent on
editorial publishing and freshness.

## Recommendation

Choose B. It offers fast orientation without flattening regional recipe truth or
making every browse direction an editorial claim.
