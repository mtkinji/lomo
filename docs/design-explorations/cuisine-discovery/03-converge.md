# Converge: Illustrated Cuisine Families

## Decision

Add 20 canonical cuisine families covering every exact catalog cuisine. Feature
12 on Meals and expose the full family set through `See all`. A family opens a
matching inventory with alphabetized regional labels available as secondary
filters.

## Reductive decisions

- One square white-background illustration per canonical family.
- No image for every subcuisine.
- No cuisine landing-page domain, preference profile, ranking model, or new
  navigation root.
- No flags, people, costumes, landmarks, or baked-in labels in artwork.
- The UI owns card radius; source assets remain square.

## Capability delta

Today Maya can choose from a long exact-cuisine filter or scroll recipe shelves.
After this release she can enter a broad food direction visually, then narrow to
a region without losing exact recipe provenance.

## Bet

We're betting that a small visual vocabulary will make cuisine browsing feel
inviting without making the Meals home noisier. If families are understood but
regional chips are ignored, retain the family rail and simplify the reveal.
