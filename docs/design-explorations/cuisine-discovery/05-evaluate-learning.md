# Evaluate Learning: Cuisine Discovery

## Questions

- Do the small cards read as cuisine navigation rather than Collections?
- Can someone predict what opens before tapping?
- Do regional labels clarify specificity without becoming filter clutter?
- Are the illustrations recognizable, respectful, and distinct at card size?

## Evidence and decision

Use Andrew-only visual dogfood first: open several families, refine to a region,
add a meal to Meal Plan, enlarge Dynamic Type, and relaunch. Keep the system when
the rail remains calm and shortens discovery. Revise the family names or mapping
when a destination feels culturally incoherent; revise art when it relies on a
stereotype or becomes ambiguous at small size.
