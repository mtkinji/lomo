# Converge

Keep the expiring hosted feedback link, but make it a task rather than an
explanation. The first viewport names whose Plan this is and asks `Which meals
would you eat?` directly above compact Plan-like rows. A guest can choose every
meal that works and optionally reveal one `Suggest a meal` field.

Existing choices become positive support on their normal Plan rows. Because a
new suggestion has no row yet, preserve it in a compact organizer receipt. A
guest choice never creates a Household member, edits the Plan, or triggers the
signed-in hard-pass grocery gate. After submission, and only then, offer the
free app as an optional way to keep participating in future meal planning.

The bet is that temporary, capability-scoped participation produces useful
input with less friction and less privacy cost than Household onboarding—and
that a concrete choice plus one optional idea is clearer than a survey preamble
or a reaction palette. The app invitation earns its place only after the guest
has finished the job.
