# Yes-And: Kwilt Money Capability Integration

Original idea: fold Kwilt Money into the new Kwilt navigation paradigm as the first
imported capability.

**Yes, and what if it could...** prove the durable import contract that Games and later capabilities reuse.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: future capabilities arrive without multiplying app shells or global owners.
- New value: a tested capability navigator, lifecycle, settings, and exact-return pattern.
- Cost delta vs. original: low
- Anti-pattern check: pass; reuse contracts, not genericized product UX.

**Yes, and what if it could...** make one household identity explicit across Goals and Money without merging their domain models.

- Serves: `jtbd-carry-intentions-into-action`
- Job elevation: household intentions and financial reality can relate without duplicating people or permissions.
- New value: future bounded links such as opening a Goal from a Money category.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if participation and privacy boundaries remain explicit.

**Yes, and what if it could...** let unified Chat explain the month using bounded Money evidence and return to the exact category or transaction.

- Serves: `jtbd-get-help-without-retelling-my-life`
- Job elevation: Maya can ask about current reality without reconstructing it manually.
- New value: evidence-grounded read help before Money mutations are enabled.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass if Chat never replaces the authoritative Money surface.

**Yes, and what if it could...** unify financial privacy with Kwilt's app-switcher and foreground lifecycle instead of locking the whole app indiscriminately.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: sensitive Money content stays protected without making Goals or To-dos feel blocked.
- New value: capability-local unlock and background-cover rules.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; use native Face ID/passcode behavior, not custom security theater.

**Yes, and what if it could...** consolidate Money widgets and Screen Time controls into Kwilt's existing native targets.

- Serves: `jtbd-put-intention-before-impulse`
- Job elevation: financial intention reaches glanceable and spending-time surfaces from one installed app.
- New value: one extension family, app group, and release contract.
- Cost delta vs. original: high
- Anti-pattern check: pass if permissions remain contextual and existing selections are not falsely claimed to migrate.

**Yes, and what if it could...** give account deletion, export, privacy disclosures, and entitlement state one complete cross-capability truth.

- Serves: `jtbd-trust-this-app-with-my-life`
- Job elevation: Maya can understand and control all data attached to her Kwilt identity.
- New value: fewer orphaned records and contradictory subscription states.
- Cost delta vs. original: medium
- Anti-pattern check: pass; these stay global settings domains.

## Frame recommendation

**Run the design-thinking loop with the original frame.** Money-first is already the
right scope. The adjacencies belong as later phases and acceptance contracts, not as more
surface area in the first learning release.
