# Evaluate Learning: Explore Missions and Stories System

## Decision to make

After the learning release, decide whether to:

1. expand Missions and place-triggered Story prompts;
2. refine the same loop before expanding;
3. keep Stories but remove the Mission ceremony from ordinary capture; or
4. stop and preserve only the media foundation.

## Primary learning questions

1. Does the invitation make the outing feel more meaningful or merely more tasked?
2. Can a recipient explain what the sender will and will not see?
3. Is photo plus sentence enough to feel like a Story worth revisiting?
4. Does the linked To-do help follow-through without creating duplicate status work?
5. Can users find the Mission again before and after completion?
6. Do Stories feel like the natural library, or do users repeatedly ask for raw media management?
7. Does Place context add meaning without making location feel observed?

## Qualitative evidence

Run moderated sessions with:

- a parent sending a child or partner a local Mission;
- a person asking an older family member for a place-linked Story;
- a traveler receiving a Mission in an unfamiliar city;
- a solo user creating a self-Mission;
- a privacy-sensitive participant who passes or revokes.

Ask participants to explain:

- the difference between Mission and To-do;
- who can see the Story before and after return;
- whether Kwilt knows they arrived;
- where they would find the result next month; and
- what they expect “remove” and “delete everywhere” to do.

## Behavioral signals

Healthy signals:

- Recipients make a deliberate Accept/Pass decision.
- Accepted Missions are reopened from Missions or the linked To-do.
- A meaningful share of accepted Missions produce a saved Story.
- Returned Stories are reopened later from Stories, Missions, or Places.
- Most Stories reach value with one to three assets and little editing.
- Audience changes and revocations are rare but successfully completed when needed.
- Users correctly predict that senders cannot see route or arrival.

Warning signals:

- Users complete the To-do and believe the Mission is complete without a result.
- Mission and Story inventories feel like duplicate inboxes.
- Users routinely save media but abandon Story creation.
- Senders expect live location or recipients feel monitored.
- Story sharing is mistaken for Household-wide visibility.
- Users cannot find a returned Story outside the Mission card.
- Raw asset search is repeatedly requested for a concrete job.

## Promotion gates

Do not add nearby recommendations, badges, or multi-stop hunts until:

- the Accept → To-do → Story → Return loop is understandable without explanation;
- Story and Mission states reconcile deterministically under retry and offline use;
- negative authorization and revocation tests pass;
- shared derivative metadata has been inspected on a signed device;
- children and caregiver boundaries have an accepted capability policy;
- Explore fog has visual, tap, marker, search, and VoiceOver non-leakage proof; and
- returned Stories demonstrate revisitation, not only one-time sending.

## Expansion decisions

### Add nearby Missions when

Place-based invitations prove valuable and users ask “what could we do here?” Recommendation candidates remain private, session-scoped, explainable, and dismissible.

### Add scavenger hunts when

Single-result Missions are understood and a multi-stop completion contract can remain lightweight. A hunt should compose Missions rather than create a second task/result system.

### Add badges when

Users value a private record of meaningful kinds of exploration. Badges should summarize lived experiences, not pressure frequency or rank family members.

### Add a raw asset library when

Users repeatedly need to find, reuse, or govern media that has no Story home. Until then, offer **Add from Kwilt** inside Story composition and object-specific attachment pickers.

### Remove automatic linked To-dos when

Recipients experience them as duplicate administration. Replace automatic creation with **Add to To-dos** while retaining Mission durability.

## Job-flow update after ship

If the loop succeeds, update `job-flow-maya-move-family-life-forward`:

- **Schedule or hand off** may rise when accepted Mission projection demonstrably improves follow-through.
- **Family participation** may rise when multiple family roles can complete and return without privacy confusion.
- **Keep using system** may rise only with longitudinal evidence that Stories are revisited and Missions do not create pressure.

Infrastructure completion alone does not change delivery scores.
