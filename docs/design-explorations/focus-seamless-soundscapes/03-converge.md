# Converge: Focus seamless soundscapes

## Chosen direction

Create purpose-built loop masters, using ElevenLabs inpainting or looping generation for candidate material and deterministic local mastering for the accepted seam. Keep the existing playback owner and picker, grouping choices into Music and Nature.

## Capability delta

Today, the user can choose seven Focus soundscapes, but six remote music choices expose their four-minute ending and restart.

After this release, the user can choose corrected music or one of five continuous nature environments—Quiet Rain, Forest Stream, Ocean Waves, Fireplace, and Night Meadow—without managing audio files or noticing a composed ending.

Still unsupported: simultaneous layers, automatic rotation, favorites, downloads, user audio, and a claim of gapless iOS playback before device evidence.

## Reductive decisions

- One picker and one persisted selection remain the complete interaction model.
- Add text grouping only; no icons, cards, descriptions, preview buttons, or second page.
- Nature loops contain no speech, prominent animals, thunder, sirens, bells, or singular events that make repetition obvious.
- Each published object receives a new content-addressed path; existing immutable objects are not overwritten.

## Activation

The choices appear where soundscape selection already happens. No coachmark or promotion is needed. Adoption is a user selecting a nature environment and returning to it in later Focus sessions.

## Bet

We are betting that corrected masters plus a small nature set make Focus feel more continuous without making the setup feel larger. If source-corrected tracks still reveal a boundary on signed hardware, revisit playback with an app-owned native gapless loop rather than adding UI.

## Success signal

Three-repeat audition renders have no audible seam; automated audit shows no leading/trailing silence and no material boundary energy discontinuity; fixed-volume physical-iPhone listening across locked/background playback does not reveal a restart.
