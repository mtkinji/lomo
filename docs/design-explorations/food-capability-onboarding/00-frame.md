# Frame: Make Meals Easier

## Offered direction

The Food promise is broader than Recipes or meal planning:

> **Make meals easier.**

Kwilt can reduce work across deciding what to make, planning with the household, compiling recipe
ingredients into Groceries, and cooking without juggling a long recipe or another app.

## Target audience and representative persona

- Audience: `audience-aspirational-family-organizers`
- Persona: Maya
- Hero JTBD: `jtbd-move-the-few-things-that-matter`
- Job flow: `job-flow-maya-feed-household-with-less-work`

Maya is close enough to another meal or shopping cycle that the unanswered work is becoming
visible. She may need help deciding, getting ingredients together, or cooking. She does not want to
set up a food-management system before Kwilt becomes useful.

## Job-flow gap

The complete Food loop is the value: preserve or find useful meals, make a manageable plan, include
the household when helpful, compile the right ingredients, and cook with less attention overhead.
Current capability ownership is intentionally separate:

- Recipes owns reusable recipe truth, provenance, and Cook Mode.
- Meal Planning owns meal candidates, household input, and the active plan.
- Groceries owns compiled ingredients, list truth, and shopping handoff.
- Household Food defines the program-level continuity between them.

The onboarding gap is not a missing explanation of Recipes. It is the absence of one coherent,
clean-account way to recognize the complete promise and begin at the part of the loop that matters
now.

## Aspirational design challenge

How might we help Maya recognize that Kwilt can make the whole meal loop easier and reach one real
result immediately, while teaching only the part she needs now and keeping Recipes, Meal Planning,
and Groceries truthfully owned?

## First-value boundaries

The broad promise may end in different capability-owned results:

- **Plan:** a small editable `Next meals` set containing at least one meal Maya deliberately kept.
- **Ingredients:** a reviewed Grocery list compiled from the Recipes or meals she chose.
- **Cook:** a real Recipe opened in a resumable Cook Mode with the first actionable cue ready.

The Household Food coordinator may route and resume. It cannot create these receipts itself.

## Non-negotiable constraints

- A clean account must have a useful route without importing a Recipe or creating a Household.
- Household participation is optional value, not required setup.
- Meal planning does not require a seven-day calendar, dates, or a fixed cadence.
- Ingredient compilation is automatic only from Recipes or meal occurrences the person actually
  chose; it must not imply pantry or purchase truth.
- Cook Mode requires a real Recipe and must preserve exact progress if interrupted.
- No global notification, photo, microphone, location, retailer, or financial permission request.
- An exact Recipe share, import, Plan link, or Grocery handoff bypasses generic Food orientation
  when it proves a more specific intent.
- Reuse existing illustration language; add art only after the required teaching moments are
  accepted.

## Copy posture

Recommended chooser copy: **Make meals easier**.

`Make mealtime easy` is the motivating product idea, but `mealtime` can sound limited to the table
or eating moment. `Meals` more clearly contains deciding, planning, ingredients, shopping, and
cooking while avoiding an absolute promise that every meal will be easy.
