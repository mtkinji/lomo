# Evaluate learning: Native first meal cycle

## Decision criteria

- A clean account reaches a real Recipe in one action from the Food value door.
- The person can create a durable Plan without Household setup.
- Optional sharing is understood as feedback for this Plan, not Household enrollment.
- At least one selected meal produces a real Grocery list without reconstructing the choice.
- A grocery-first person can still open Groceries and begin a manual list.

## Outcomes

Record observed completion, confusion, abandonment point, and whether the person can explain the
loop in their own words. Promote, revise the coachmark sequence, or retire the guide based on the
observed behavior; do not preserve it merely because it shipped.
