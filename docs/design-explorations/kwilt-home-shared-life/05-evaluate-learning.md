# Evaluate learning: Connection and participation

## Questions

1. Do ordinary, self-authored moments appear alongside capability-origin posts?
2. Do recipients feel included, encouraged, or inspired to do something together?
3. Do contextual offers arrive at useful moments, and which capabilities earn them?
4. Can people explain who sees a post, a private cheer, and an outward household story?
5. Can a quieter participant get value through reading, replying, or saving without feeling obliged to post?
6. Does one stream help catch-up, or do people repeatedly need a household-first space?
7. Does following another household create meaningful connection beyond existing household use?

## Evidence plan

Use a proposed 14-day invitation-only TestFlight period after lifecycle verification. Recruit at least two households with multiple adult participants and one Goal-support relationship. Give one practical introduction, then observe without daily posting assignments. Review at day 7 and day 14; extend if real usage is too sparse to support a decision. This small cohort supplies qualitative evidence, not statistical retention claims.

Record examples of voluntary direct posts, prompted posts, replies, saves, shared plans, return visits to old moments, ignored offers, and misunderstood audiences. Ask for concrete stories: "What did you learn about someone?" and "Did this lead to anything you did together?" Do not equate a passive view with connection.

Provisional positive threshold: in each household, at least two people participate voluntarily on multiple days, with ordinary-life content and at least one meaningful response or downstream action. At least one outward household post must reach an approved external participant and produce useful interaction before claiming connected-household value. These are learning heuristics, not quotas shown to users.

## Instrumentation

Capture offer shown/dismissed/opened, draft saved, publish succeeded/failed, post opened, response submitted, attachment action succeeded/failed, history revisited, and follow requested/accepted/removed. Dimensions: source capability, direct versus contextual origin, audience kind, attachment kind, and entry point. Use opaque correlation identifiers only where needed and existing analytics identity conventions.

Never include post text, reply text, photos, Goal titles, place names, coordinates, or relationship names in analytics. Manual quotes require participant permission. Separate delivery failures from lack of interest. Keep push engagement distinct from voluntary return.

## Disconfirming signals and decisions

| Evidence | Response |
| --- | --- |
| People post only after reminders; others rarely respond | Revisit desire and activation; do not compensate with more prompts. |
| Ordinary posts are rare because everything looks like completion reporting | Improve direct composition and card language before adding adapters. |
| Household posts are repeatedly missed among external content | Test household-first entry or rooms; preserve approved connections. |
| People save places/recipes but do not converse | Explore discovery-and-participation as a stronger center; do not call it failed merely for low comments. |
| Audience or private-thread confusion | Hold expansion and correct the model/copy; require successful re-verification. |
| Offer dismissal is frequent or completing actions feels interrupted | Tune timing, eligibility, and controls; keep manual sharing available. |
| Stories are shared and revisited, but posts do not lead to plans | Continue if connection itself is clearly valuable; plans are one outcome, not a requirement. |

## Verification before learning claims

Test source commit → offer → draft → upload/publish → authorized delivery → response → source action → revisit. Exercise duplicate retries, failed media, unauthorized readers, membership changes, source deletion, post withdrawal, account switch, stale caches, and blocked users. Verify private Goal notes never widen their audience. Verify new followers do not gain old posts under the proposed publication-time rule.

Use focused logic/permission tests and source adapters first, task-completion verification at implementation handoff, and separate multi-account native/device evidence. No tests or runtime results in this design turn establish implementation readiness.
