# Converge

Choose contextual corrections.

It best serves Maya because the assumption is exposed where its consequence is
already visible, while the detailed edit remains in an existing focused
surface. The category owns whether its money is protected or flexible. A
transaction normally inherits that choice through its category; choosing a
different treatment changes the category assignment or marks the transaction
outside the plan instead of storing a contradictory transaction flag.

The bet is that three small, consistent correction points will improve trust
more than a comprehensive settings screen. The trade-off is that bulk category
role editing remains out of scope.

## Interaction contract

- Primary action: the assumption row or field in the current context.
- Must show: current living target, current category role, and current
  transaction treatment.
- Reveal later: target rationale, category choices, funding rhythm, and plan
  consequence.
- Must not add: a plan dashboard, a transaction-level protected boolean,
  duplicate role terminology, or silent mutation.
- Required states: legacy inferred role, explicit role, saving/error, outside
  plan, split treatment, and no matching category.
