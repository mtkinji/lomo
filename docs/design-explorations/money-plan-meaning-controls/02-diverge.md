# Diverge

## 1. Contextual corrections

Make the living target actionable in the flexible-spending disclosure, add a
separate `Counts as` choice to category planning, and expose a transaction's
category-backed plan treatment from transaction detail.

- Strength: corrections live beside the answer they explain.
- Risk: three surfaces must use exactly the same language and domain truth.

## 2. Central plan settings

Put living target and every protected/flexible category in one plan-management
screen, then link there from Budget.

- Strength: one administrative destination.
- Risk: asks Maya to leave the evidence she is trying to understand and creates
  a planning dashboard that is heavier than the job.

## 3. Inline controls everywhere

Add target steppers, protected toggles on category tiles, and independent
protected/flexible controls on every transaction.

- Strength: maximum immediacy.
- Risk: turns Budget into an editor, creates contradictory transaction and
  category truth, and greatly increases accidental changes.
