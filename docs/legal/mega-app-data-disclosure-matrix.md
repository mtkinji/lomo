# Kwilt Mega-App Data Disclosure Matrix

**Release baseline:** Current Kwilt submission candidate; reconfirm against the exact archive selected for submission
**Reviewed:** September 3, 2026
**Purpose:** Product and engineering source of truth for the public Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, permission prompts, App Store Connect App Privacy answers, Google Play Data Safety answers, and account-deletion verification.

This is not legal advice. Counsel should review final consumer-facing language, especially eligibility, dependent profiles, financial information, regional privacy rights, arbitration, and shared-content retention.

## Product-wide rules

- Kwilt is one Tools for Life app spanning Planning and Chat, Money, Explore, Meals/Recipes/Groceries, Games, Household and Screen Time, Focus, Calendar, Health, attachments, subscriptions, and connected tools.
- `Local-first` does not mean `device-only`. Core workspace state can begin on-device, while sign-in, sync, sharing, AI, durable Chat, Money, Explore history, Meals, multiplayer Games, connected providers, analytics, subscriptions, and support communications use network services.
- Capability data is private by default. Visibility changes only through an explicit share, invite, household role, multiplayer room, connected-provider authorization, or publication action.
- Kwilt does not sell personal information and does not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- Analytics must not include user-entered free-form text, financial transaction content, precise location/path content, Health data, Apple Family Controls tokens, calendar event content, recipe text, grocery item text, Chat content, or game submissions.
- People under 13 may not create or control a Kwilt account. An adult account holder may create a parent-managed dependent profile and enable bounded household capabilities. A person between 13 and the local age of majority may use an account only with a parent or guardian's consent.

## Account-deletion release contract

Release readiness requires evidence for all of the following:

1. Authentication is required and deletion is explicitly confirmed in-app.
2. Device sessions are signed out and local account-scoped caches are cleared.
3. Plaid Items and access tokens are removed before their database connection rows disappear.
4. Retailer, calendar, MCP/connected-tool, push, and other provider tokens are revoked or deleted where supported.
5. User-owned Storage objects are removed through the Storage API before Auth deletion.
6. Direct user-owned rows cascade or are explicitly removed.
7. Person/Household records do not block Auth deletion. Private person-owned Recipes, Meals, Groceries, and dietary data are removed. Shared data survives only when needed by another authenticated participant and is de-identified.
8. A surviving household is transferred only to another authenticated adult. A dependent never becomes the owner by deletion side effect.
9. App Store subscriptions remain managed by Apple and are not cancelled by Kwilt account deletion.
10. Operational logs, fraud/security records, processor records, and backups may remain only for disclosed limited purposes and schedules.

## Store-label guardrails

- Tracking: **No**, unless a future build links Kwilt or third-party data across other companies' apps/websites for advertising or measurement.
- Data linked to identity: answer **Yes** for cloud records keyed to a Supabase user/person/household ID, even when a capability is optional.
- Optional collection: explain feature gating in the policy, but include every type collected by any path in the submitted build.
- Apple device-only exceptions: Family Controls application/category tokens and a local Face ID result are not collected by Kwilt when they never leave the device.
- Location: background Explore recording and signed-in sync require Precise Location disclosure; occasional map/place lookup language alone is insufficient.
- Games: remote rooms, scores, moves, submissions, and votes require Gameplay Content disclosure.
- Financial: account/transaction/budget data must be disclosed even though payment-card credentials are not collected by Kwilt.
