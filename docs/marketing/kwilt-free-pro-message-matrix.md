Every purchase surface should say that Apple shows the current price, billing period, and introductory eligibility before confirmation. Restore and Manage Subscription remain available regardless of tier.

## Upsell copy contract

Every contextual offer must pass a three-second read:

1. **Outcome:** the title says what the person is trying to accomplish, not
   merely that the action requires Pro.
2. **Mechanism:** the explanation says what Kwilt actually does to produce that
   outcome.
3. **Control:** the proof points say what the person chooses, reviews, or
   approves before anything changes.

Use **View Pro plans** when the next screen is plan choice. Price, cadence,
Family Sharing, and introductory eligibility belong to the Apple-backed plan
chooser. Never imply that tapping the contextual CTA purchases a subscription.

Avoid labels such as *more power*, *advanced*, *deeper*, *smarter*, *insights*,
or *flexible* when a plain description of the actual behavior is available.
Do not list unrelated bundle benefits on a contextual paywall. Retired paid
boundaries are Free; remove their gates and sales copy rather than polishing
them.
