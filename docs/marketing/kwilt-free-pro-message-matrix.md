# Kwilt Free and Pro message matrix

| Capability | Free message | Pro message | Do not claim yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Plan and follow through with Arcs, Goals, To-dos, Focus, attachments, and views | Not a paid differentiator | Artificial Arc or Goal limits |
| Money | See the upgrade path before connecting an account | Build and maintain a budget from connected accounts and transactions | Production Plaid reliability until verified |
| Personal Screen Time | Build one clear rule using Focus, time, or daily use | Combine conditions and connect access to real steps or Money | Guaranteed blocking or Apple parity without signed-device proof |
| Family Screen Time | Learn what the setup requires | Coordinate caregiver-managed rules and temporary changes | Launch marketing before two-device proof and App Review clearance |
| AI | Use standard AI within the Free allowance | More capacity plus attachment analysis, scheduling, background work, and external agents | Unlimited AI |
| Cook and Live | Available as bounded MVP previews when enabled | No present paid claim | Subscription value, permanence, or smart-speaker replacement |
| Creator offer | Optional challenge or template | Same Apple-priced Kwilt Pro | Creator code grants Pro, guarantees a discount, or stacks offers |

Every purchase surface should say that Apple shows the current price, billing period, and introductory eligibility before confirmation. Restore and Manage Subscription remain available regardless of tier.
