# Kwilt Money Copy Voice

Kwilt Money uses the same voice system as Kwilt. Budget can be more literal about money state, but it should not become a separate financial coach, finance dashboard, parent-control product, or productivity app.

Source of truth: `kwilt-copywriting` skill.

## Voice

Kwilt sounds like a smart, warm coworker who helps the user make progress on what matters.

For Budget, that means:

- Tell the truth about the money pattern.
- Offer one useful next step.
- Keep the user in control.
- Stay warm without getting soft or vague.
- Never shame spending, drift, missed plans, or app access.

## Rules

1. Prefer concrete over abstract.
2. Prefer small next steps over broad financial transformation.
3. Use evidence first, advice second.
4. Name tradeoffs plainly.
5. Avoid urgency, guilt, scorekeeping, and moral language.
6. Do not sound like a therapist, guru, productivity coach, bank warning, or parental-control system.
7. Introduce Kwilt as the follow-through home only after the user asks to view the idea.
8. Use active voice for requirements and outcomes.
9. Name the real action required; do not hide it behind broad phrases like "review reality" when the product requires a specific budget or spending check.

## Use

- `Shopping is running hot.`
- `Set a goal?`
- `Want help changing this pattern?`
- `Kwilt helps carry Budget patterns into small goals and next steps.`
- `Pause before household extras this month`
- `Before buying household extras, add the item to a 24-hour list.`
- `Transaction rows stay in Budget.`

## Avoid

- `Optimize your finances`
- `Build discipline`
- `Stop bad spending`
- `Fix your financial health`
- `Become your best self`
- `You failed your budget`
- `Set a Kwilt Goal now`
- `Crush your spending goals`

## Crossover Rule

Budget owns the evidence. Kwilt owns follow-through.

The Budget surface should say what happened and ask whether the user wants to set a goal. The interstitial can introduce Kwilt as the place to carry that goal into a first step. Do not frame the handoff as Budget being insufficient; frame it as the apps working together.
