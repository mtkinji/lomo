## Evidence boundaries

The stills show typography hierarchy, composition, wording, imagery and visible controls. Andrew's narration supplies perceived calm, intelligence and onboarding fatigue. Neither establishes measured activation, retention, conversion uplift or optimal sequence length.

The composer followed by findings does not prove those findings used the entered context. A count of actions does not prove execution. Displayed savings, member counts, certification and pricing are Origin's historical claims, not verified facts or Kwilt claims. The final viewport does not prove the promised actions are absent elsewhere.

Exact animation, transition duration, video looping, dismissal behavior, branching, persistence, accessibility and platform coverage cannot be inferred from stills. No current Origin web lookup or new runtime walkthrough was performed for this catalog entry.

## Preserve / Translate / Reject

| Preserve | Translate into Kwilt | Reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One unmistakable promise | Household-wide promise, then the person's chosen capability | Financial-advisor category or generic AI-assistant positioning |
| Benefit before effort; connective invitations | Explain why join, access or purchase helps this chosen path now | Abrupt auth forms or unrelated promotional drawers |
| Spacious imagery and decisive headline hierarchy | Shoreline opening; coherent Kwilt illustrations and quiet input surfaces | Clouds, copied fonts/assets, decorative meaningless icons |
| One understandable decision at a time | Shortest capability-owned setup that produces real value | Identical screen counts or unnecessary profile questions |
| Findings presented as meaningful moments | Actual understood, organized, created or enabled result | Fabricated insights, forced three-card sequences or new product features |
| Promise of continuing assistance | Enter the usable result in the chosen capability | Another mandatory invitation after promising entry |
| Contextual, clear offer | Actual entitlement, eligibility, localized price and renewal terms | Borrowed discount, social proof, urgency or unsupported savings claims |
