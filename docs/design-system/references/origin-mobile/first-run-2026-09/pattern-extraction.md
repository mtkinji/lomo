# Candidate: promise-led capability onboarding

Pattern ID: `PAT-onboarding-promise-led`  
Status: **Candidate**, cross-capability design direction; not an implemented or visually accepted flow.  
Source: [Origin first-run evidence](source.md), `REF-2026-09-28-origin-first-run`.  
Last reviewed: 2026-09-28.

## Job and selection

Help a new user understand what Kwilt will do for them, choose a starting capability, make informed commitments, and reach a useful result. Setup should feel like the product beginning to help, not paperwork before use.

Use for first-run or first activation of a chosen capability. Do not replay the brand story for routine use, force returning subscribers through an offer, or replace a small contextual permission request with a full onboarding sequence.

## Reusable narrative—not a fixed screen count

1. **Promise:** one clear description of the product and its benefit.
2. **Personal relevance:** choose the first capability; make its benefit tangible.
3. **Invitation to join:** explain why an account is needed at this point; preserve the chosen intention through authentication.
4. **Relevant offer:** when a paid prerequisite applies, explain the actual purchase in the same visual and verbal story.
5. **Guided setup:** small purposeful invitations, each with a visible reason; ask only what changes the experience.
6. **Tangible payoff:** reveal what was actually understood, organized, created or enabled.
7. **Continued use:** enter that result directly, with a relevant next action available.

These are narrative functions, not seven mandatory screens. Combine adjacent functions when clearer; omit fulfilled or unnecessary commitments. Existing authentication, entitlement and permission contracts determine available branches. The candidate offer position is after understood value and before paid setup, not a blanket monetization-policy change.

## Messaging analysis

- **Keep subtle branding:** a small, upper-centered logo, separate from the core message.
- **Keep message placement and singularity:** one dominant core message in the central composition; no competing feature list or secondary promotional message. Exact Kwilt typography and geometry remain governed by its own system and rendered review.
- **Adapt the bottom action:** preserve bottom orientation, but use Kwilt's fully rounded button and existing dock geometry—equidistant from the sides, bottom corners and bottom. Do not copy Origin's button shape or replace the canonical dock contract.
- **Omit “Always on” and an equivalent badge:** Andrew finds the referent unclear; its possible implication of background AI work is an interpretation, not verified behavior. Kwilt has no selected equivalent promise to communicate here. Do not invent one to fill the space.

Scope: initial app landing only. This selection does not extend video, sparse copy or this composition to capability setup, forms, offers, findings or routine app use. No existing canonical rule is superseded. Status: owner-selected design qualities; not a new implementation request, completed trial or runtime-verified canonical promotion. Any later trial must preserve legibility through the video, static/Reduce Motion fallback and existing accessible action geometry.

- **One dominant message:** concrete, plainspoken and short. Establish what the product is and what it does before listing features.
- **Invitation before instruction:** explain the benefit of the next request. Account creation should feel like continuing the selected setup, not restarting it.
- **Show relevance, not just capability names:** the choice names a useful direction; the next moment makes that direction tangible.
- **Questions teach through participation:** each answer must actually shape the result. Do not ask for context merely to imply personalization.
- **Earned proof:** separate facts, suggestions and completed actions. A financial total can demonstrate competence without being advice.
- **Offer continuity:** purchase is a transparent commitment to the understood benefit, not an unrelated promotional interruption.
- **Honest ending:** a CTA promising entry must enter usable content. Request notifications later when the person chooses the specific ongoing service.

## Page anatomy and visual style

Three-second read: what this moment offers or asks, why it matters, and how to proceed.

Scan order: orientation → dominant message → meaningful supporting image/evidence/control → current action. A sparse promise can omit explanatory copy; a purchase cannot omit essential terms.

Two complementary modes:

- **Promise/reveal:** spacious composition, one headline, optional concise support, meaningful full-background or large artwork, one dominant action. Atmospheric treatment marks significance rather than adding decorative waiting.
- **Decision/input:** quiet stable canvas, clear label/control relationships, honest progress where known, accessible recovery and a consistent action region. No video behind dense forms.

For the current household direction, preserve the shoreline as the opening treatment and parchment/coherent editorial illustration family afterward. A later atmospheric variant requires specific review; this pattern does not reintroduce ocean video behind every page.

Grouping and spacing: the shared shell owns outer gutters and chrome; copy forms one compact title/support group; illustration has a protected content-safe area; choices form a distinct, internally consistent group. Keep title/chrome anchors stable between equivalent steps. Flexible space can center choices between copy and dock clearance on roomy screens, but must collapse into scrolling at small sizes and enlarged text. Use canonical spacing/typography and dock clearance, not measurements traced from Origin. Avoid a hard illustration-to-footer seam unless an actual surface boundary is needed.

Transitions: preserve orientation across page changes; use agreed page progression plus subtle content reveal without delaying action availability. Exact timings require rendered review. Provider handoffs return to the same task and retained state. Stills are not evidence for Origin's motion implementation.

## Consolidated learning from the continued walkthrough

Captured September 28 after Andrew clarified that a newly arriving message was not intended to redirect the preceding analysis. The staged-reveal notes were saved before that interruption; this section brings the related insights together so later screenshots do not displace them.

- **Pacing exists at two levels.** Page transitions move between decisions; soft, time-delayed section reveals guide attention within a page. Andrew specifically identifies the latter as part of Origin's appeal. A static mock alone cannot evaluate that quality.
- **Motion is part of the explanation.** Introducing meaningful information groups progressively can make a result feel presented to the person rather than dumped onto a dashboard. That interpretation is a design hypothesis grounded in Andrew's reaction, not measured conversion evidence. Preserve a coherent reading order without making users wait for decoration.
- **Depth does not require more gates.** The gathered page supports scanning the headline figures, reading separate findings and reaching a concluding interpretation by scrolling. These are layers of one page, not additional mandatory onboarding steps.
- **The recommendation has a rationale.** The subsequent page pairs one proposed budget with historical context and reasons. The useful relationship is proposal plus explanation, not a requirement to copy the chart, financial formula or text density.
- **Understanding precedes commitment.** The documented sequence now distinguishes gathered evidence, suggested budget with rationale, and finalization. “Continue” gives way to “Set budget” at the explicit commitment point; no post-save result has yet been supplied.
- **Guidance leaves room for agency.** Andrew confirms dragging the handle to change the savings amount. The finalization states pair the proposed budget with its savings consequences; the more demanding target changes both wording and color to communicate ambition, rather than treating every larger savings estimate as unqualified success.
- **The invitation can happen inside the app.** The earlier “Your budget is ready” drawer gives a capability a contextual introduction after first run. Reuse that narrative function without forcing a new onboarding funnel or claiming a result exists before it does.
- **Repeat the grammar, not the finance workflow.** Across capabilities, use supported evidence, a useful proposal, understandable consequences, user control and a specific commitment where those functions actually exist. Do not invent editors, rules, calculations or actions to fill a universal template.

Together: **invite → establish understanding → explain a useful suggestion → let the person adjust and understand consequences → explicitly commit → continue into the real result**. This is a candidate translation, not a claim that every transition, save or destination has been observed in Origin. The exact placement of the earlier review/loading state remains unverified. Authentication and purchase remain contextual prerequisites, not steps to replay in an already active capability.

Retain the caveats alongside the inspiration: Origin's paragraph density, possible metric-definition mismatch, text entering top chrome, unknown calculation assumptions and unknown post-save behavior are not qualities to inherit. Soft reveal requires accessibility and early-interaction handling; semantic feedback requires explicit language as well as color. The source captures and firsthand reports are cataloged in the [post-launch study](../post-launch-budget-2026-09/source.md).

### Candidate: staged informational reveal

Status: Candidate refinement, September 28. Evidence: [Andrew's firsthand report and suggested-budget captures](../post-launch-budget-2026-09/source.md). This is a selected motion quality, not measured timing or native proof.

Job/selection: guide attention through an explanatory or first-value moment without presenting all information at once. Use a small number of meaningful groups, not every word, icon or chart bar as a separate event. Routine forms and frequently revisited utility pages should not require a presentation sequence.

Anatomy: stable page/chrome/action region → primary message → key result → supporting visualization/evidence → rationale. This order is a proposed Kwilt translation; Origin's exact animation order has not been recorded. Groups follow the same semantic and visual reading order. Shared motion infrastructure should own choreography; the capability owns content and real readiness. `HouseholdStarterFlow.tsx` and the shared capability setup shell are starting points for inspection, not proof of existing support.

Do: reserve final layout geometry; keep gutters, title anchors and dock clearance stable; use a restrained group-level reveal; make completed content immediately available on revisit; let scrolling ahead reveal needed content without a blank timed gate. Timing and easing should use the project's motion conventions and be judged in a recording, not invented from screenshots.

Don't: delay Continue merely to finish decoration; equate a timed reveal with AI computation; auto-scroll or move focus as each group appears; obscure errors, purchase terms or permission consequences behind a timer; replay the whole sequence after a provider return. A genuine unready result still needs truthful loading/error behavior, not a premature reveal.

States/accessibility: fresh entry, interrupted/revisited page, scrolling before completion, late data, failure and navigation away. Reduce Motion should render the complete composition without a staged delay. Maintain one coherent accessible reading order and access to complete ready content without repeated live announcements. Essential consent/decision information must be available before action. Cancel pending animations on exit.

Allowed variants: brief entry reveal for a result or explanation; static complete state for returning users, accessibility preference or immediate interaction. Exact duration and offscreen trigger policy remain open. This does not replace the canonical loader or change persistence/navigation behavior.

Proof: rendered screen recording with real content, early scrolling/Continue, back/return, reduced motion, enlarged text and screen-reader traversal. Obtain explicit scoped acceptance before promoting this into an app-wide motion rule.

## States and accessibility

Specify first-time and returning users; authenticated/unauthenticated; eligible/ineligible/already subscribed; free/paid capability; purchase pending/cancelled/failed/restored; permission granted/denied/restricted; provider cancellation/return; loading/error/partial/empty evidence; save failure; resumed setup; successful persisted result. Skip/back/close must have explicit truthful destinations. Never promise guest browsing if it only opens authentication.

Keep full copy available to assistive technology during animation; support Reduce Motion and a static media fallback. Maintain text contrast over all media frames, reading order, accessible focus on page changes, announced status, enlarged text, reachable touch targets, keyboard-safe inputs and readable purchase disclosures. Decorative images should not compete with meaningful spoken content.

## Allowed variants across capabilities

Repeat the narrative and visual grammar, not financial objects. Each capability supplies its existing prerequisites, setup decisions, truthful payoff and destination. Money may reveal supported account evidence; another path may reveal an actual saved rule, plan or goal only if its current workflow produces it. This is not authorization to invent household invitations, automatic purchases, new calculations or actions to fill the template.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Explain the benefit immediately before requesting access | Drop a permission dialog into an unexplained step |
| Keep the selected intention through join, offer and setup | Restart with generic marketing after the choice |
| Present a supported result with evidence and limitations | Manufacture three insights, savings claims or an action count |
| Use purposeful illustration with protected copy space | Obscure copy or add unrelated lifestyle imagery |
| Offer paid setup with truthful terms and decline behavior | Force free paths through purchase or hide renewal details |
| Land in the result the user just created | Promise entry, then cover it with another setup request |
| Collapse redundant story moments | Optimize for a polished but unnecessarily long sequence |
