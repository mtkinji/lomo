# Help Us Enjoy Being Together

## When this job is hot

Two or more family members or friends want an easy reason to spend time together. They may share a room or live far apart, and nobody wants to plan an event, learn a system, coordinate several accounts, or appoint a referee first.

## What progress means

People can choose a game, understand the next action, and begin in seconds. Local and remote forms preserve the same meaningful shared loop without making one person administer the moment.

## What done feels like

Starting was effortless, everyone could participate, and the people involved feel they spent time together rather than merely using the same app.

## Anti-patterns

First-party single-player games, mandatory accounts, onboarding tours, productivity framing, public leaderboards, setup-heavy rooms, manipulative streaks, or competition that diminishes a child or guest.
