# Leafling Weather Acting Study 01

This study proves four semantic weather clips for every evolution form without adding renderer-authored body motion:

- `weather-notice`: sky stillness, ears lead, eyes understand, ready hold;
- `wind-brace`: hear gust, lower weight, held brace, leaf rebound, grounded recovery;
- `rain-flinch`: first drop, tuck, twist, shake off, find cover;
- `sun-bask`: find warmth, close eyes, inhale, exhale, content hold.

The first implementation deliberately remixes approved atlas drawings. That is a limited-animation edit, not a claim that the drawings were originally authored for weather. The contact sheet and motion previews determine whether the new sequence creates a coherent performance. A beat that retains the wrong original meaning earns one new row; the renderer may not compensate with whole-sprite tilt or bounce.

Baby uses shorter holds and a lower compressed brace. Young is quick and elastic. Guardian holds the strongest key longer and recovers deliberately. Every clip remains grounded and shares the existing `(80, 120)` anchor.
